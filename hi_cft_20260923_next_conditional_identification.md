The modular-data and character conclusions do not require that external

identification argument. The statement does not assert such a proof,
and has no implication identifying the continuum limit of the rho chain.

The positive decomposition of omega_U into conformal vectors of regular
subalgebras gives C2-cofiniteness. A generalized-module extension of the
vacuum splits by its simultaneous nonnegative stress energies; this
makes the tensor unit projective. Grothendieck--Verdier duality gives
split evaluation onto the unit, and the Etingof--Penneys rigidity theorem
then yields rigidity. Projectivity of the unit implies semisimplicity.
The details and two independent audits are in the rationality notes.

invertible-rank argument without importing a C*-category or conformal-net
comparison hypothesis.

The actual order-thirteen core AL has fourteen irreducible modules.
Their lowest weights, combined with the positive-stress identity and
the retained affine-character inequalities, bound every simple U-module
top by17/10. Exact irreducibility of the80-dimensional complementary
current space therefore gives the displayed all-weight B branching

For H=V(E8), the proper-case restriction is

       H=1+X+Y+Y*,  qdim Y=qdim Y*=qdim B.

The Lagrangian-algebra correspondence gives Rep(U)=Z(F), where F is
the category of H-modules in Rep(U). Its rank is the squared-multiplicity
sum in H: four if Y and Y* differ, six if they coincide. The actual
automorphisms fixing U and the local lattice-module category force
the invertible part of F to be untwisted Vec(Z3).

In rank four, the near-group multiplicities are0,2,3,6. Multiplicity0

make U an A4 fixed algebra in a holomorphic c=8 extension, but every

proved current data. For multiplicity3, center induction and two
indicator moments force the standard modular data in either orientation;

At c=8, those data permit only one nonvacuum character pole, at
h=2/13. Its nonnegative integral coefficient gamma contributes13 gamma

Finally, Vercleyen's classification gives at most four pivotal tensor

The ring has trivial universal grading and hence a unique pivotal
