## Identification and the remaining unitary-CFT distinction

Under properness, the separate audited dependency chain gives the
Haagerup modular data and full gamma=0 character. The older route to the
stronger ribbon equivalence Rep(U) equivalent Z(H3) invokes the explicitly
external exhaustive pivotal classification; those classification logs
have not been independently replayed here. The new complete-unitarity
argument below permits a separate published C*-classification route.
This audit does not reprove the earlier long rationality/character chain.

The finite gates themselves add no complete-unitarity theorem. U has
the inherited positive vacuum form as a PCT-stable lattice subVOA, but
strong unitarity means that every irreducible module is unitarizable;
complete unitarity additionally concerns positivity of the canonical
Hermitian forms on fusion products. These distinctions are stated in
[Gui's geometric-positivity paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.11856).
His [extension theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.03484) ascends from a
completely unitary conformal subalgebra to its unitary extension; it
does not directly descend from V(E8) to its subalgebra U.

Consequently a successful finite certificate can legitimately establish
the explicit unitary strongly rational chiral VOA and its algebraic
Haagerup identification, subject to the external classification step.
Claiming a fully unitary module/fusion realization requires an additional
complete-unitarity argument. The new conditional argument below provides
one for this actual U; it is separate from the finite gates themselves.
Identifying the natural VOA and net tensor functors is a further question.
Abstract ribbon equivalence to a unitary center alone does not compare
the VOA's canonical Hermitian fusion forms. The new argument explicitly
supplies positivity rather than inferring it from that abstract equivalence.
