# A current bound for the reverse Izumi–Xu modular representation

23 September 2026. Independent root derivation. This is initially a
conditional modular-representation argument: identifying every relevant
categorification with one of the two ribbon orientations is a separate
classification input. It does not prove properness of U.

Assume a positive-character c=8 VOA has the reversed modular data of
G2,3 tensor A2,1, and that the distinguished twist-one extension object
is 1 plus X, corresponding to the G2 labels 1 plus64 in the second-factor
vacuum sector. Assume its extension character is that of B=V(E6+A2),
whose first two coefficients are1,86. Write n=dim U1. The complementary
G2 modular block has coordinate v0=chi_U-chi_X and nonzero coordinates
minus the characters of G2 labels77,14,7. Tensor with the A2 character
block. The usual charge-conjugate character equality reduces to the
subspace even in both discriminant coordinates.

After multiplication by eta^8 this is the weight-four Weil representation
of the discriminant form -(r^2/7+s^2/3), r mod7 and s mod3. Verification
of this modular-block identification should use the explicit matrix in
[Abhishek et al., equation3.9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.00275), with
complex conjugation, the minus signs on the three nonzero coordinates,
and the eta multiplier. No dimension formula for vector-valued modular
forms is needed below.

## A positive lattice for this Weil representation

Take the rank-four positive even lattice with Gram matrix

    [ 2 -1  0  0 ]
    [-1  2 -1  0 ]
    [ 0 -1  2 -1 ]
    [ 0  0 -1  6 ].

Its leading principal determinants are2,3,4,21. Its discriminant group
is cyclic of order21, generated by

    eta=(16,11,6,1)/21,  q(eta)=8/21.

The map (r,s) -> (9r+7s)eta has quadratic form
6r^2/7+2s^2/3, equal to the required negative form modulo integers.
The first three basis vectors span the root lattice A3. Decompose a
vector orthogonally into this three-dimensional subspace and its
one-dimensional perpendicular part, and put

    P(x)=||x_A3||^2-3||x_perp||^2.

This is a harmonic quadratic polynomial. Its vector theta series is
therefore a weight-four cusp form. The character representation is
even in r and s separately, while the lattice need not realize these
two symmetries individually. **Project both vector theta series to
this subrepresentation** by replacing each component f_(r,s) with
(f_(r,s)+f_(-r,s))/2. Simultaneous negation already preserves them.
On the cyclic label t this projection is (f_t+f_(13t))/2. Let H denote
the projected harmonic series, Theta the projected ordinary
weight-two series, and put

    F=-6 D_2 Theta,  D_2=q d/dq-E2/6.

The Serre derivative makes F a holomorphic modular form of weight four.
Its constant vector is the unit vector at discriminant zero.

## Only two coefficients are needed

The lattice has exactly the twelve A3 roots: the squared norm of the
perpendicular projection of a vector with last coordinate m is
21m^2/4, so m!=0 cannot give norm2. Hence at discriminant zero

    Theta_0=1+12q+...,
    F_0=1-84q+...,
    H_0=24q+....

The only nonvacuum polar exponent after division by eta^8 occurs
at discriminant energy2/21. It is the orbit (r,s)=(+/-2,+/-1).
For (2,1), (9r+7s)eta is congruent to

    z=(1,2,3,4)/21,

which lies in A3-perp and has squared norm4/21. It is the unique
shortest vector in this discriminant coset. Its projected partner
(-2,1) has cyclic label10. Every vector there has last coordinate
10/21 modulo integers, so its squared perpendicular norm is at
least25/21, greater than4/21. It contributes no polar vector.
The projection consequently halves the polar coefficients:

    [q^(2/21)]Theta=1/2,
    [q^(2/21)]F=3/14,
    [q^(2/21)]H=-2/7.

Let A>=0 be the polar top coefficient of the corresponding actual
character (A=0 if its lowest weight is lifted by an integer). The
minus sign in the modular-block coordinate means that its coefficient
is -A. The combination F+cH has the correct vacuum pole and this
polar part precisely when

    c=(3+14A)/4.

Subtract (F+cH)/eta^8 from the actual complementary character vector.
All poles at infinity cancel. Both vectors transform for the same
finite-image weight-zero representation, so the difference is a
constant invariant vector: each component is a holomorphic function
on the compact modular curve of a finite-index kernel. The vacuum
coordinate has T eigenvalue exp(-2pi i/3), so that constant has zero
vacuum coordinate. In particular it cannot change the coefficient
used next.

Because chi_U+chi_X=chi_B, the q coefficient of eta^8 v0 is2n-94.
Equating it with the lattice construction gives

    2n-94=-84+24c,
    n=14+42A >=14.

Thus this ribbon orientation, with the specified B extension, cannot
describe the proper candidate with six currents. The proof requires
the explicit modular-block/label identification and the stated
extension object; it does not follow merely from equality of global
dimensions. The construction and first coefficients admit a tiny
exact lattice check. The separate non-reversed argument gives a
different current bound, n=15+A+10B.
