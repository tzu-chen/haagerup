# Final scope review

The report and identification note are covered by
`hi_cft_20260923_proof_artifact_audit.json`. This supplementary review
was written after that manifest and does not alter its files.

The final literature check confirms that the universal-algebra step
does not require free generation. In
[Linshaw, Section 3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1710.02275), a degenerate
nonlinear conformal algebra still has a graded universal vertex algebra
and a unique simple graded quotient. The parafermion algebra is an
explicit example there; its higher null relations do not invalidate
this argument. The reconstruction note supplies the finite OPE closure
needed to apply this statement here.

The proved finiteness statement concerns the actual generated U. It
does not resolve whether U equals the ambient lattice algebra B. In
particular, C2-cofiniteness cannot itself distinguish the proposed
Haagerup theory from B, which also has this property.

The next existence step must address this distinction. A sufficient
concrete target is an orthogonal projection on B, commuting with every
mode of the generators of U, which fixes the vacuum and annihilates
the certified 80-dimensional current complement. The existing
finite-grade invariant spaces do not supply such a projection on all
of B. Alternatively, identification of a rational conformal frame
inside U could make its full extension and branching computable.
The remaining stresses with charges 32/19 and 182/95 give specific
subalgebras to investigate in that direction; their rational
extensions have not been established.

No Haagerup categorical equivalence or relation to the c approximately
2 chain is asserted. All numerical jobs have finished or were stopped
at the checkpoints described in the report.
