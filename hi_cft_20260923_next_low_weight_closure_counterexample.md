# Pairwise positive-product closure does not control the generated low weights

23 September 2026. Exact analytical counterexample, with no numerical
job. The proposed general implication is false even in a positive,
unitary lattice VOA and for a PCT-fixed quasiprimary weight-two space
containing the ambient conformal vector.

The failed implication is: if S1 is a current Lie algebra, S2 is
PCT-stable, the current actions close, and

    a_1 b in S2,  a_2 b in S1,  a_3 b in C1  (a,b in S2),

then the vertex subalgebra generated by S1 and S2 has no additional
weight-one or weight-two states. In the counterexample S1=0, so every
current-action condition is vacuous, and a_2 b=0 for all a,b in S2.

## Positive even lattice and explicit cocycle

Let L have basis a1,a2,a3,a4 with Gram matrix

    G=5 I4-J4,

where J4 is the all-ones matrix. Thus ai^2=4 and (ai,aj)=-1 for
i!=j. The eigenvalues are1,5,5,5, so L is positive definite; its
integral Gram matrix has even diagonal, so it is an even lattice.
Put a5=-(a1+a2+a3+a4). Then a5^2=4 and (a5,ai)=-1 for i<=4.

Use a bimultiplicative lattice cocycle defined on the basis by

    epsilon(ai,aj)=-1 if i>j, and +1 if i<=j.

It has epsilon(alpha,beta)/epsilon(beta,alpha)=(-1)^(alpha,beta),
as required. Also epsilon(-alpha,-beta)=epsilon(alpha,beta).
For each of the five ai one has epsilon(ai,-ai)=1. For a5 the
last equality follows from the six lower-triangular factors.

The standard lattice-operator formula in this convention is

    Y(e^alpha,z)e^beta
      = epsilon(alpha,beta) z^(alpha,beta)
        exp(sum_(n>0) alpha(-n) z^n/n) e^(alpha+beta).

For a primary-source account of the lattice construction and its
positive Hermitian form, see
[Dong--Lin, *Unitary vertex operator algebras*, Section4.4 and
Theorem4.12](https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.2361).
The anti-linear PCT involution sends h(-n) to -h(-n) for real h,
and e^alpha to e^-alpha in this real cocycle convention.

## The closed fifteen-dimensional slice

Write h=L tensor C and let Q be the ten-dimensional space spanned by
h(-1)k(-1)1, with h,k in h. Define

    Xi=e^ai+e^-ai                  (i=1,2,3,4),
    X5=i(e^a5-e^-a5),
    S1=0,
    S2=Q + span_C{X1,X2,X3,X4,X5}.

All listed generators of S2 are PCT-fixed. They are quasiprimary of
weight two; Q contains the conformal vector of the full rank-four
lattice VOA. The sum is direct by lattice momentum, so dim S2=15.

The pairwise positive products satisfy every claimed closure condition:

* Q_1 Q is contained in Q, Q_2 Q=0, and Q_3 Q is scalar. These are
  the usual Heisenberg quadratic contractions.
* For q=h(-1)k(-1)1, q_1 Xi=(h,ai)(k,ai) Xi. The products q_2 Xi
  and q_3 Xi vanish; skew symmetry gives the same closure in the
  reverse order. The weight-one/zero charged spaces vanish since
  ai has squared norm4.
* For i!=j all Xi_n Xj vanish when n>=1: the relevant momentum
  pairings are +1 or -1, so the largest possible pole is simple.
* For every i, Xi_1 Xi=ai(-1)^2 1, Xi_2 Xi=0, and Xi_3 Xi=2 1.
  The same formulas hold for X5 because its two exponential
  coefficients have product1. Opposite-momentum derivative terms
  cancel in the self-product.

All higher positive products vanish by weight. Thus this example
also has a commutative Griess product and a positive invariant
bilinear form on the PCT-real S2.

## Four generating fields create a new weight-two vector

Set b12=a1+a2 and b123=a1+a2+a3. The lattice formula gives exactly

    X2_0 X1 = -(e^b12+e^-b12),
    X3_1 (X2_0 X1) = -(e^b123+e^-b123),
    Y := X4_2 (X3_1 (X2_0 X1)) = e^a5+e^-a5.

In the three steps the relevant inner products are -1,-2,-3;
the chosen mode takes the leading pole, so there are no oscillator
correction terms. The cocycle factors are respectively -1,+1,-1.
The opposite-sign momentum terms have positive pairings and do not
contribute at these modes.

The vector Y is independent of S2: its only momenta are +/-a5,
and its coefficients are linearly independent of those of X5.
Hence the generated VOA already has an additional weight-two state.

## A fifth generating field creates a current

The leading next-to-top coefficient of an opposite-momentum product
is (e^a5)_2 e^-a5=a5(-1)1 and
(e^-a5)_2 e^a5=-a5(-1)1. Therefore

    Y_2 X5 = -2i a5(-1)1 != 0.

This is a weight-one state outside S1. In fact the generated VOA is
all of V_L. The displayed current acts at mode zero on Xi, i<=4,
to produce nonzero multiples of e^ai-e^-ai, since (a5,ai)=-1.
Together with Xi this separates e^ai and e^-ai. Their mode-two
products give all four basis currents. Those currents and the
lattice basis exponentials generate V_L.

The lattice has no norm-two vectors: for integer coordinates n,
the norm is 5 sum ni^2-(sum ni)^2, whose residue modulo5 lies in
{0,1,4}, whereas2 does not. Its full weight-one space is therefore
exactly the four-dimensional Heisenberg space.

## Consequence for the research proof

The usual Jacobi identities that reduce three-field low-weight
feedback are consistent with this example. The first new weight-two
state above requires four initial weight-two fields, through two
intermediate weight-three states. Thus those identities cannot be
extended to the requested universal closure theorem.

This counterexample does not establish that additional currents or
weight-two states occur in the particular Haagerup-generated VOA.
It establishes that the certified S1/S2 pairwise closure, PCT
stability, positivity, and presence of the full stress do not by
themselves exclude them. A properness proof needs a further global
constraint, an actual ambient symmetry, a strong-generation theorem,
or an independent decomposition of the generated subalgebra.
