## Reconstruction lemma

Suppose two simple CFT-type vertex algebras are generated by a Virasoro
field L and a primary a of weight three. Set b=a_1 a and d=a_1 b.
Suppose their full singular OPEs a_n a, a_n b, and a_n d agree, for
every n>=0, as the same normally ordered differential polynomials in
L,a,b,d. Then the two vertex algebras are isomorphic, with these
generators identified.

Here the phrase "same polynomials" includes the second-order pole
a_1 d: it must be given as a polynomial, not introduced as another
independent generator. In our application it is the weight-six
decoupling relation. It also includes both simple poles a_0 b and a_0 d.

To see the finite closure, the Virasoro OPEs with b and d follow by
commuting Virasoro modes past their definitions. For any s in
{L,a,b,d}, Borcherds' iterate identity gives

    b_n s = a_1(a_n s) - a_0(a_(n+1) s)
            + a_(n+1)(a_0 s) - a_n(a_1 s).

The known first-row OPEs, sesquilinearity and the noncommutative Wick
formula express this as a polynomial in L,a,b,d and their derivatives.
Likewise, from d=a_1 b,

    d_n s = a_1(b_n s) - a_0(b_(n+1) s)
            + b_(n+1)(a_0 s) - b_n(a_1 s).

Thus every pair of generators has the same singular OPE in both
algebras. All resulting polynomials have conformal weight strictly
less than the sum of the input weights. Perform the reductions in
increasing total input weight: b with b first, then b with d, then
d with d. Nested Wick brackets use only the already determined lower
total weights. The usual normal-order
spanning/reconstruction argument supplies a common graded universal
vertex algebra, spanned by differential normally ordered monomials
in the four positive-weight generators, which maps onto each algebra.
Its degree-zero space is C1, so it has a unique maximal proper graded
ideal. The simple quotients are consequently identical. Equivalently,
in the unitary case the common OPEs determine the Gram matrices of all
normally ordered monomials, and their radicals give the same quotient.

This argument does not require free generation: extra null relations
are allowed. It uses the full polynomial closure, and would not apply
to a comparison of only numerical dimensions or the leading pole.

## Consequence of the comparison

All gates hold, so the lemma identifies the subalgebra with
K(sl2,13), which is regular. The previously certified positive stress
identity then proves C2-cofiniteness of U. Explicitly, replace each
compact-group average by a finite convex combination. This writes
the stress of U as a positive linear combination of stresses of
regular affine, Potts and parafermion subalgebras.

For v in the maximal weak U-module D(U) inside the graded completion,
regularity makes each of these stress zero modes locally finite on v.
On each ambient grade they are Hermitian. Their finite sets of
eigenvalues on v give upper bounds M_i, so the positive stress identity
implies n*||v_n||^2 <= (sum_i c_i M_i)*||v_n||^2. Thus v has bounded
grade support. Hence D(U)=U, and the standard completion criterion
gives C2-cofiniteness. This is the argument of
[Dong–Wang, proof of Theorem 3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1005.1709),
applied to the exact stress decomposition here.

C2-cofiniteness does not prove U is proper, rational, or has the
Haagerup double as its representation category.
