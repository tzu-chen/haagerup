# Conditional X2 rank producer: resource and proof audit

23 September 2026. Producer prepared and syntax-checked only; no numerical
job was launched by this agent.

The new, self-contained driver is
`hi_cft_20260923_next_x2_conditional_rank.py`. It reads the existing exact
field certificate and imports the frozen grade-two engine and the existing
split-prime adapter. It does not edit either import or any old checkpoint.

## Mathematical certificate

Before reducing coefficients modulo p, the producer constructs the exact
rational Gram projection of all 86 ambient grade-one basis states onto the
orthogonal complement of the six K currents. It verifies six orthogonality
equations per vector and extracts an exact 80-dimensional basis. The Gram
matrix and every projection formula are saved. The invariant bilinear
orthogonal complement equals the Hermitian orthogonal complement because
the six-current space is PCT-stable.

It uses the already audited split prime p=1000003, with sqrt(13)=231543 and
sqrt(-3)=1000. The adapter verifies primality and both square-root identities;
its rational constructor rejects denominators divisible by p. Elimination
divides only nonzero field elements. The exact X1 basis remains rank80
after reduction. The certified S2 is reconstructed from explicit U words,
retaining 120 original representatives rather than complicated echelon
combinations.

The independent grade-two vectors are generated by K(-1) on X1, then by
all S2 mode-zero actions on X1. After each stage, the producer closes its
current finite span under the four K raising/lowering zero modes and u_1.
Cartan zero modes are scalar on each block. These are all actual U modes.
No claim about full U-module closure follows merely from this tested closure.

Every accepted vector is saved in a streamed JSONL record with its sparse
finite-field coefficients, word, source-vector number, pivot, and normalized
echelon-row hash. Operator definitions, exact X1 words, complete basis order,
source hashes, file hash, and block ranks are included in an atomic summary.
Thus both independent vectors and deterministic word replay are available;
the certificate is not merely a numerical rank claim. A process killed
between checkpoints can leave extra JSONL rows beyond the summary hash;
the last complete atomic summary identifies its certified prefix by the
`independent_vectors` count and SHA256. No resume is currently implemented.

Under the established properness fork B=U direct sum X, the exact initial
space is X1. Therefore every recorded vector lies in X2 if U is proper.
Since S2 is a 120-dimensional subspace of U2 and dimB2=1370:

* Rank1250 implies, conditionally on properness, X2 has dimension1250
  and U2=S2. This requires only a characteristic-zero lower bound.
* Rank greater than1250 contradicts properness, and the producer stops
  immediately after accepting the first such independent vector.
* Rank below1250 gives only a conditional lower bound.

Reduction modulo p can lower rank, so a modular rank below1250 cannot
prove an upper bound. A rank1250 result is not advertised as an unconditional
exact characteristic-zero rank of the generated span. The dimension
squeeze is the essential additional argument.

## Liveness and limits

The ambient grade-two basis has1370 states. The driver asserts every Cartan
block has at most100 states (the zero block was independently measured86).
It accepts at most1251 vectors, with at most100 terms each. Its echelon
rows and unreduced accepted vectors therefore each have at most125100
sparse entries. Actual blocks are much smaller on average. Expected combined
storage for these collections is below60 MiB including Python dictionaries,
integer field elements, state references, and temporary reduction copies.

Both vertex and basis-mode caches are replaced by single4096-entry bounded
caches. Their values are low-grade sparse primitive actions, not whole
field matrices. Conservative allowance for both caches, rational scalars,
state tuples, dot/cocycle caches and allocator overhead is128 MiB. The
largest mode evaluation has at most100 output coordinates and never
constructs a dense1370-square matrix. Inputs, exact X1 rational work,
120 S2 vectors, JSON source parsing, basis/word tables, checkpoint JSON and
one current output line are small; allow another48 MiB. Deserialized source
JSON is capped at8 MiB on disk and retained only once; its expansion is
included in that allowance.

Estimated peak RSS is below0.25 GiB; the review budget is0.5 GiB. The
driver checks high-water RSS after each candidate and stops at0.5 GiB,
though that check is at mode boundaries, not an allocation barrier. The
hard process address limit must be at most2 GiB and the permanent launcher
group/watchdog/reserve/thread constraints still apply. There are no worker
processes, NumPy/SciPy/BLAS libraries, or detached jobs.

The internal SIGALRM is300 seconds maximum. Root should additionally use
an external timeout if desired. Prior grade-three finite-field work of
similar size took47 seconds; these extra zero-mode closures could cost
more, so300 seconds is a bounded pilot, not a promised completion time.
The driver saves after each S2 operator. On its own timeout/RSS stop it
marks the last complete atomic summary stopped and preserves that summary's
certified prefix; it does not certify a potentially interrupted insertion.

Suggested root-owned launch shape, after obtaining the numerical slot:

    python hi_cft_safe_run.py --limit-mib 2048 -- timeout --signal=TERM --kill-after=15s 330s python hi_cft_20260923_next_x2_conditional_rank.py

The producer and imports must remain frozen once its first checkpoint is
written, because the certificate binds their SHA256 hashes.
