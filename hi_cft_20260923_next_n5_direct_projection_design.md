# Deferred direct projection of normal-word candidates

Design only,23September2026. Root explicitly prioritized the frozen paired
pipeline and completed exact certificates; no new producer or numerical job
is authorized by this note.

The present pipeline first forms an ordinary selected N5 prime span and then
projects its accepted raw columns to joint current highest spaces. For low
Cartan weights, most ordinary columns may disappear or become dependent
after projection. A future producer can instead stream exactly the same
normal-word candidates, apply the current-zero polynomial immediately, and
retain only the independent projected columns. Test projected targets after
a small number of new columns and stop as soon as all chosen targets lie
in the projected span. This needs no full ordinary N5 rank calculation or
ambient grade-five basis.

The candidate recipes remain the two already authenticated families:

* A current(-1) or translation mode on a saved raw N4 word or canonical P4.
* A frozen full-S2 basis field(-1) on a frozen S3 basis vector.

Every accepted record should contain its exact source recipe, target Cartan
weight, ordered LEFT/RIGHT FE projection factors, projected raw vector,
pivot and normalized prime row. Source metadata binds the frozen N4
SQLite/summary, exact checkpoint24, canonical P4 artifacts, field/mode engine
and new producer. Target records similarly contain A[i]_0 P4_j followed by
the same projector. Current covariance ensures every projected candidate
is still an actual normal word combination in N5. Exact membership still
requires rational reconstruction or exact lifting and a full sparse residual.

Two compatible ways to retain an exact certificate path are available.

1. **Small genuine raw source, proposed by c2_axes.** Keep the ordinary raw
   candidate only when its projection is independent. Projected independence
   implies independence of the corresponding ordinary vectors, so a small
   raw Gaussian reduction supplies valid ordinary pivots and rows for an
   ordinary-source database. It has at most as many rows as the projected
   rank, rather than the full ordinary Cartan rank. Retain the actual raw
   targets as well. The existing frozen exact selected-N5 provider and exact
   projected consumer can then remain unchanged. The two databases need a
   careful protocol: checkpoint their shared logical cursor consistently,
   close/freeze the raw database, compute its final digest, and only then
   finalize the projected source binding. Do not bind a still-mutating raw
   database or call a non-echelon candidate list an ordinary basis.

2. **Direct exact recipe adapter.** Give the new output a distinct format
   describing projected normal recipes, with no claimed ordinary N5 rank.
   An exact adapter needs only the existing grade-four raw-DAG provider:
   reconstruct each normal recipe from its exact S2/S3/N4/P4 inputs, apply
   the rational projector, compare the full prime specialization, and use
   the selected small projected pivot minor and full exact residual. This
   removes the intermediate raw-N5 database but needs a new source-audited
   adapter; it must not silently reinterpret the frozen ordinary format.

Retain the existing40000term vector cap, bounded primitive/composite caches,
rank≤300 review gate, disk limits, serial execution,3GiBAS and permanent host
reserve. A candidate's raw, raised, lowered and projected forms can overlap,
so projection does not automatically lower peak memory. Its expected benefit
is fewer retained columns, smaller exact systems and earlier containment,
not an unmeasured claim of faster mode generation. The approach is most
useful if the fresh operator6 measurement shows a large ordinary rank but
a much smaller highest rank.
