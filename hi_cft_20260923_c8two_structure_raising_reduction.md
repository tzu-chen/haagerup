# Raising coinvariants reduce the unfinished weight-four gate

New c=8-only window, 23 September 2026. This is a proved reduction and
root-only certificate design. No numerical job was launched by this agent.

Let Q=S3/N3 and W4=N4+P4. The frozen exact dimensions are dim S3=956,
dim N3=634, hence dim Q=322. The previously established current covariance
makes N3, S3, N4 and W4 stable under K=sl2+sl2. In particular, P4 is the
actual checked type (0,2), not an assumed three-dimensional quotient.
The lower closure identities give a_0 N3 subset N4 for each primitive
weight-two highest field a, since a_0 is a derivation of normal products.

Consequently

    f_a: Q -> B4/W4,       [p] -> [a_0 p]

is well defined. If E_L,E_R are the two raising zero modes, highestness
of a gives

    f_a(E_i q)=E_i f_a(q),       i=L,R.

The kernel of f_a is therefore stable under both commuting nilpotent
raising operators. This statement does not require any as-yet-unproved
weight-four containment.

Let C_a be the span in Q of right inputs whose a_0 products have already
passed a full exact H4 residual check. A sufficient condition for the
entire a-family is

    Q=E_L Q+E_R Q+C_a.                                      (1)

Indeed C_a is in the kernel. In M=Q/ker(f_a), equation (1) says
M=E_L M+E_R M. Iteration kills M, since a sufficiently high power of the
ideal generated by the two commuting raising operators is zero. Thus
f_a=0. This is elementary nilpotent Nakayama, not an assumption that
the normal span is already a vertex algebra.

Only the coinvariants Q/(E_L Q+E_R Q) matter. In each irreducible K-copy
one lowest-weight vector generates all vectors under the raising
operators. Thus one lowest-right input per irreducible copy suffices for
each highest-left a. The earlier multiple highest-channel test list was
sufficient but not minimal for this purpose. No PCT or Weyl identification
of the chosen P4 space is needed.

The same observation says that a raw a_0 P4_minus containment would imply
all three a_0 P4 containments. A projected lowest channel alone does not:
projection discards the other tensor-product channels. The raw lowest
weight block may be much larger, so this is not automatically a faster
grade-five computation.

It forms the raising-image span plus these seed coordinates. In exact

quotient. Greedily adding saved P3 basis inputs until full rank chooses
exactly this many additional inputs. This is the minimum number of
additional scalar inputs needed by criterion (1), not a claim that no
other VOA identity could imply some of them. No dense new field is needed.

The machine-readable plan has

    additional_tests: [{operator: integer, exact_input: saved_row_name, ...}]

and per-operator status both with current seeds and conditional on the

In optional prime mode, full rank 322 is still a valid characteristic-zero
spanning certificate. Every column is the specialization of an actual

## Reductions not used

PCT/Weyl reflection cannot silently identify the unfinished blocks:
current stability gives Weyl symmetry, but applying a Weyl element also
changes the fixed highest-left field. PCT stability of the selected
W4 would itself need proof and is not inferred from a modular quotient
dimension. The new argument uses raising intertwining directly.
Likewise the previously rejected single-u shortcut still has a same-weight
mode cycle and is not revived by this coinvariant argument.
