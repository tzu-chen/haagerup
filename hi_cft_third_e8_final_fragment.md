For a strongly regular unitary c=8 chiral algebra with the specified
Haagerup modular data, the nineteen-current character is impossible.
The six-current character has only two surviving current algebras:
A1_1 times A1_39 and A1_3 times A1_13. Their full-current commutants
have central charges170/41 and18/5. In particular this construction
cannot produce a current commutant near charge2. This is a theorem
about the proposed chiral construction, not a bound on the chain's
central charge.

The proof combines exact quadratic and quartic current traces with
finite-dimensional representations in the published character system.
The weight-two and weight-four modular-form spaces and all moment
identities were verified by guarded Fraction certificates. Odd graded
character coefficients fix the symmetric invariant-form conditions.
The mixed and fourth traces exclude A1 plus three abelian currents,
and exhaustive finite support for the small grounds and d5,d6 leaves
the two pairs above. No nonvacuum-module energy comparison or automatic
unitarizability assumption is used in these final finite-representation
constraints. Earlier bounds67/22 and the archived catalog counts have
been superseded by this two-pair result.

In the additional actual inclusion W contained in B=E6_1 times A2_1,
the two placements are fixed by the grade-one trace and embedding
classification. Their current-commutant weight-two algebras have
dimensions4 and5. Exact Griess calculations exclude every Hermitian
charge-two stress invariant under the full current group. The executed
factored grade-two character check further shows that the corresponding
retained commutant inside W has weight-two dimension2 in each case.
These conclusions concern B, not the larger E8 commutant.

Classifying the real Griess axes and testing both complementary stress
moments leaves only the charge pair(2400/1271,70/31) for the first
placement and(32/19,182/95) for the second, each with two signs of the
extra weight-two field. The inserted-current Jacobi identity gives an
exact additional exclusion: the other surviving second-placement pair,
(10/7,76/35), forces three ground-block eigenvalues whose weighted
squares disagree with its modular second moment. The remaining pairs
pass the complete tested ground constraints. This selects necessary
Virasoro factors in a putative W; it does not construct the factors'
full chiral algebras or establish their rationality.

There is still an explicit noninvariant charge-two stress: the Cartan
Heisenberg stress. The Heisenberg-coset rationality theorem and the
rank-two restriction lemma force its actual rank-two lattice commutant
inside B to lie entirely in W. The canonical residual channel on that
lattice is therefore the identity. This rules out that particular
Cartan route to a nontrivial residual Haagerup action; it does not
classify every noninvariant conformal vector or every physical defect
action on modules.
