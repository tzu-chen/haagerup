# The actual residual cosets are standard rational diagonal cosets

23 September 2026. The lattice-neighbor audit and affine-pair uniqueness
identify the complete opposite commutants, not just their weight-two
algebras. Write Lambda for the actual E8 lattice, B=V_(E6+A2), and
C_E=Com(A_L,V_Lambda), C_B=Com(A_L,B). Then

\[
 C_E\cong Y=\operatorname{Com}(A_{1,13},F_{4,1}\otimes A_{1,1}).
\]

The order-three grading of V_Lambda over B transports through the
neighbor. The argument below identifies its action on Y and gives

\[
 C_B\cong \operatorname{Com}(A_{1,13},
             A_{2,1}\otimes A_{2,2}\otimes A_{1,1}),
\]

where the diagonal A1 has levels 4+8+1. Both cosets are strongly
rational. This does not yet prove that the original generated algebra
U is proper in B, or identify Rep(U).

 D'=(A_{1,3}\otimes A_{1,13})\oplus(J_3\otimes J_{13}).

## Transporting the order-three grading

Let chi:Lambda→Z/3 have kernel the E6+A2 lattice. Its grading
automorphism g has fixed VOA B. With the notation of the independent
audit, a=a0 and t lie in this kernel. Put

\[
 \Lambda_0=\{x\in\Lambda:(x,t)\in2\mathbb Z\},\qquad
 \Lambda'=\Lambda_0\cup(t/2+\Lambda_0).
\]

There is an unambiguous character chi' on Lambda' given by
chi'(x)=chi(x) for x in Lambda_0 and chi'(t/2)=0. The relation
2(t/2)=t is compatible because chi(t)=0. On N its grading action is
the original action. On the transported core it is trivial: natural
glue transport replaces n mu+eta by n mu+eta+n a, and n a has zero
character; the new torus generators t/2 and 2a have zero character.
Thus g' fixes D' pointwise, including both affine factors. Its fixed
algebra on the shared residual C_E is precisely C_B.

After conjugating the affine pair to the standard pair, g' preserves
its full centralizer F4,1 tensor A1,1. This VOA is generated by its
currents, so its automorphism is determined by the two simple Lie
factors. The factors cannot be exchanged. Pointwise fixation of the
diagonal level-thirteen sl2 forces identity on the external A1 and
pointwise fixation of the index-twelve sl2 in F4. Consequently its
action on Y comes from the finite centralizer of that F4 sl2 triple.

## The finite centralizer and its order-three conjugacy class

For the index-twelve embedding the F4 adjoint restricts as

\[
 52=6V_2\oplus4V_4\oplus2V_6.
\]

Hence the centralizer of the nilpotent raising operator has dimension
6+4+2=12, while the full sl2-triple centralizer has zero-dimensional
Lie algebra. This is the orbit F4(a3). The characteristic can also be
chosen as h=(4,2,2,0) in the usual Euclidean F4 coordinates. Against
the simple roots e2-e3, e3-e4, e4, (e1-e2-e3-e4)/2 its weighted
Dynkin diagram is 0200 and h squared is24, giving index12.

[Westaway, Section4.9](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.01838v3)
identifies this precise characteristic-zero weighted diagram with
F4(a3) and component group S4. Independently,
[Xue, Section5.1 and Table13](https://jolt.centre-mersenne.org/item/10.5802/jolt.950.pdf)
give the centralizer dimension12 and the same component group, and
identify their F4 orbit/component data with characteristic zero.
The sl2-triple centralizer
is a Levi subgroup of the nilpotent centralizer. Its identity
component is trivial here, so the full triple centralizer is S4.

The restriction of g' to Y is nontrivial: its fixed weight-two space
has dimension five, whereas Y_2 has dimension thirteen. Therefore
its F4 component is an element of order three in this S4. All such
elements are conjugate in S4, and the conjugation fixes the chosen
sl2 triple.

One standard such element is the grading automorphism for the
conformal Z3 extension

\[
 F_{4,1}\supset A_{2,1}\otimes A_{2,2}.
\]

The two nontrivial simple-current summands have current tops
(3,6bar) and (3bar,6), of dimension eighteen each. Its fixed VOA is
exactly A2,1 tensor A2,2. The principal A1 in the two A2 factors has
levels four and eight, so the grading element centralizes the same
index-twelve diagonal A1. Conjugating the actual order-three element
to this representative identifies C_B with the displayed standard
triple coset. No uniqueness assertion for abstract mirror VOAs is
used.

As a further source check,
[Premet, Section3.9, case4](https://eprints.maths.manchester.ac.uk/496/2/nilp.pdf)
identifies the order-three component class for F4(a3) with
A2 plus tilde-A2, consistently with this representative.

## A complete rational conformal frame

The standard inclusions give three commuting simple unitary factors
inside both standard residual cosets, with stresses summing to their
full residual stress:

\[
 \operatorname{Vir}(4/5)\ \otimes\
 \operatorname{Com}(A_{1,12},A_{1,4}\otimes A_{1,8})\ \otimes\
 \operatorname{Vir}(34/35).
\]

The first stress is omega_(A2,2)-omega_(A1,8); unitarity identifies
the VOA generated by that stress with the simple unitary minimal
Virasoro model at c=4/5. The last is the level-one GKO coset for
(12,1;13), namely the minimal model M(14,15). The middle factor has
central charge 64/35 and is strongly rational by
[Lin, Theorem5.7](https://arxiv.org/html/2107.09400),
applied at k=8. Positive integer levels are admissible. The charge
sum is 4/5+64/35+34/35=18/5.

Both residual cosets are simple unitary VOAs of CFT type. Their
restrictions to this regular conformal subVOA are direct sums of
ordinary modules; finite-dimensional homogeneous pieces make all
multiplicities finite, because there are finitely many simple modules
and the conformal vectors agree. The extension result reproduced
as Lin Theorem3.10 gives C2-cofiniteness. We do not use the blanket
rational-extension assertion reproduced as Lin Theorem3.11, so no
unstated nonzero categorical-dimension hypothesis enters here.

Return now to the original embeddings A_L in V_Lambda and in B.
The core is strongly rational and is its own double commutant in
each ambient VOA, by
`hi_cft_20260923_next_structure_core_maximality.md`. Its stress is
quasiprimary for the ambient conformal vector. The two ambient VOAs
are strongly rational lattice VOAs. The just-proved C2-cofiniteness
therefore verifies all assumptions of
[McRae, Theorem6.11](https://arxiv.org/html/2108.01898), which gives
strong rationality of C_E and C_B directly. There is no need to
transport a maximality theorem to D', or prove positivity for every
abstract module of the level-four diagonal coset. The rationality
proof uses only the minimal Virasoro algebra in the first factor;
establishing a Potts weight-three generator is unnecessary.

Alternatively, once Y is regular, its order-three fixed algebra is
regular by
[Carnahan--Miyamoto](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.05645).

The exact residual-axis charge spectrum independently contains the
expected minimal coset charges 6/7, 52/55 and34/35 for the pairings
(4,1;5), (8,1;9) and(12,1;13). Its unique c=4/5 axis has spectrum
0^3,(2/5)^1,2^1 on C_B, also agreeing with the standard construction.
These exact finite calculations support, but are not needed in place
of, the all-weight neighbor proof above.
