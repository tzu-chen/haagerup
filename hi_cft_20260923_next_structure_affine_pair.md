# The level-three / level-thirteen affine pair in E8 is unique

23 September 2026. Any pair of commuting affine A1 subVOAs at levels
three and thirteen in V_E8 is conjugate to the standard pair obtained
from G2,1 tensor F4,1, with A1,3 in G2,1 and diagonal A1,13 in
the centralizing A1,1 tensor F4,1. This identifies their full
commutant with

\[
 Y=\operatorname{Com}(A_{1,13},A_{1,1}\otimes F_{4,1}).
\]

Applying this result to the actual residual VOA still requires the
lattice-neighbor / parafermion reconstruction audited separately. The
affine-pair argument itself has no residual-rationality hypothesis.

## Uniqueness of the first A1

For an index-three sl2 in E8, the characteristic Cartan element h is
an E8 cocharacter of norm six, with the normalization [h,e]=2e.
The E8 cocharacter lattice equals its root lattice. In the standard
Euclidean realization, its norm-six vectors have precisely three
coordinate types, up to permutation and signs:

\[
 (2,1,1,0^5),\quad(1^6,0^2),\quad
 ((3/2)^2,(1/2)^6).
\]

There are respectively 1344, 1792 and 3584 such vectors. The Weyl
group of D8, contained in that of E8, is transitive within each type:
zero coordinates remove the sign-parity distinction in the integral
types, and E8's half-integral parity condition is exactly the even-sign
condition in the last type. Reflection in the root
delta=(1/2,...,1/2) joins the types. It sends

\[
 (2,1,1,0^5)\longmapsto(1,0,0,-1^5),
\]

using inner product two, and sends (2,1,-1,0^5), whose inner product
with delta is one, to a vector of the half-integral type. Thus all
norm-six characteristics form one E8 Weyl orbit.

For completeness, two sl2 triples with the same h are conjugate by
the centralizer G_0 of h. In the h-grading g=direct_sum g_j,
the sl2 representation theory gives [g_0,e]=g_2: each even
highest-weight representation has a surjective raising map from
weight zero to weight two. Hence the G_0 orbit of each such e is open
in g_2. Two nonempty open orbits in this irreducible vector space
coincide. The triple, including f, is then determined up to conjugacy.

The standard index-three A1 lies in G2,1, whose conformal
A1,1 tensor A1,3 subalgebra is familiar. Its centralizer in E8 is
F4 plus A1. This can also be read from

\[
 248=(14,1)+(1,52)+(7,26),\qquad
 7\downarrow(A_1\times A_1)=(2,2)+(1,3).
\]

The G2 adjoint has a single A1,3-invariant A1,1 adjoint, and the
seven has none. Thus the current centralizer is exactly F4⊕A1,
with both factors at level one.

## The second A1 must use the index-twelve F4 embedding

A commuting A1,13 lies in that current centralizer. Its projection
to the A1 factor has index zero or one. The possible F4 indices are

\[
 1,2,3,4,6,8,9,10,11,12,28,35,36,60,156,
\]

and an F4 sl2 is determined up to conjugacy by its index. In
particular index thirteen is absent and index twelve is unique.
These consequences of Dynkin's classification are explicitly recorded
in [Penkov–Zuckerman, Section 3, Table B](https://math.constructor.university/penkov/papers/gen_hc_r_final.pdf).
The index-thirteen sum therefore has projections 12+1. The index-twelve
representative is the diagonal principal A1 of the commuting A2
subalgebras at indices one and two, as constructed in the F4 note.
Conjugating within F4 times A1 fixes the first A1,3.

## Why this is a full VOA commutant statement

The conformal branching

\[
 G_{2,1}=(A_{1,1}^{(0)}\otimes A_{1,3}^{(0)})
       \oplus(A_{1,1}^{(1)}\otimes A_{1,3}^{(3)})
\]

and the branching of its seven-dimensional module into labels
(0,2) and (1,1) show that the A1,3 vacuum occurs in V_E8 precisely
with multiplicity VOA A1,1 tensor F4,1. The superscripts here denote
integrable affine highest-weight labels. Hence

\[
 \operatorname{Com}(A_{1,3},V_{E8})=A_{1,1}\otimes F_{4,1},
\]

and taking the second commutant gives Y as claimed. All inner Lie
automorphisms used above lift to V_E8 because it is generated by
its affine E8 currents.

The integer simple-current extension
D_std=(A1,3 tensor A1,13) direct_sum(J_3 tensor J_13) has the same
conformal vector as the affine pair. In a unitary ambient VOA their
zero-energy commutants coincide, so replacing the pair by D_std does
not change Y.
