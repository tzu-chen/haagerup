## 2. Exact properness dichotomy

Let \(K_1=U_1^{\rm known}\) be the six affine currents, and let
\(b_1=K_1^\perp\subset B_1\). Its dimension is 80. Exact arithmetic
verifies that K zero modes and all the known weight-two zero modes
preserve \(b_1\). Their action on \(b_1\) is **irreducible**.

Here is the finite-dimensional proof. The joint Cartan eigenspace of
weight (0,10) in B1 is one dimensional. Its vector is cyclic for the
tested zero-mode algebra: exact elimination gives all 80 dimensions.
The algebra is closed under adjoints, by the certified PCT stability and
the current/weight-two adjoint formulas. An invariant orthogonal
decomposition would put this one-dimensional eigenspace entirely in
one summand; cyclicity forces the other summand to vanish.

Consequently,
\[
\boxed{U\ne B\ \Longrightarrow\ U_1=K_1,\quad \dim U_1=6.}
\]
Indeed, any extra current has a nonzero component in b1; irreducibility
would put all b1 in U. Since B is generated by B1, that would imply U=B.
This does **not** decide which alternative holds.

There is a further conditional consequence: if U is proper, the
U-module generated by b1 is an irreducible module of lowest weight one
with 80-dimensional top space. Positive-energy modes cannot map b1
to the vacuum, since their adjoints map the vacuum into U1, orthogonal
to b1. Unitarity splits submodules, and the irreducible zero-mode
action on b1 then makes the generated module irreducible. This does
not assert that B has only these two U-module summands.

## 3. An actual Potts algebra inside U

\(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}4&-2\\-2&4\end{smallmatrix}\right)\), so

\[
p_1p=2p,\quad p_2p=0,\quad p_3p=2/5,\quad \theta p=p.

\((h_\alpha(-1)^2+h_\beta(-1)^2+h_\alpha(-1)h_\beta(-1))/15\).
The six vertex coefficients are recorded in the certificate. In the
physical gauge, p is a torus/cocycle transform of the standard Potts
vector in \(V_{\sqrt2 A_2}\).

The standard weight-three extension generator also belongs to U,
**exactly**, not merely numerically. With
\(A=\alpha-\beta\), \(D=\alpha+2\beta\),
\(E=-2\alpha-\beta\), and \(X_\delta=5p_\delta e^\delta\), it is
\[
w_P=h_Ah_Dh_E
 -9\{h_A(X_\gamma+X_{-\gamma})
      +h_D(X_\alpha+X_{-\alpha})
      +h_E(X_\beta+X_{-\beta})\}.
\]
Products here are normally ordered. Exact mode calculations verify
\(p_1w_P=3w_P\) and \(p_nw_P=0\) for n=2,3,4. Exact elimination
expresses it using already generated weight-three products.

Thus

P=L(4/5,0)\oplus L(4/5,3)\subset U

is the rational three-state Potts VOA. The identification uses the
standard lattice realization, including its weight-three generator,

## 4. A rational c=14/5 core, with a determinant-39 lattice

Let
\[
t=a+b+c=(1,1,0,1,1,-3,1,1),\qquad
r=t/3=a_0/2+H/6.
\]
Then \(r^2=8/3\), and the exact calculation gives
\[
p_1u=\tfrac23u,\quad p_2u=p_3u=0,\qquad
u_{-5}(u_{-3}u)=-\tfrac35 e^t.
\]
The last identity only needs leading lattice products: all omitted
powers are too high to contribute. It therefore does not require
enumerating the weight-twelve ambient space.

Together with PCT and the left currents, this puts V_L in U, where
\[
L=\mathbb Za_0+\mathbb Zt,\qquad
G_L=\begin{pmatrix}2&3\\3&24\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det G_L=39.
\]
The Potts plane is orthogonal to this rank-two lattice. The field u is
a highest vector in \(V_{L+r}\otimes\psi\), where \(\psi\) is a
Potts simple current of weight 2/3. Its lattice weight is 4/3. Its
total weight is therefore the observed integer 2.

The subVOA generated by V_L, P, u and its PCT conjugate is the actual
three-sector simple-current extension
\[
T=(V_L\otimes P)\oplus(V_{L+r}\otimes\psi)
                  \oplus(V_{L+2r}\otimes\psi^2).
\]
The order-three fusion closure and the unique vacuum prevent extra
copies in this generated extension. Unitarity identifies the
zero-energy part of the complementary stress with the commutant, so
the charged vectors have no hidden positive-energy factor.
Consequently T is strongly rational, by the simple-current extension
theorem; see [Arakawa–Lam–Yamada, Theorem 2.4](https://arxiv.org/html/1701.06229).

Its stress tensor and central charge are
\[
\omega_T=\tfrac14 h_{a_0}(-1)^2+\tfrac1{156}h_H(-1)^2+p,
\qquad c_T=14/5.
\]
The complementary stress \(\omega_B-\omega_T\) has c=26/5. All the
commuting-stress identities were checked exactly.

There is also a simple categorical description of this **known core**.
The discriminant group of L is cyclic of order 39, generated by
\(g=H/39\), with q(g)=1/39. Moreover r=26g modulo L.
Condensing the diagonal order-three simple current in
\(\operatorname{Rep}(V_L\otimes P)\) leaves the order-thirteen
generator 3g, of quadratic form \(q(j)=3j^2/13\). Hence
\[
\operatorname{Rep}(T)\simeq
\mathrm{Fib}\boxtimes\mathcal C(\mathbb Z_{13},3j^2/13),
\quad \operatorname{Dim}\operatorname{Rep}(T)
=\frac{13(5+\sqrt5)}2.
\]
This is standard simple-current condensation applied to the certified
lattice data. It is **not** the representation category of U.
In particular T has four currents and is not G2 at level one, despite
having the same central charge.

**Theorem for the constructed candidate.** U is C2-cofinite.
The identified parafermion algebra is regular, by
[Arakawa–Lam–Yamada](https://arxiv.org/html/1701.06229).
The proof now applies the positive-stress argument in
[Dong–Wang, proof of Theorem 3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1005.1709):
restriction of a weak module in the completion to each regular
subalgebra makes its stress zero mode locally finite. Positivity and
the displayed identity then bound the ambient grades of each vector,
so that the maximal weak module in the completion is U itself.

Matching c and lambda alone would not have discharged the hypothesis.

Properness, rationality and the Haagerup representation category remain

C2-cofiniteness also makes the branching of B finite. As a unitary
ordinary U-module, B decomposes orthogonally into irreducible modules:
graded invariant subspaces have invariant orthogonal complements.
C2-cofiniteness gives finitely many irreducible U-module types. Each
type has a fixed lowest conformal weight, and its multiplicity is
bounded by the finite dimension of that graded piece of B. Thus only
finitely many summands occur. This proves finite module branching,
not a numerical categorical index or a classification of all sectors.

## 9. What still separates this candidate from a Haagerup existence proof

The necessary all-weight claims must not be replaced by low-weight
agreement:

1. **Properness:** prove U is not the entire lattice VOA B. The exact
   irreducible current module gives a sharp dichotomy but does not
   resolve it.
2. **Rationality and sector data:** establish semisimplicity and
   identify the irreducible modules, their characters and fusion.
   C2-cofiniteness alone is not used as a rationality theorem.
3. **Haagerup identification:** prove the required categorical
   equivalence, or construct the corresponding noninvertible symmetry
   with its associativity data. A match of several character
   coefficients is insufficient.

The proposed rank-two extension would have B=U plus a module with
80-dimensional weight-one space and extension dimension
(13+3*sqrt(13))/2. Neither that full branching nor that dimension has
been proved here. The conditional construction in
[Gannon–Rayhaun, Section 4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.05279v1#S4.SS3)
does not by itself supply existence.

The session stays on these construction questions. No identification
with the c approximately 2 chain is claimed.
