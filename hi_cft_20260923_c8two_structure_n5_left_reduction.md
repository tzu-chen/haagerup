# Moving current lowerings off the left factor in grade five

This is an algebraic reduction for the full formal normal spaces. It was
independently checked by the c2_axes agent. It does not establish an exact
upper rank for the 6,007 selected grade-four raw words used by the current
producers, and does not change those producers' positive-subset scope.

Let P_w be the joint highest-weight projector for the two compact sl2
zero-mode actions, at a weight w with nonnegative coordinates. For either
lowering operator F_i,

    P_w F_i |_(weight w+2e_i) = 0.

Indeed, every irreducible summand meeting weight w+2e_i has highest weight
at least w+2e_i in that factor. Its weight-w vector has no component in the
highest-weight-w summands selected by P_w.

Current zero modes are derivations of every vertex product. Consequently,

    P_w ((F_i a)_(-1) p) = -P_w (a_(-1) (F_i p)).

The two lowering actions commute, so this identity can be iterated in both
factors. Since the exact space S3 is K-stable, all lowerings of its vectors
remain linear combinations of its authenticated exact basis. Therefore
the highest projection of every product of a current descendant of a
primitive weight-two highest field with S3 is in the span of projected
products having the primitive highest field itself on the left.

The actual exact S2 construction is the span of the current normal space
K(-1)K + TK and the K-modules generated by the ten original primitive
weight-two fields. This decomposition is directly visible in the
weight-two construction of `hi_cft_20260923_c8_exact_grade3.py`, whose
number-field run has dimension 120.

The current normal portion does not require additional primitive products.
For currents x,y and p in S3, the iterate identity is

    (:xy:)_(-1) p
      = sum_(i>=0) x_(-1-i) y_(-1+i) p
        + sum_(i>=0) y_(-2-i) x_i p.

The sums truncate. The certified current positive modes put the inner
vectors in the known lower spaces; y(-1)p is already a normal weight-four
vector. The identity [T,x(-r)]=r x(-r-1) then reduces every higher negative
current term into

    D5 = K(-1) W4 + T W4,
    W4 = N4 + P4,

where N4 is the full formal normal weight-four space. The case TK is the
same translation identity. Thus

    P_w N5 = P_w (D5 + sum_a a_(-1) S3),

with a running through the ten primitive highest fields. This proves the
left-factor reduction without assuming strong generation or properness.

There is a similar reduction for D5 itself. The two current adjoint
representations are generated by the raising currents E_left and E_right.
If the entire K-stable W4 is available on the right, the same transfer
identity reduces projected current(-1) products to these two highest
currents, together with the translation products.

The last premise must be respected in implementations. A selected list of
6,007 actual normal words is known to lie in formal N4, but finite-prime
selection alone does not prove that its characteristic-zero span equals
formal N4 or is K-stable. Hence the saved primitive producer remains a
positive containment search. Replacing its six current families by two
without also supplying the needed lowered right inputs is not justified
by this theorem alone. An exact identity obtained from any of these
positive candidate subsets remains a valid identity in formal N5.
