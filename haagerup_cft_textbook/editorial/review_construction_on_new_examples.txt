l04: SVD, optimal truncation, and variational update
--------------------------------------------------
1. l04_tensor_networks.tex:24--29.
With coefficient matrix M=U Sigma V^dagger, the right ket has
coefficients conjugate(V_ja), as stated. Omitting that conjugation
would give the wrong coefficient matrix for complex states.

2. Lines 66--82.
For an arbitrary normalized Schmidt-rank-D vector, projection onto
its left support gives squared overlap <=Tr(P_W rho_L). The Ky Fan
bound gives sum of the D largest p_a, and the normalized Schmidt
truncation attains it. This optimizes over all support subspaces,
not just coordinate truncations. If epsilon is the discarded weight,
squared overlap is 1-epsilon and the phase-aligned squared vector
distance is 2-2 sqrt(1-epsilon). The text keeps these conventions
separate. The single-cut versus full-optimization warning is correct.

3. Lines 84--93.
For H=-|Phi><Phi|, every product state has energy >=-1/2, attained
for example by |00>. Bond dimension two reaches -1. This is a
clean example of an exact variational minimum inside an insufficient
family, rather than an algorithm failing to converge.

4. Lines 158--186.
The map from one site's tensor coordinates to the physical state is
linear with all other site tensors fixed. N=T^dagger T and
H_eff=T^dagger H T give the generalized Rayleigh quotient. Variation
gives H_eff a=E N a. On ker N, T a=0 and H_eff a=0, so removing the
null coordinates is legitimate. On the remaining positive support,
N^-1/2 H_eff N^-1/2 is Hermitian and its lowest eigenvalue is the
minimum in the linear update space.

5. Lines 188--200.
For v_1=|00>, v_2=|Phi>, the displayed N and H_eff are correct.
At a=(0,1), H_eff a=-N a and the physical state is |Phi>.
At a=(sqrt2,-1), the physical state is (|00>-|11>)/sqrt2,
which has unit norm and energy zero. H_eff alone has trace -3/2
and determinant zero, hence eigenvalues -3/2,0. The apparent
energy below -1 really is a coordinate-norm error.

6. Lines 203--226.
The open-chain canonical-gauge simplification, null support caveat,
and uniform-tensor caveat are all needed and correctly stated.
Changing one shared tensor everywhere is not a linear global update.
Monotonicity holds for an exact one-site minimization that contains
the old vector; a subsequent rank truncation can raise the energy.
None of this establishes a global nonlinear optimum.

7. l_solutions.tex:280--293.
For p=(1/2,1/3,1/6), the best rank-two state has amplitudes
sqrt(3/5),sqrt(2/5), fidelity5/6, norm error
sqrt(2-2sqrt(5/6)), and projector energy-5/6. Best rank-one
energy is-1/2. The solution correctly includes arbitrary basis changes.

l06: light-shell bound and cubic normalization
----------------------------------------------
8. l06_identification.tex:80--178.
The integral Lorentzian pairing bound 2 sqrt(Delta_p Delta_q)<1
forces zero on the light shells; their span is isotropic of dimension
at most two. For equal radii, each momentum lies in at most one
equilateral triad. The displayed expansion
sum T_i^2=1+(sum b_i c_i)^2-2 sum b_i^2 c_i^2<=4/3
is correct. Pairing opposite triads for a Hermitian field gives
(4/sqrt3) sum a_T^3 <= sqrt(2/3) when2 sum a_T^2<=1.
Thus the complex and Hermitian constants have the stated factor.

9. Lines 211--237 and 405--442.
For a unit charged C3 field, only phi^3 and (phi^dagger)^3
survive in the Hermitian quadrature's self-cubic. Their sum divided
by(sqrt2)^3 is Re(exp(3i theta)c_phi)/sqrt2.
The orthogonal stabilizer of a nonzero harmonic cubic on the real
plane is D3. A reflection-fixed axis is a cubic extremum, not an
arbitrary zero-cubic direction. Therefore the final multiplication
by sqrt2 is necessary and gives2/sqrt3 exactly.

l06: shift subgroup and isometric embedding
-------------------------------------------
10. Lines 284--319.
The current-trivial kernel has no charge-lattice action, so the OPE
makes its phases characters of Gamma. Effectiveness gives distinct
characters and distinct shifts. The annihilator argument proves the
restriction map Gamma->K-hat is onto, hence index|K|. A consistent
projection in each shifted sector selects one nonempty Gamma_0 coset,
also with allowed ordinary torsion. The |K| cosets are disjoint.
OPE closure and bosonic locality provide an even integral additive
lattice; dividing covolume|K| by the number of cosets gives1.
The conclusion is another Narain lattice, not merely an invariant
sublattice with missing sectors. Consistency of the actual gauging
is an essential stated premise here.

11. Lines 321--329.
The subgroup lift concerns the K-monodromy-supported subspace with
the remaining equivariance retained. Local sphere monodromies then
generate only K holonomies, and the common gauge-volume factor is
removed by vacuum normalization. Thus normalized correlators and the
two-point metric agree with the corresponding subspace of the
K orbifold. The proof does not assume a geometric residual quotient
or introduce an extra sqrt(|G|) in a normalized cubic. This scope
matches the normal-subgroup argument used later.

l06: rotations and determinant-character selection
--------------------------------------------------
12. Lines 331--368.
For the identity component of the integral SO(2,2) action, finite
lifts in SL2 x SL2 have rotation fractions0,1/2,+/-1/3,+/-1/4,

13. Lines 369--403, including the newly added regulator derivation.
The expansion of exp(-a epsilon)/(1-exp(-epsilon)) gives
S_a=epsilon^-2-B_2(a)/2+O(epsilon). Since
B_2(1-theta)=B_2(theta), the regulated half-difference is
theta(1-theta)/2. For a scalar, both chiral weights must rise
to at least the larger oscillator floor. Consequently the bound
uses max(theta_L(1-theta_L),theta_R(1-theta_R)), not their
average. The smallest nonzero table bound is5/36.

For(1/12,5/12), the ground weights are11/288,35/288.
Their sum23/144 has spin-1/12, so it is not a scalar state.
The scalar bound is35/144 and need not be attained.
The accompanying orbifold solution at l_solutions.tex:525--542
has all these fractions and normalization factors correct.

14. Lines 405--442.
The quantum-character image is abelian, hence1,C2,orC3 insideD3.
In the first two cases the selected real direction is fixed by all
characters, so its monodromy support lies in[G,G]. Both determinant
characters are therefore+1. In the C3 case the charged eigenline
defines an element of exact order3 in G_ab. Every order-two
determinant character evaluates trivially on that element.
This makes the proper-rotation threshold applicable in both cases.
It does not require identifying the physical C3 with a quantum
symmetry. The hypothesis that the doublet is the entire real scalar
eigenspace is indispensable for the D3 restriction and is on page.

f05: regularized gamma integral and shifts
------------------------------------------
17. f05_arithmetic.tex:183--220.
The small-t hyperbolic quotient is
lambda/(2 omega1 omega2 t)
+lambda(lambda^2-omega1^2-omega2^2)t/(12 omega1 omega2)+O(t^3).
After the common-integrand subtraction and dt/t it is bounded.
The strip gives exponential decay of the hyperbolic term and the
subtracted tail is O(t^-2). Uniform compact bounds justify
holomorphy; the exponential has no strip zeros. Reflection,
midpoint normalization1, and positivity on the real strip follow.

18. Lines232--269.

gamma(omega2)=2pi R/omega1. Reflection and positivity give

f05: column transform to all quadratics
---------------------------------------
20. Lines338--392.
The analytic column transform and the restricted contour continuation
are stated as supplied analytic input with their domain. I did not
independently re-prove that contour theorem. I checked the exceptional
sample bookkeeping: away from a=h, the kernel residue equals
(-1)^h sqrt(d_n) F(h) u_h(a) exp(-2pi i k a/n). At a=h,
the difference is precisely that prefactor times
(1+1/d_n)exp(-2pi i k h/n), producing the printed omega2
correction. The endpoint phase agreement for even h is correct.

21. Lines396--423.
For odd h and0<=k<=(n-1)/2, the companion frequency is
pi-2pi k/n. The four printed gamma arguments satisfy
x_++y1=Omega+omega1 and x_-+y2=omega1. Their sine quotient
has exponent sign(-1)^((h-n)/2+k); the exponential prefactors
give the same sign times exp(-2pi i kh/n), so the signs cancel.
The denominator argument lies in(0,1): it is
h/(2omega1)+omega2(1/2-k/n)>0, and at k=0 it is below1
because h<n. This verifies the Fourier-product normalization.

22. Lines425--447.
Rotation symmetry gives B_(h,a)=u_(-h)(a-h). The transform of
C_h(g) has the factor exp(+2pi i kh/n), cancelling the product's
negative phase. A constant transform tau0^2 inverts to
tau0^2 delta_(g,0), with no extra n. At h=0 direct expansion
gives n-2tau0+tau0^2 delta_(g,0); the dimension equation makes
its constant term -tau0^2/delta. Thus the argument proves all
quadratics conditional on the stated analytic column identity.
