Overall result
--------------
The retained-algebra exclusions are substantially and appropriately more
limited than exclusions by central charge. I found no wrong rational value,
twist-residue list, norm obstruction, or concrete counterexample to the stated
candidate conclusions. In particular the E6 partial/fully condensed branches
are correctly distinguished, and the remaining intermediate-extension problem
is explicitly left open. The principal improvements are an explicit global
unitarity/completeness hypothesis, a defect-category inclusion rather than an
equality, and a slightly more conditional description of the E6 transfer.

The f05 result is not merely speculative or based on finite numerical
residuals: the repository contains the final all-odd-order formal coefficient
theorem. Conversely, the short revised preprint still has documented analytic
exposition gaps, and neither the formal coefficient theorem nor its recorded
axiom audit formalizes the categorical reconstruction or CFT conclusion. The
textbook must represent all three facts together. I implemented this distinction
in f05 with parent authorization.

1. Candidate-family hypotheses and category conventions
------------------------------------------------------

1a. l06:435–450 and 622–646: make the shared assumptions explicit.

The underlying audit results concern bosonic unitary rational full CFTs,
retaining the specified rational chiral algebra in both chiralities, with
complete physical sewing/OPE data and a unique vacuum. The text currently says
"physical rational full CFT" and later uses a real positive inner product to
exclude odd-order rotations of a one-dimensional primary space. This is
consistent with the book's intended setting, but the hypothesis should not
be left to the word "physical" alone.

Suggested sentence near 442:
"Throughout this test, assume a bosonic unitary rational full CFT with a
unique vacuum, retaining the stated algebra in both chiralities and satisfying
the usual completeness and sewing assumptions."

The full-center/graph-pairing statement at 483–502 is then appropriately
scoped. In particular it uses the *maximal algebras actually present in the
full theory*, not a largest abstract extension one could choose. Its use of
a braided equivalence between the two chiral representation categories is
compatible with the overline on the right-moving modules; if the right
category is already defined with reversed braiding, the same statement would
instead be an equivalence into its reversal. The current convention agrees
with the rest of the book.

1b. l06:538–541: clarify the category containing the symmetry kernel.

"The chiral-trivial defect category is Morita equivalent to the product
representation category" is ambiguous after passing to maximal left/right
chiral algebras. The category of all defects preserving the *original retained*
V is the bimodule category _A Rep(V)_A for the full-CFT algebra A; it is Morita
equivalent to Rep(V). A symmetry fixing the *maximal* left/right algebras
pointwise lies among its invertible objects. The subcategory formed by this
smaller kernel need not itself have the full Morita-equivalent dimension.

Suggested replacement:
"Every symmetry in the remaining kernel is an invertible defect preserving
the original product algebra V. All V-preserving defects form a bimodule
fusion category Morita equivalent to Rep(V), of global dimension ... ."

Then the order-three pointed subcategory sits in that ambient defect category,
and the existing divisibility argument applies verbatim. This repair also
avoids propagating the slightly loose wording in the source note
hi_cft_tricritical_nondiagonal.md:158–175.

1c. l06:669–685: E6 commutant transfer should remain visibly conditional.

The genuine exceptional affine (E6)_4 commutant algebra is established in
the source note. The explicit partial-category conclusion in
hi_cft_e6_extension_status.md:242 onward begins with "Assume the affine
commutant algebra above has been transferred ..." and is a categorical
calculation, not a complete classification/VOA identification. l06:680
correctly says "Under the specified ... transfer", but 673–674's earlier
"supplies an algebra that can be transferred" can read as completion of all
VOA transfer/existence steps.

Suggested wording:
"A genuine exceptional affine (E6)_4 extension supplies a commutant algebra
for a proposed coset extension. Under the specified categorical transfer,
the following partial-category calculation applies."

Or keep the stronger algebraic transfer wording but explicitly say it is
an algebra-object statement and does not claim that the corresponding VOA
or every intermediate extension has been independently constructed here.
Do not place E6 in the completed exclusion inventory.

2. Checks that passed and small teaching repairs
----------------------------------------------

2a. l06:454–468, the two commuting order-three actions.

If the surviving shift S has order three, acts faithfully, and commutes with
the faithful full Haagerup action, then it cannot equal alpha or alpha^2:
rho alpha^k and rho alpha^{-k} are distinct simple lines for k=1,2. The
intersection of the two order-three groups is therefore trivial, so their
commuting product is Z3^2. These premises are essential; neither survival nor
faithfulness follows solely from a finite-size momentum cone. The current
paragraph explicitly keeps these premises and the original/gauged field
dictionary. The mixed-anomaly statement is correctly phrased as a possible
extension law, not a universal result of gauging.

2b. l06:504–526, minimal products at c=21/10.

The final list M4^3 and M3 M5^2 is correct. Add one short omitted case:
"If the smallest label is at least five, the total is at least 12/5."
The remaining cases are already explained correctly. This makes the claimed
complete classification transparently complete for a first-year reader.

2c. l06:528–552, tricritical Ising cubed.

The even-fermion supports have weights three and cannot change weight two.
The weight-two product has three primitive stress idempotents; its permutation
group contributes a Sylow-three subgroup of size at most three, while the
kernel consists of characters of the bosonic support subgroup and is a
two-group. The induced modules with the nontrivial Fibonacci label in one
factor are moved by every nontrivial three-cycle, preventing independent
left/right three-cycles in a graph pairing.

Hand check of the arithmetic step: phi+phi'=1 and phi phi'=-1 give
(phi+2)(phi'+2)=5. Thus the norm of 64(phi+2)^3/3 is exactly
64^2*5^3/9, not an integer. Fusion-subcategory divisibility is contradicted
if an order-three invertible lies in the ambient Morita-equivalent category.
For Z9, the cube of an order-nine symmetry would lie nontrivially in that
forbidden kernel. This justifies excluding either group of order nine.

2d. l06:554–579, Ising times two M5 factors.

The exact extension classification is quoted input, appropriately marked.
Conditional on the two Potts extensions being forced by an order-nine group,
the subsequent uniqueness/neutrality argument is elementary and correct.
Potts-pair residues lie in (1/15)Z/Z. No nonzero difference among
0, 1/2, 1/16 belongs to that subgroup, so T makes the Ising sectors block
diagonal. Commutation with S then equates all three Potts-pair blocks, because
the vacuum row S_{0i} is nonzero. The Ising-spin block therefore contains the
Potts vacuum pair. Nonvacuum Potts chiral weight 1/15 is greater than 1/16,
so no other scalar can have total dimension 1/8. Its Hermitian real line
is fixed by every odd-order symmetry. A finite gap near .26 is correctly
treated as motivation; its limiting value is an extra premise.

An optional worked example could display Z = I_Ising tensor N and the
one-line off-diagonal block equation S_{0i} S_P N_i = N_0 S_P S_{0i}.
Using the diagonal block's commutation with S_P then gives N_i=N_0.

2e. l06:581–620, type-A candidates and W4 sign.

The charge formulas and surviving candidates are correct. The sentence
excluding N>=7 uses monotonicity in k and in N at k=2. Add
"For N=2,3 the charge is at most two" before checking N=4,5,6.
At k=2, c(7,2)=6(1-56/72)=34/15; the relevant values are
c(4,4)=3(1-20/72)=13/6 and c(6,2)=5(1-42/72)=25/12.

The common-field sign argument is correct. Multiplication by five takes
sin^2(4pi/9)/(sin(pi/9)sin(2pi/9)) to a positive square divided by
sin(5pi/9)sin(10pi/9)<0. The common automorphism 113 fixes the numerator
dimension field (113=1 mod16), acts as five on the denominator field
(113=5 mod18), and is coprime to the common conductor. Add the last fact
if presenting it as a complete stand-alone example: gcd(113,288)=1.

The other W4 conclusions rely on the quoted affine extension classification,
faithful coset transfer, and the noncollapsed T,W3 generation at this
particular minimal point. They are not deductions from the sign of one
dimension alone; the surrounding text/inventory treats those classification
steps as input. All added fields indeed have weights >=4 in the audit.

2f. l06:649–660, W6 and E8.

These are compressed summaries of exhaustive local audits, not independently
reproduced proofs. The inventory's constructioninput label and final paragraph
make that distinction. For precision change "the only bosonic extension" at
649 to "the only nontrivial bosonic extension". The vacuum algebra is also
an extension in the usual convention.

The W6 fixed-point resolution/faithful dual Z3 action is precisely what is
needed to prevent an independent right three-cycle. E8's real-generator
argument uses distinct *strong-generator* weights and induction on the
subalgebra generated at lower weights; it does not claim that the full
weight-h subspace is one dimensional. The existing wording preserves this.

2g. l06:690–711, E6 scalar arithmetic checked by hand.

For the partial category, start with affine residues
0, 1/9, 1/5, 11/45, 4/9, 26/45, 3/5, 7/9, 4/5, 41/45.
Adding 0,1/2,5/16 modulo one, taking 0<h<1/2, then doubling gives
  7/45, 13/72, 1/5, 2/9, 9/40, 2/5, 161/360,
  22/45, 5/9, 3/5, 5/8, 37/45, 61/72, 8/9.
For example 26/45+1/2 = 1+7/90 produces 7/45;
7/9+5/16 = 1+13/144 produces 13/72;
41/45+5/16 = 1+161/720 produces 161/360.
The minimum and the open gap (9/40,2/5) are therefore as stated.

For the fully condensed category the possible Fibonacci residues are
0,3/5,1/5,4/5. Adding the Ising residues gives just
1/5,9/40,2/5,3/5,5/8 below total dimension one. The word "contained"
is essential: a residue does not force the actual lowest module weight to
equal its representative, or force a pairing to include that module.
The chapter keeps this distinction and cannot thereby be read as predicting
characters from a braided category.

Useful extra worked check: the topological central charge of the fully
condensed category is 3*(-14/5)+5/2 = -59/10 = 21/10 mod8; that of the
partial category is c((E6)_3)+5/2 = 78/5+5/2 = 181/10 = 21/10 mod8.
Agreement is a necessary consistency check, not an identification of VOAs.
