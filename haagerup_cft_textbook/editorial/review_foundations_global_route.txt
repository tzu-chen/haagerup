Overall result
--------------
The route is conceptually consistent on its major distinctions. I found
no new failure in the reviewed construction proofs. The change to put
complete unitarity before characters is beneficial: positivity now
precedes the orthogonal Virasoro decomposition used for primary counts.
The most useful repairs are a label-transport clarification, an explicit
module/intertwining-field prerequisite, and several short notation resets.
No changes to the physics counts or displayed four-point functions are
recommended by this pass.

PRIORITIZED CONCRETE REPAIRS
===========================

1. Avoid silently identifying the original branching object Y with the
   selected H3 algebra's object a.

   Location: chapters/c05_branching.tex:742--744; compare
   chapters/r04_unitarity.tex:532--539,
   chapters/r05_full_theories.tex:33--39, and
   chapters/r07_defects.tex:365--391.

   c05 presently writes X=b and Y=a for the original H/U extension.
   The later a label is fixed by the chosen center equivalence and
   canonical H3 algebra, whose underlying object is 1+b+2a. The
   text carefully does NOT identify the original H/U algebra with that
   selected algebra. Label transport should respect the same caution.
   The dimension of X makes its b label unambiguous. The two central
   labels a,c0 have the same dimension, twist and scalar character;
   those numerical facts do not identify the original Y with selected a.

   Conservative repair:
     "The unique central object of dimension d^2 is subsequently called
      b, so X=b. The original object Y is one of the two twist-one
      dimension-s labels a,c0 after choosing a center equivalence.
      We retain Y for the original embedding; the label a in the
      selected H3 extension refers to its canonical algebra."
   It is also fine to give a proved simultaneous labeling convention,
   but that requires an explanation. No later scalar character formula
   changes because chi_a=chi_c0. This is a transport ambiguity, not a
   claim that the two canonical algebra objects necessarily differ.

   Source check: hi_cft_c8_manuscript_structure.tex:595--614 retains X,Y;
   its character section:1539 introduces the Evans--Gannon labeling.
   hi_cft_c8_manuscript_analytic.tex:967--975 explicitly declines to
   identify selected A3 with the original embedded H/U algebra.

2. Add the missing bridge from VOA representations to chiral fusion.

   Locations: chapters/r02_vertex_algebras.tex:571--608 currently define
   module grading conditions without defining a module action;
   chapters/r04_unitarity.tex:7,35--41 use intertwining-operator spaces;
   chapters/r06_correlators.tex:344--375 use lattice intertwiners;
   chapters/r08_sewing.tex:16--20 use intertwiner multiplicities.

   A short subsection in r02 (near the lattice-module discussion) would
   make these chapters substantially easier for beginning PhD readers.
   Suggested content, about one page:

   * A V-module M carries Y_M(v,z), v in V, whose modes act on M and
     obey the same vacuum, translation and Jacobi/OPE identities.
     It need not contain a vacuum state or an internal state-field map
     assigning an endomorphism-valued field to every m in M.
   * A module homomorphism f:M->N is a linear map commuting with all
     these V-modes. This is different from an intertwining FIELD of
     type (P; M,N), assigning to u in M a field I(u,z):N->P{z}.
     Its powers can be fractional; for three top states the leading
     power is h_P-h_M-h_N when that channel coupling is nonzero.
   * In the rational tensor category,
       N_{MN}^P = dim I(P;M,N) = dim Hom(M tensor_fusion N,P).
     Fusion is not the ordinary tensor product of the two infinite
     state spaces. This directly connects f01's fusion spaces to r04.
   * Lattice example: the leading exponential action is
       V_alpha(z)|beta> = epsilon(alpha,beta)
                         z^(alpha,beta)|alpha+beta> + ... .
     With alpha,beta in the appropriate dual-lattice cosets this is
     an intertwining field between sectors. The identity
       (alpha+beta)^2/2-alpha^2/2-beta^2/2=(alpha,beta)
     explains its exponent and its charge-addition fusion rule.
   * One sentence on the contragredient M': the graded dual, with its
     dual V-action, realizes the categorical dual; a bar on a full-CFT
     factor denotes the antiholomorphic copy. The all-self-dual examples
     later hide this distinction numerically, not conceptually.

   This need not reproduce the full formal intertwiner Jacobi identity.
   The type of each map and its simplest example are the missing steps.

3. Update the construction roadmap to the actual chapter order.

   Location: chapters/c01_lattice_embedding.tex:419--431.
   The roadmap still places characters before positivity, although
   the master, r00:145--148, and the now-valid r03:233--239 dependency
   put unitarity before character-based primary decomposition.
   Swap the two table rows. At c01:440 "This order" will then refer
   to the actual order again. Character uniqueness itself need not
   depend on complete unitarity; the ordering is pedagogical and also
   supports the subsequent primary-count interpretation.

4. Explicitly reset the meaning of a,b,c after the parafermion proof.

   Location: chapters/c03_factors.tex:231 and :316.
   The proof temporarily abbreviates a3,b4,d5 by a,b,d. Immediately
   after that section, "Set v=a+b+c" returns to the lattice momenta.
   Add "Return now to the lattice momenta a,b,c defining u in
   [eq:c-u]. Set v=a+b+c." This is a one-sentence repair with a
   disproportionately large benefit for a first reading.

5. Define or eliminate the unexplained word "semiconformal."

   Location: chapters/c04_regularity.tex:84--91.
   The positive regular stress family is the central hypothesis of the
   rationality argument, so every condition in its definition should
   be available to the reader. "Semiconformal" has not been defined.

   Either define the standard condition explicitly (the nonnegative
   ambient Virasoro modes restrict on A_i to its intrinsic ones), or
   state directly the actual needed hypotheses used here: the stress
   is PCT-real and ambient quasiprimary of weight two, so its modes
   have the displayed adjoints. The surrounding grading/translation
   requirements are already stated. The choice should match the source
   theorem's hypotheses; no stronger unproved property is needed just
   to explain the adjoint identity.

6. Mark internal Lie-algebra spin where the prose says "right spin."

   Locations: chapters/r06_correlators.tex:172--184 and :358.
   These are internal sl2 labels, not physical left/right chirality or
   conformal spin. Although c01:164--166 and r05:196--198 explain this
   convention correctly, r06's "right spin-two multiplet" is especially
   easy to misread directly after the full-CFT spectrum chapter.
   Suggested wording:
     "The pairs below are dimensions for the two internal current
      algebras, both holomorphic."
     "an internal right sl2 spin-two multiplet (1,5)"
     "the internal right Cartan charges ..."
   Add an H_L,H_R / omega_L,omega_R row to r09 if useful. Current r09
   explains A_L but does not explicitly record the main internal-L/R
   convention.

SECONDARY PRECISION AND NOTATION IMPROVEMENTS
===========================================

7. The lattice L_0 is named exactly like the ubiquitous energy operator.
   c03:324--371 uses L_0 for the rank-two lattice, intentionally avoiding
   the parafermion stress L, but thereby colliding with Virasoro L_0.
   This is locally disambiguated by type, so it is not a mathematical
   error. If undertaking a small notation cleanup, use Lambda_0 for
   the lattice. The rename must also cover c06 and its corresponding
   solutions, not only c03; root is currently splitting solution files.
   A narrower repair is to explicitly point out the collision once.

8. The lattice stress omega_L in c01:90--98 is later the internal affine
   stress omega_L in c03:428ff. The first can be omega_{V_L} or omega_B
   after specialization. K similarly denotes an affine VOA in c01 and
   its six-dimensional weight-one space in c02:115,527. These are
   manageable local redefinitions; a single sentence relating K_1 to
   the current space would suffice. Avoid a large mechanical rewrite.

9. r02:581 uses U direct-sum c1 before the label c1 has been introduced.
   Replace it with a generic example of one integral-weight module and
   one module of lowest weight 1/3, or add a clear forward reference.

10. c03:379,412 identify BRAIDED categories using Fib and SO(3)_13.
    The fusion-ring meaning is already familiar, but the braiding
    choice should be stated once: the neutral Potts Fibonacci object
    has lowest weight 2/5 and twist exp(4 pi i/5); SO(3)_13 is the
    even-label braided subcategory of the standard SU(2)_13 category.
    This makes clear why merely naming a fusion ring would not suffice.
    The even labels are already given at :414--415, so this is short.

11. c03:366 first uses "strongly rational" before its full definition
    in c04:50--51. A forward reference suffices. This is lower priority
    than defining modules/intertwining fields.

POINTS CHECKED AND FOUND CONSISTENT
=================================

* Central charge: c=8 always means one chiral charge, with full
  (c_L,c_R)=(8,8). r01 cylinder energies, r05 E=Delta-2/3, vacuum
  energy -4 pi v/(3L), and the modular fixed-point mean Delta=2/3
  agree. The boundary annulus uses the correct single chiral c/24.

* Weight and dimension: chiral u has h=2; full scalars have h=bar h;
  diagonal relevant scalars are top-top states with Delta=2h_i<2.
  The tables distinguish state counts, Virasoro-primary component
  counts, affine multiplets, and quantum dimensions. d3's actual
  15/13 versus twist representative 2/13 is handled consistently.

* Mode indices: a_(n) changes weight by wt(a)-n-1; L_n=omega_(n+1).
  The strong-generation, partial-stress, annulus Laurent, and OPE
  calculations use this same convention. A module zero-mode action is
  not mistaken for an entire chiral field action in r05.

* Positivity dependency: c04 proves regularity using inherited vacuum
  positivity and a trace argument on arbitrary module tops; it does
  not assume all modules unitary. c05 extracts the ring and twelve
  center objects; r04 completes sector/fusion positivity before the
  final unitary classification. r03 then uses actual positive forms
  for orthogonal descendant subtraction. No circular use of the
  selected character table to prove finite closure was found.

* Original versus selected extension: apart from item 1's potential
  label binding, the distinction H versus H' is maintained. r05 and
  r07 consistently attach the canonical H3 action to H', not to an
  automatically identified original lattice embedding.

* Symmetry scopes: diagonal-U Verlinde lines are the twelve simples
  of Rep(U)=Z(H3). The selected extension's six first-factor lines
  form H3; all rational U-preserving bimodule defects form
  H3 x H3^op and number 36. Local H' modules form Vec. Invariant
  spaces U x bar U and U x bar H' give 36 and 1488 dimension-two
  scalars respectively; unrestricted E8 gives 61504. These are
  consistently separated and derived later, not conflated.

* Opposite versus reverse: r07 reverses tensor ORDER in C^op using
  T_{x,y}; r08 reverses BRAIDING in Rep(U)^rev. Boundary annuli retain
  x* in x* tensor y when the six-object ring is noncommutative and
  alpha is not self-dual. The twelve center labels' self-duality
  justifies dropping conjugations in their Cardy/Verlinde examples.

* Fourier/braiding sign: f03's positive half-braiding, its negative
  Fourier S convention, f04's inverse-double-braiding orientation,
  r02's lattice S and r07's Z3 condensation double braiding agree.
  No sign repair is needed.

* Correlators versus blocks: r06's chiral adjoint does not introduce
  an antiholomorphic field. Its Gaussian u four-point function is an
  actual meromorphic correlator; its light lattice sector functions
  are selected channel combinations. r08 properly refuses to declare
  their individual absolute squares the diagonal bulk correlator.
  The Ising normalization, BPZ exponents, half-normalized epsilon
  block, orthogonal F change, C_sigma_sigma_epsilon=1/2, and
  branch-independent full correlator match each other.

* Marginal deformations: r05 separates charge zero-mode energy from
  oscillators/neutral energy, internal L/R from chirality, dimensional
  marginality from exact marginality, and existence of an ordinary
  conformal family from transport of full Haagerup junction data.

REPETITION AND TEACHING FLOW
===========================

The book is long, but most repeated distinctions now appear at the
moment they prevent a concrete false inference. I would not remove
the local reminders about unitarity, selected H', or nonvacuum sewing.
They serve different steps. The clearest opportunity for optional
trimming is c01's initial lattice-VOA definition/root-current example:
r02 already teaches that machinery in detail. c01 could refer back
and retain the project-specific Gram/cocycle choices and embeddings.
Conversely, do NOT trim the explicit c03 dependency order or c04
noncommuting-stress argument; these fill genuine proof gaps for the
target reader.

An end-of-part summary could be a small object map, not another audit
warning: U (six currents) -> original B/H branching -> abstract center
identification -> selected H' -> its two full-theory choices. Existing
roadmaps largely provide this already. The short label and module
repairs above are higher value than adding another long overview.

No textbook or solution source was edited during this review.
