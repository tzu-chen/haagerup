Verdict: PASS for the stated current-preserving ordinary-orbifold
proposition. The enhanced-parent inventory is correctly marked as an
imported construction input, with a narrower interval and specialized
classification/spectrum lemmas. No substantive correction required.

1. Anomaly and gauging premises (250--255, 284--319).
The proof needs an actual consistent ordinary gauging, not just a finite
group of symmetries or a formal charge-lattice action. This premise is
explicit. Its chosen anomaly trivialization restricts to K, so the
intermediate K gauging is available with the restricted ordinary torsion.
The ineffective case is excluded, which is necessary for distinct shifts,
the perfect finite pairing, and a single toroidal vacuum. The argument
does not silently claim that G/K is geometric after K gauging.

2. Shift lattice (284--319).
The annihilator proof of surjectivity Gamma -> Khat is correct: a proper
image gives a nontrivial element of the finite double dual K acting
trivially on every charge. Each projected sector is a nonempty Gamma_0
coset, and distinct k give distinct rational shift classes. Ordinary
locality and the Gaussian vertex OPE make their union even integral and
additive. Its covolume is [Gamma:Gamma_0]/|K|=1. There is no additional
fixed-point multiplicity in a pure shift sector. Thus the resulting
theory is again Narain. The proof uses the consistent-orbifold premise
essentially at the integrality/locality step.

3. Correlator normalization (321--329).
The K-monodromy subspace embeds into the K-orbifold Hilbert space with
the inherited two-point metric. Remaining G-equivariance is a restriction
on this subspace. Sphere monodromies lie in K, ordinary torsion restricts,
and the change in gauge volume cancels against vacuum normalization.
No extra square-root group-size multiplier is introduced into normalized
cubics. This agrees with the separate affine-kernel source derivation.

4. Rotation classification (331--403).
Both positive determinant signs put the charge action in the identity
component, modeled by (SL2Z x SL2Z)/diagonal center. Finite lifts have
finite order because a power is central in both factors. Enumerating
fractions 0,1/2,+/-1/3,+/-1/4,+/-1/6 and reducing a+b,a-b gives exactly
the table, including (1/12,5/12). The scalar condition requires twice
the larger chiral ground weight, not their sum. All numerical fractions
in the table are exact and correct. The regulator derivation of
h_theta=theta(1-theta)/2 is also correct.

5. Quantum-character selection (405--442).
Characters remain genuine symmetries with ordinary discrete torsion:
e_g -> chi(g)e_g preserves the twisted multiplication. The complete
eigenspace premise puts their action in the harmonic cubic stabilizer
D3, whose abelian subgroups are 1,C2,C3. For image 1 or C2 the chosen
real direction has |CXXX|=|c_phi|/sqrt(2) and is quantum invariant;
characters then force commutator support and both determinant signs +.
For image C3, finite abelian double duality gives order-three classes in
G_ab, killed by each order-two determinant character. The resulting K
support and parent bounds give exactly 2/sqrt(3).

Optional pedagogical clarification at 426: the physical C3 and the
quantum C3 have the same image subgroup in D3 because D3 has a unique
subgroup of order three. Hence their two complex eigenlines coincide;
no assumption that the physical symmetry commutes with every quantum
symmetry is necessary. The current sentence is correct but compressed.

Optional normalization clarification at 445--451: this circle example
uses h=(m/R+wR)^2/4, whereas the CFT primer uses the alpha'=2 convention
h=(m/R+nR/2)^2/2. The radii obey R_here=R_primer/sqrt(2); R -> R/n is
unchanged. Either convert the displayed convention or mention this
rescaling once to help a reader comparing the worked examples.

6. Enhanced-current inventory (481 onward).
The bounded SU2 projection is correctly attached to the sign-fixing
kernel in the latest text. Conjugating Cartans by connected current
groups is an isomorphism of CFT presentations, not a claim that those
connected groups can themselves be gauged anomaly-free. The component
lemmas are clearly imported, and the bound is not extended beyond the
stated narrow interval or to generalized non-group gauging.
