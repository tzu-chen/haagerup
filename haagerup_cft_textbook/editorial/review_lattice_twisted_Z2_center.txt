RESULT

All proposed formulas pass:
  t^2=(-1)^g;
  (g,t)*(h,u)=(g+h,(-1)^(gh)tu);
  theta_(g,t)=c_g(g)=t^g.
With canonical positive pivotal structure, no extra final sign is
needed in theta=c_g(g). The duality/pivotal calculation below is
important because dropping its signs gives the wrong intermediate
formula.

The only essential naming caution is that sbar is the anti-semion
(the conjugate-twist label), NOT the categorical dual of s.
All four simples are self-dual. This agrees with f04's explanation
that categorical duality preserves the twist scalar rather than
complex-conjugating it.

1. HALF-BRAIDING IN THE BOOK'S DIRECTION

Use the associator
  a_(a,b,c): (a tensor b) tensor c -> a tensor (b tensor c)
with scalar omega(a,b,c), and c_g(x):g tensor x -> x tensor g.
To move g through x tensor y, the actual path is

  g(xy) --a^-1_(g,x,y)--> (gx)y
        --c_g(x) tensor 1--> (xg)y
        --a_(x,g,y)--> x(gy)
        --1 tensor c_g(y)--> x(yg)
        --a^-1_(x,y,g)--> (xy)g.

Consequently
  c_g(x+y)
    = omega(x,g,y)/
        [omega(g,x,y) omega(x,y,g)] c_g(x)c_g(y)
    = (-1)^(gxy)c_g(x)c_g(y).

The unit condition is c_g(0)=1. Setting x=y=1 gives
1=(-1)^g t^2, hence t^2=(-1)^g. Thus t=+/-1 for g=0 and
t=+/-i for g=1.

These are all the simples, not just four exhibited objects. For a
graded object with a multiplicity space in degree g, its generator
half-braiding is an operator T satisfying T^2=(-1)^g I. That
polynomial has distinct roots, so the space decomposes into the
two scalar half-braidings. There are two choices in each degree.

2. TENSOR PRODUCT HAS ITS OWN ASSOCIATOR FACTOR

For the product of (g,c) and (h,d), move the pair through x:

  (gh)x --a_(g,h,x)--> g(hx)
        --1 tensor d(x)--> g(xh)
        --a^-1_(g,x,h)--> (gx)h
        --c_g(x) tensor 1--> (xg)h
        --a_(x,g,h)--> x(gh).

The product half-braiding is therefore
  c_(g tensor h)(x)
    = omega(g,h,x) omega(x,g,h)/omega(g,x,h) c_g(x)c_h(x)
    = (-1)^(ghx)c_g(x)c_h(x).
At x=1 this is the claimed (-1)^(gh)tu.
Multiplying the two phases without this factor would incorrectly
turn the center's fusion group into a cyclic group of order four.

3. POSITIVE PIVOTAL STRUCTURE AND TWIST

Here is a concrete gauge compatible with f01's left-dual convention.
Every underlying g is its own dual. Set
  coev_g:1 -> g tensor g to scalar 1,
  ev_g:g tensor g ->1 to scalar (-1)^g.
The zigzags each acquire the associator omega(g,g,g)=(-1)^g,
so they are identities. Evaluation and this particular left
coevaluation are not adjoints for g=1; the relevant opposite
duality maps supply the compatible unitary conjugate solution.
There is no conflict with unitarity.

The canonical positive pivotal in this gauge is j_g=(-1)^g.
Indeed f01's trace convention gives
  d_g=ev_(g*) (j_g tensor 1) coev_g=(-1)^g j_g=1.
The duality tensorator gives the identity double-dual tensorator
on Z2 in this gauge, and j_(g+h)=j_g j_h, so this is monoidal.

For complete transparency, the Drinfeld bend u_g:g -> g** uses
the path
  g --1 tensor coev_(g*)--> g(g* g**)
    --a^-1_(g,g*,g**)--> (g g*)g**
    --c_g(g*) tensor 1--> (g* g)g**
    --ev_g tensor 1--> g**.
Its scalar is
  u_g=1 * (-1)^g * c_g(g) * (-1)^g=c_g(g).
The compatible ribbon twist is theta_g=u_g^-1 j_g, giving
  theta_g=(-1)^g/c_g(g)=c_g(g),
because c_g(g)^2=(-1)^g. For g=0 both sides equal one;
for g=1 this uses t^2=-1.

This convention also reproduces f03's untwisted Z3 result:
there coev=ev=j=1 and u_(g,chi)=chi(-g), so theta=chi(g).
Thus the positive twist orientation used in the proposed example
matches the book's existing half-braiding convention.

4. FUSION, BALANCING, AND COMPARISON

Name the simples
  1=(0,1), b=(0,-1), s=(1,i), sbar=(1,-i).
The product rule gives
  b^2=s^2=sbar^2=1,  s*sbar=b,  b*s=sbar.
Their twists are respectively 1,1,i,-i. All dimensions are one.
This is the double-semion center. The notation sbar indicates
opposite semion twist, not s*. Since s^2=1, s*=s, and likewise
sbar*=sbar.

The braiding from (g,t) to (h,u) is c_g(h)=t^h.
The monodromy is therefore t^h u^g. Balancing agrees:
  theta_[(g,t)*(h,u)]/(theta_(g,t)theta_(h,u))=t^h u^g.
For example the self-monodromy of s is i^2=-1, whereas the
mutual monodromy of s and sbar is i(-i)=1.
The boson b has monodromy -1 with either semion. These facts
also prove that only the unit is transparent. The Gauss sum is
1+1+i-i=2, equal to the total quantum dimension, as for a center.

For the untwisted associator, the same derivation instead gives
t=+/-1 and componentwise fusion (g,t)*(h,u)=(g+h,tu).
Its twists are 1,1,1,-1: the toric-code center. Both centers
have fusion group Z2 x Z2, but their twists differ. Thus the
associator changes the braided center without changing either
the original Z2 fusion ring or, in this example, the center's
abstract fusion ring.
