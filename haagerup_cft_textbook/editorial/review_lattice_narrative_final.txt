RESULT

The narrative passes apart from one useful boundary/size-sequence
clarification below. I found no statement that identifies the physical
chain's continuum, proves its vacuum count, or proves its bulk gap
from the saved finite records. The October 5 conclusions remain
appropriately qualified, and the exact comparison constructions are
kept distinct from the target Hamiltonian.

ONE ACTIONABLE CLARIFICATION

l06_identification.tex, closing paragraph beginning "For a gapped
interpretation with q thermodynamic vacua" (approximately line1564):

Specify periodic sizes compatible with the proposed spatial period
and the chosen boundary/flux sector before imposing the q-state
collapsing cluster and positive E_q-E_0 bound.

Suggested start:
  "For a gapped interpretation with q thermodynamic vacua, take
  periodic sizes compatible with the proposed spatial period and
  boundary sector. The corresponding target is a complete
  collapsing cluster, ..."

Reason: in a translation-broken period-three phase, rings with length
not divisible by three can force a domain wall. Its mobile states can
add low levels whose spacings collapse with circumference even when
the unfrustrated pure phases have a positive bulk gap. Such rings do
not have the same q-vacuum finite-volume pattern. They require their
own domain-wall/sector interpretation. This is particularly relevant
because the final finite variational records mention a period-three
pattern.

The same qualification can be added economically to l02:38-42,
where the first general definition says a gapped chain has a uniform
large-L energy bound above the vacuum sector: "on compatible large
rings" is enough. The later l02 carrier paragraph already correctly
distinguishes lengths divisible by three from other residual
translation boundary conditions.

WHAT NOW WORKS WELL

- l01 begins with an explicitly defined constrained periodic path
  Hilbert space. Its regular space includes compatible flux sectors;
  it is not silently identified with a total-vacuum fusion space.
  Its topological-eigenvalue example explicitly says that vacuum-like
  line eigenvalues do not identify a conformal family. The final
  spectator example shows why symmetry alone does not determine c,
  while expressly saying that adding a spectator changes the model.

- l02 opens by stating that every finite chain has discrete low levels,
  whether critical or massive. It imports r01's cylinder formulas
  with explicit vacuum and common-velocity assumptions. The exact
  Ising example supplies a real calibration without transporting its
  correction exponents to Haagerup. The closing light-shell census
  and singlet estimates do not claim continuum completeness or
  exact marginality.

- l03 opens with a conditional local-field expansion, keeps source
  normalization explicit, and points to r01/r08. The current test
  explains that a small finite-space singular value is not a global
  no-current result, and separates charge conjugation from chirality.
  Its last response test rejects only a specified one-bare-coupling
  finite drift model; it explicitly does not infer two infrared
  couplings or a beta function.

- l04's transfer discussion separates a pure clustering component,
  periodic transfer roots, and a cat. The current stationary sequence
  is qualified by an optimization test, not by a physical error
  estimate. Adjacent entropy slopes are not presented as a plateau.
  Growing transfer length is allowed to reflect an unsaturated but
  finite physical length. The entropy geometry and finite-entanglement
  substitution are stated as distinct steps.

- l05's opening, parent theorem, and following paragraph all distinguish
  a sufficiently long-range exact parent from both the short
  missing-channel anchor and H=-sum P_rho. The rank-four vacuum form
  factors are explicitly conditional on approaching that vacuum
  structure. Selection of the Y_alpha=1,Y_rho=d sector is made
  separately from existence of four ground states. The final
  symmetry-filter argument explains why equal limiting energy
  densities do not prove an additional pure vacuum.

- l06 opens with separate microscopic, continuum-state, local-OPE,
  and categorical-junction requirements. Its ending states directly
  that the physical chain has no identified continuum CFT. Long
  finite-distance correlations are not made into an infinite plateau,
  ground-state vacuum count, or gap. The proposed common field
  dictionary uses the same states, normalizations, velocity, and
  corrections across observables. It separates a strong numerical
  identification from convergence sufficient for a proof.

CROSS-CHAPTER INTERPRETATION

r01's integer-spin statement concerns ordinary bosonic local fields
under the usual discrete-spectrum state-operator assumptions. r07
expressly allows fractional spin in nontrivial defect sectors. l02's
carrier momentum subtraction and l01's finite-sector distinctions
prevent those statements from being confused with ordinary lattice
momentum labels.

r07's twelve Verlinde lines in the diagonal c8 theory and its six
Haagerup lines in the selected extension are distinguished, as are
finite tube factors and infinite-dimensional physical multiplicity
spaces. l01 uses the same vacuum invariance condition Y_a=d_a under
its stated unique-vacuum conformal-limit hypothesis. Neither chapter
identifies those c8 Hilbert spaces with the physical chain simply
because categorical labels recur.

No other repair requested. Further warnings or restatements would
mostly duplicate distinctions the text already makes clearly.
