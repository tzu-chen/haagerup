1. Central-charge convention and the replica Schwarzian

r01:87–90 explicitly uses c for each chiral copy: (c_L,c_R)=(c,c).
l04:379 repeats that convention, and :389 correctly assigns chiral charge
nc to the sum of the n replica stresses. Thus none of the formulas below
use c as c_L+c_R.

For g(w)=(w-u)/(w-v), hand differentiation gives
  g'/g = (u-v)/[(w-u)(w-v)],
  {g,w}=0,
  {g^(1/n),g}=(1-n^(-2))/(2g²).
The stress expectation on one sheet is c{z,w}/12. Summing n sheets gives
  <T_total(w)>
   = [c/24](n-1/n)(u-v)²/[(w-u)²(w-v)²].
The normalized Ward identity for two conjugate chiral primaries gives
the same rational function with coefficient h_n. Hence
  h_n = c(n-1/n)/24,
  hbar_n = c(n-1/n)/24,
  Delta_n = h_n+hbar_n = c(n-1/n)/12.
Equations l04:405–419 are correct. The sheet multiplicity n and the
left-plus-right factor two are both present, exactly once.

2. Interval, circle, and single-cut entropy

A full scalar two-point function scales as ell^(-2Delta_n), not
ell^(-Delta_n) or ell^(-4Delta_n). Therefore
  log Tr rho_A^n = log C_n - [c/6](n-1/n) log(ell/a),
and division by 1-n gives
  S_n = [c/6](1+1/n) log(ell/a)+k_n.
The n->1 limit is c/3, as written at l04:433.

The cylinder Jacobians replace ell by
  [L/pi] sin(pi ell/L),
with the same coefficient and cutoff a. The text specifies the circle
vacuum and additional finite-lattice corrections; it does not apply the
vacuum answer indiscriminately to excited states.

For a clustering massive vacuum with one diverging physical length, a
half-line replica moment is one twist expectation. Its leading scale is
  <T_n> ~ xi^(-Delta_n),
so the regulated moment is A_n(a/xi)^Delta_n. This gives
  S_n^half = [c/12](1+1/n) log(xi/a)+s_n+o(1).
Thus the factor two between l04:428 and :452 is the difference between
two entangling endpoints and one. It is not a second change in c.

The required assumptions are visible: the full vacuum and cutoff at
:378–384; physical continuation of the positive regulated density matrix
at :431–434; clustering massive vacuum and one large length at :444–447.
Most importantly, :456–458 treats replacement of physical xi by a
finite-bond MPS transfer length as an additional hypothesis. It does not
derive finite-entanglement scaling solely from the replica calculation.
The subsequent component/entropy conventions must still be respected.

The statement about analytic continuation is sound: integer replica
values alone are not being declared an unrestricted uniqueness theorem.
The actual positive-density-matrix powers supply the intended physical
continuation. The S_infinity example additionally states the regime in
which the largest Schmidt probability obeys the scaling law.

4. Cat entropy and the qutrit counterexample

l04:354 now explicitly requires mutually orthogonal Schmidt supports
on BOTH sides of the cut. This is sufficient for the stated formulas:
write |Psi>=sum_j sqrt(w_j)e^(i theta_j)|psi_j>. Orthogonality on the
traced-out side kills all cross terms. Orthogonality on the retained
side makes rho_A the direct sum of w_j rho_j. Its eigenvalues are
w_j p_(j,a), so
  S1 = -sum_j w_j log w_j + sum_j w_j S1(rho_j),
  Sn = log[sum_j w_j^n Tr(rho_j^n)]/(1-n).
The text correctly reserves the Shannon-plus-weighted-average form for
von Neumann entropy and gives the different general Renyi formula.

For three equally weighted components with identical probabilities p_a,
the full spectrum consists of p_a/3 repeated three times. Consequently
Tr rho_cat^n=3^(1-n) sum_a p_a^n and Sn_cat=Sn_component+log3, including
the largest-probability limit. A fixed additive term leaves a limiting
logarithmic slope unchanged, whereas changing weights can affect finite
secants. The qualifications at :473–479 are sufficient.

The qutrit example is exact:
  <Phi_j|Phi_k>=(1/3)sum_a zeta_3^[(k-j)a]=delta_jk;
  (1/sqrt3)sum_j |Phi_j>
    =(1/3)sum_a(sum_j zeta_3^(ja))|a,a>=|0,0>.
Each component is maximally entangled, but their Schmidt supports overlap.
This directly demonstrates the distinction between global orthogonality
and the support hypothesis. It is a useful counterexample and contains
no missing normalization factor.

5. Vacuum pants exponent and the branch-geometry bridge

The pants map at l03:539–540 has
  z'(w)=[L/(2pi)](w-1+x)/[w(w-1)].
At its joining point w*=1-x, z'=0 and
  z''(w*)=-L/[2pi x(1-x)].
Therefore, locally,
  z-z*=-L(w-w*)²/[4pi x(1-x)]+higher powers.
This is a twofold branch: one full circuit in the regular coordinate
w covers angle4pi in z. The n=2 replica weights above are
  h2=hbar2=c/16, Delta2=c/8.

For the specified normalized periodic three-cylinder sewing, there is
one such joining singularity in the amplitude. The geometric input is
therefore A000~C(x)L^(-Delta2)=C(x)L^(-c/8). Squaring the amplitude
gives exponentc/4 and hence
  -log|A000|²=(c/4)log L-2log|C(x)|+...
exactly as in l03:598–611. One must not square the twist dimension before
squaring the overlap, or treat the amplitude as a two-twist interval
correlator. The book does neither.

The current wording also correctly distinguishes the local twist-weight
calculation from its application to normalized pants: :607–608 labels
that application as the geometric input, rather than pretending the
interval Renyi moment is itself the pants amplitude.

The bridge retains the essential scope:
- periodic pants geometry, not an open-chain cut;
- normalizable identity vacuum;
- fixed nonzero split fraction0<x<1;
- unit-normalized external cylinder vacua;
- microscopic seams approaching the specified sewing;
- no additional leading junction field.
The vacuum normalization cancels extensive cylinder propagation factors;
the surviving scale anomaly belongs to the joining geometry. Identity
sewing with a nonzero leading coefficient is implicit in the displayed
asymptotic. A different junction field or a nonidentity lowest state
would require a different analysis.

For identical shape and seam at L,L', C(x) cancels in the magnitude
ratio. The prefactor-8 in l03:618 is consequently correct. The text
retains finite-size/conical/bulk/logarithmic corrections and does not
promote this two-size diagnostic to a determination of the physical
chain's exact central charge.

Adjacent check: dividing the outgoing Schwarzian contribution
[-c/4]B(x) by the chiral stress-state norm sqrt(c/2) gives
-B(x)sqrt(c/8), matching l03:654–657. This c/8 under a square root is
compatible with, but conceptually distinct from, the twist Delta2=c/8.

product states". Its intended GHZ branches are clear, but the later
claim at :470–472 applies across any nontrivial cut. As the chapter
itself emphasizes, global orthogonality alone does not imply both-side

If desired, make the elementary example completely literal by replacing
its first sentence with:
  Let |o_j>=|j>^{tensor N}, j=0,1,2, where the one-site |j> are
  orthonormal and alpha cycles them.
Keep the following order-parameter sentence unchanged.
Then the product-cat entropy and its exercise solution follow without
any implicit homogeneity assumption. This is a premise clarification,
not a correction to the intended example or any entropy exponent.
