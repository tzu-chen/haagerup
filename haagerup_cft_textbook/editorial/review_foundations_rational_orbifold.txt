Primary theorem check
---------------------
Dong--Ren--Xu, On Orbifold Theory, arXiv:1507.03306v2,
https://arxiv.org/html/1507.03306, Section 2 assumptions (V1)--(V4),
Theorem 4.4, and Lemma 4.7: the draft supplies their simple CFT-type
parent, finite automorphism group, rational/C2-cofinite fixed algebra,
and strictly positive nonvacuum twisted lowest weights. The parent and
fixed algebra are CFT-type here because V_L is and the group fixes its
vacuum. The fixed-algebra assumptions imply the required twisted
rationality. The theorem identifies the vacuum S-entry scaling, hence
the global-dimension factor |G|^2; positivity gives the positive dimension
function, equal to FPdim. Thus the notation FPdim in the draft is justified,
not an unproved substitution of categorical for Frobenius--Perron dimension.

Etingof--Nikshych--Ostrik, On Fusion Categories, arXiv:math/0203060v11,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0203060, Corollary 8.14 and Proposition 8.27:
an indecomposable module category's dual has the same global FP dimension,
and integer global FP dimension forces each simple's FP dimension to be
the square root of an integer. These are precisely the two implications
used here. Weak integrality does not assert integer dimensions for all
objects and is not being conflated with integrality.

Lattice and holomorphic specialization
-------------------------------------
The lattice calculation is immediate from the already established module
labels L*/L: every label is invertible, so FPdim Rep(V_L)=|L*/L|.
Consequently FPdim D=|G|^2|L*/L| is an integer for every admitted sewing
algebra A, not only for A=1. Morita invariance concerns the defect category
and does not require that the chiral orbifold category remain pointed.

For unimodular L this is |G|^2. More generally the same theorem applied
to a holomorphic parent gives |G|^2, provided the same fixed-algebra and
twisted-positivity premises are retained. An optional one-sentence remark
can make this case explicit. No untwisted group-double identification is
needed: a group associator anomaly does not change this global-dimension
argument. The worked untwisted S3-center example is correctly labeled as
an example, rather than asserted as every holomorphic orbifold's category.

Simple-image issue
------------------
The displayed d^2=(11+3 sqrt(13))/2 excludes a SIMPLE image of rho.
The draft correctly acknowledges that arbitrary sums can have irrational
squared dimension and cites Proposition c-faithful-descent to close this
gap for a unitary strong tensor action. I rechecked that proof by hand:

* The finitely many simple summands of the six images generate a fusion
  category because the source fusion and duality rules close that list.
* For the induced dominant functor, the adjoint unit algebra has a central
  lift containing the central unit once. If it is nontrivial, the smallest
  nonunit central dimension 3d forces FPdim A >= 1+3d=d^2.
* The target global dimension is at most 3+3/d^2, below the Fibonacci
  minimum for any nonpointed fusion category. A noninvertible simple has
  squared dimension at least two, so ranks at least three have dimension
  at least four; the rank-two minimum is the Fibonacci category.
* The resulting pointed target would give every object integer dimension,
  contradicting preservation of the positive dimension d of rho.

Thus the functor is fully faithful, rho remains simple, and ENO's
simple-square obstruction applies. The proof is not merely a claim that
all tensor functors preserve simplicity. Full faithfulness is special to
this Haagerup source and its center dimensions.

The final preservation-scope paragraph is correct: all A-bimodule sewing
choices are covered, but Virasoro-only defects or an unspecified asymmetric
chiral framework are not placed in this finite category by the argument.
