Needed concise clarification
---------------------------
l06_identification.tex:794--802. Explicitly require the internal part S
of translation to have order three. Faithfulness and commuting with the
Haagerup action do not themselves imply that order. Suggested opening:
"Suppose the ordinary internal part S of translation has order three,
survives faithfully, and commutes with the full Haagerup action."
The displayed alpha/rho argument then correctly proves that S is not
alpha or alpha^2, and commuting independent order-three generators give
Z3^2. This is a continuum symmetry premise, not a consequence of finite
translation alone. The subsequent original-versus-gauged dictionary and
possible Z9 qualification are appropriate.

Confirmed live scope and arguments
---------------------------------
1. Finite Narain orbifolds: l06:295--332, 334 onward, 500--565.
The kernel orbifold is another Narain theory under the stated consistent,
effective gauging assumptions. The charge-character surjectivity and
covolume proof do not assume a geometric shift. Normalized K-supported
correlators enter by an isometric subgroup lift; no extra square-root of
the group order belongs in the cubic. Current-preserving and enhanced
finite-group cases remain distinct; the latter correctly imports its
bounded-order spectral lemmas. The finite-size numerical values and
full-shell isolation are not promoted to continuum proofs. No claim to
classify all compact c=2 CFTs or generalized categorical orbifolds is made.

2. Canonical Cardy test: l06:727--769.
The maximal chiral algebras are recovered using strictly positive
nonvacuum lowest weights, so arbitrary internal symmetries preserve them
setwise. The scalar-ground multiplicity n_i^2 and the real-doublet
argument are correct under the isolated full-shell hypothesis. The
spatial-parity conclusion is restricted to the canonical identity-algebra
sewing and the stated mixed data. It does not follow just from a torus
partition function, and the text explicitly avoids that inference.

3. Rational extension/internal-symmetry strategy: l06:782--842.
Unique vacuum, unitarity, rationality, completeness and physical sewing
are on the page. Passing to actual maximal chiral algebras and their
graph full-center algebra justifies the multiplicity-one pairing and
all nonzero fusion-channel phase equations. A formal matrix commuting
with S and T alone would not suffice; the text says so. Total positivity
of extension constituents is used only as a necessary filter, not an
existence criterion.

4. Minimal products: l06:844--931.
The hand classification at 21/10 gives exactly M4^3 and M3 M5^2.
The tricritical symmetry-kernel norm is 64^2*5^3/9, correctly nonintegral.
It is used on the original-algebra-preserving kernel, not on every
symmetry indiscriminately. For the Potts-pair case, T separates Ising
blocks because Potts residues have denominator fifteen; the nonzero
Ising S vacuum row forces all blocks equal. The Ising spin scalar at
1/8 is unique since nonvacuum Potts h>=1/15>1/16. An odd-order internal
symmetry fixes its one-dimensional real space. The contradiction still
requires a true limiting jointly neutral scalar gap above 1/8.

5. Principal W candidates: l06:933--1015.
The c(N,k) interval reduction correctly leaves W4(8,9) and W6(8,9).
The W4 sine-sign example and 113 modulo 16 and 18 are consistent; the
common-field automorphism is legitimate. The extension analysis includes
the exceptional SO(15)_1 branch, so it is not only a simple-current check.
W6 has a possible single order-three symmetry; the stated conclusion is
absence of an order-nine subgroup, not absence of every three-cycle.
The finite label tables/classifications are explicitly construction
inputs. Their full numerical enumerations were not repeated here.

These symmetry restrictions concern compulsory invertible internal
symmetries and the maximal chiral algebra. They do not separately assume
the noninvertible Haagerup line preserves the originally retained W
algebra. This differs from the dimension-field tests below, for which
preservation by the proposed noninvertible defect is essential.

6. Exceptional E6: l06:1017--1069.
The partial and fully condensed categorical branches stay conditional on
their specified algebra transfer; they are not asserted to exhaust all
intermediate VOAs. The Ising_5/2 notation identifies modular data, not a
c=1/2 VOA tensor factor. The twist arithmetic in the fully condensed
branch gives exactly the possible scalar residues listed. A pairing can
omit entries but cannot add an absent residue. No general exclusion of
every c=21/10 theory is asserted.

7. Pointed lattices, ramification and ordinary W3: c06:364--369,
474--494, 890--925.
The near-two M(41,42) times norm-twenty lattice algebra has dimension
field ramified only at 2,3,7,41: the lattice simple dimensions are all
one, so its charge discriminant does not add prime five to the dimension
field. A-bimodule dimensions are ratios of dimensions of underlying
objects and therefore remain in the same field. This excludes sqrt(13)
only for defects preserving that rational algebra, as stated.
The irrational factor's low-grade result excludes an additional ordinary
spin-three generator; it neither proves Virasoro generation at every
weight nor excludes every stress-preserving CFT at that central charge.
The weak-integral lattice argument now properly invokes the proved
Haagerup full-faithfulness result so that rho remains simple.

Useful omissions, not live proof gaps
------------------------------------
A. Rational finite-orbifold weak integrality is a genuinely broader test
than the pointed-lattice observation: twisted sectors need not be
pointed, yet their global dimension is still integral. It needs no
extrapolated gap or OPE datum. The draft
  editorial/rational_orbifold_dimension_insertion.tex.txt
states fixed-algebra rationality/C2, positive twisted lowest weights,
physical rational sewing, and preservation of that fixed algebra.
It proves the obstruction from Morita invariance and the theorem that a
simple object in a weakly integral fusion category has integer squared
dimension, explains the reducible-image issue using c06's proposition,
and works Z(Vec S3) by hand. Foundations independently checked the draft
and reported that its premises, simple-unit issue, arithmetic and scope
all pass. Suggested placement: after Cardy, before the nearby-rational
inventory. It should not replace the different stress-only orbifold
spectral tests.

B. The complete ordinary unitary W3(p,p+1) obstruction is absent from the
live chapter. A compact treatment is useful: this is a canonical family
approaching c=2, and its conductor filter illustrates why matching c is
weak. A full reproduction would be disproportionate here. The optional
draft editorial/w3_dimension_scope_insertion.tex.txt gives the hand field
test and states the all-height completion as a construction input, with
its actual categorical and finite exceptional dependencies visible.
The exact scope is every rational full-CFT sewing algebra over the
specified W3 minimal category, for W3-preserving defects. It says nothing
about Virasoro-only defects of those theories or all theories at the
same central charge. The first conductor-compatible height is p=12,
c=24/13; the divisibility filter alone leaves infinitely many heights.

The saved source proves the completed result by actual coset-category
factorization, factorization of Lagrangian full centers AS ALGEBRAS,
affine SU3 module-category classification, dimension gaps and a saved
exact 66-label height-13 comparison at the exceptional pair (12,13).
This is materially stronger than factorization/classification of modular
invariant matrices. The primary generic classification needs Remark 1.2
to extend Theorem 1.1's displayed N,k>=4 statement to SU3; the source
already cites it. The only conductor-compatible exceptional affine
height is twelve. I read this argument and its stated exact inputs but
did not independently rerun the finite certificate.
