Exact arithmetic and denominators
--------------------------------
The ordered basis is (1,delta,p,delta*p). Writing elements A+Bp with
A,B in Z[delta]/(delta^2-3delta-1), multiplication is
  (A+Bp)(C+Dp) = AC-delta*BD + (AD+BC+BD)p.
Lines 26-30 and 66-75 implement these two reductions exactly. Both relations
are monic, so the displayed four normal-form coefficients give a free
rank-four Z-module, regardless of whether any field interpretation is used.
Every operation preserves integer coefficients, and zero equality compares
all four coefficients. Python's initial integer zero in sum() is handled by
__radd__; no division or floating arithmetic enters the identities.

The B matrix at 92-95 is exactly f05 equation eq:HI-n3-matrix, with q=1-p.
For tau=delta-1, hand reduction gives
  delta*(delta-3)=1,
  tau^2=delta+2,

  tau^3=4*delta-1,
  tau^3*(delta-3)=7-delta,
  tau*(delta-2)=3.
The last identity proves tau invertible after passing to Q, which justifies
recovering A=B/tau in every complex embedding. It is not asserted that tau
is a unit in the integral coefficient algebra itself.
