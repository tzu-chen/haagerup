2. Targeted pedagogy improvements
--------------------------------

2a. c01_lattice_embedding.tex:190--191, then the transported-form section.
The inference from the displayed noncompact coordinate triple to the
simple positive-level affine quotient is valid, but the appropriate
positive form is explained later. Add a forward sentence: "The transported
positive form constructed below makes this coordinate realization compact;
equivalently one may first conjugate to that compact gauge." This helps a
reader avoid assuming that the raw right triple is already compact for
the untransported lattice PCT. No change to the embedding is needed.

2b. c04_regularity.tex:73--82 and 117--118.
The proof uses self-adjoint partial energies for the inherited positive
form. The actual affine/Potts/parafermion subalgebras have this property.
For a theorem meant to be read as a reusable abstract package, explicitly
say that the subalgebras carry their inherited unitary structures and that
their conformal vectors are PCT real/semiconformal for the ambient form,
or recall the unitary-subalgebra fact that supplies this from the chosen
embeddings. The present reader otherwise sees "PCT-stable" and "grading
compatible" followed by self-adjointness without an explanation of the
stress adjoint. This is an exposition precision request, not a claim of
a counterexample to the actual construction.

2c. c06_cosets.tex:433--438.
Give a short proof, or a cross-reference to a proof, for the countable
ordinary semisimple branching statement. It is quite important that this
is available even though neither irrational factor is rational. The useful
mechanism is positivity, commuting energies, orthogonal decomposition,
and finite ambient graded pieces; fixing an opposite ground weight makes
each multiplicity-module grade sit inside one finite ambient grade.
Immediately retain the existing warning that none of this bounds the
number of reduced branches in an energy interval. The two facts should
not be mistaken for each other.
Source: hi_cft_20260925_ident2_construction.md, section 3 and its end just
before section 4. This is already proved in the completed source notes.

2d. c06_cosets.tex:483--486.
Name the multivariable modular theorem (Krauel--Miyamoto) in this one
location and give its actual application hypotheses in a sentence:
orthogonal conformal vectors sum to the regular ambient stress, and the
vacuum two-variable trace is normally convergent. Neither reduced factor
needs rationality. The present statement is accurate; the extra sentence
would forestall the tempting but false objection that irrational factors
invalidate ambient modularity. The next sentence correctly rejects
separate modular invariance and should remain.
Source: hi_cft_20260925_axis2_sewing_trace.md, "Ambient two-temperature
modular identity".

2e. c06_cosets.tex:563--569.
The positive-weight irreducible Virasoro character formula holds for
c>1, including 1<c<25, but a beginner may expect exceptional Verma nulls.
A brief parenthesis explaining the absence of positive real degenerate
weights in this unitary range would be helpful; the vacuum is the lone
exception used below. For 1<c<25 put c=1+6Q^2 and Q=b+b^{-1}, |b|=1.
The Kac weights [Q^2-(rb+s/b)^2]/4 are nonreal for r!=s and nonpositive
for r=s. This is in the source note; no new hypothesis is needed.

2f. c06_cosets.tex:149--161 and 191--197.
The c6 transport and c2 lattice obstruction are correctly scoped but
would benefit from one sentence naming the preserved chiral algebra.
The c6 noninvertible defects preserve C6; they are not noninvertible
simple modules of V_N, whose representation category is pointed. The
reverse c2 no-go concerns V_L0-preserving defects. This prevents readers
from perceiving a contradiction between the two paragraphs. The chapter
already supplies enough information to deduce this, but making it explicit
is worthwhile for the intended audience.

c06: The perpendicular lattice glue has norm4, order6, determinant39.
The lattice double commutant follows from positive perpendicular energy
