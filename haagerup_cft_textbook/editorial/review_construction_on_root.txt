This review checked the claims against their mathematical premises and worked
the small examples by hand. No numerical jobs were launched. The examples

premises in the analytic chapter, rather than arithmetic errors.

1. HIGH PRIORITY: establish finite index of the actual smeared-field inclusion

Location: chapters/r04_unitarity.tex:173, before the introduction of its
extension algebra and the boundary fusion category.

The preceding paragraph transports complete rationality from the
bounded-insertion construction only as an isomorphism-invariant property.
That does not itself identify the actual inclusion N_U(I) subset N_H(I) as
a finite-index irreducible inclusion. Those are premises for the canonical
algebra, its all-module fusion category, and the Lagrangian-center argument.
The text currently jumps from actual branching to these objects.

Repair: insert the local irreducibility argument, then the finite-index
extension theorem. If x lies in N_H(I) intersect N_U(I)', locality makes it
commute with N_U(I') too. Strong additivity implies that N_U(I) and N_U(I')
generate the global represented subnet (remove the two endpoints from test
intervals). Thus x commutes with its local diffeomorphisms and the common
rotation generator. The zero-energy space of H is one dimensional, so
x Omega is scalar Omega; the ambient separating-vacuum property then gives
x scalar. Complete rationality gives finitely many finite-dimensional
sectors; the irreducible local-extension finite-index theorem now applies.
Its dual canonical object is the already established restriction
1 + X + 2Y. Holomorphicity of H makes that algebra Lagrangian.

This is present, with the precise Kawahigashi--Longo proposition stated, in
hi_cft_c8_manuscript_analytic.tex:111-129. It should be reproduced as a short
argument here because it closes the apparent circularity in the textbook.

2. MEDIUM PRIORITY: make the net-side no-sign step explicit

Location: chapters/r04_unitarity.tex:179-185.

"The same boundary-category argument used algebraically can now be applied"
compresses a nontrivial passage: the net boundary category must not become
the pointed rank-six alternative. The algebraic no-sign result is useful
on this side only after relating a hypothetical net automorphism to the
actual lattice VOA automorphism.

Repair: explain the short bridge. A pointed rank-six boundary category,
together with [H:B]=3, would make [B:U]=2. Such a local index-two extension
has a sign involution. The unitary VOA/net automorphism correspondence
lifts it to B, and fixed-point compatibility plus injectivity of the
subalgebra/subnet correspondence identifies its fixed VOA with U. This
contradicts the explicit no-sign calculation of the branching chapter.
Then the three invertibles, untwisted pointed subcategory, and indicator
argument apply exactly as stated.

The original argument appears in
hi_cft_c8_manuscript_analytic.tex:146-164. A concise paragraph is enough;
there is no need to repeat the full rank-six fusion-ring derivation.

3. MEDIUM PRIORITY: state the geometric scope of annulus continuation

Location: chapters/r04_unitarity.tex:308-316 and 347-353.

The finite-Laurent observation and the inverse-energy-domain lemma are
correct. Analytic continuation to the entire prescribed insertion interior
also needs a geometric coverage statement. Agreement on two open subsets
alone does not imply agreement on a disconnected domain whose other
components do not meet either subset.

Repair: name the test domains
 D_A = {xi : A_M xi is finite-energy},
 D_{B*} = {eta : B_M* eta is finite-energy}.
For one geometric representative define
 Omega = int(closure(O_in) union closure(O_out)).
Every component of Omega meets O_in union O_out, since the omitted set
lies in two nowhere-dense boundaries. The identity theorem therefore
extends each finite-Laurent matrix coefficient throughout Omega. Repeat
for every representative; the prescribed insertion interior is the union
of their interiors. This also clarifies that the argument compares bounded
extensions of forms, not graph closures of a point field on arbitrary
vectors.

At the concrete-net conclusion, it would help to display the one-line
inclusion proof on the common vacuum Hilbert space:
 A_bl(I) subset N_U(I) subset A_bl(I')' = A_bl(I).
The first inclusion was just proved; the second uses locality and the
first inclusion at I'; the last uses Haag duality of A_bl with cyclic
vacuum and its conformal covariance.

The full source mechanism is in
hi_cft_c8_manuscript_analytic.tex:470-521 and 630-651.

4. MEDIUM PRIORITY: ordinary-module definition excludes ordinary direct sums

Location: chapters/r02_vertex_algebras.tex:424-426.

The phrase "a spectrum bounded below in a translate of the nonnegative
integers" is appropriate for a simple ordinary module, but not for an
arbitrary ordinary module. For example U direct sum c_1 is an ordinary
module with integer and 1/3-plus-integer energies. No single translate
contains both. The later linear comparison explicitly uses finite direct
sums, so the narrow definition creates a small inconsistency.

Repair: define an ordinary module as L_0-semisimple with finite-dimensional
weight spaces and the standard lower-truncation condition in every
integer-shift class. Then state that each simple ordinary module in the
present positive-energy setting has spectrum h + Z_{\geq 0}; finite direct
sums can involve several such translates. A simpler introductory variant
is to label the displayed definition "a simple ordinary module" and give
the finite-direct-sum convention immediately afterward.

5. LOW/MEDIUM PRIORITY: replace "conformal inclusion hypotheses"

Location: chapters/r02_vertex_algebras.tex:359-363.

The Sugawara subtraction statement is correct for the embeddings used
here, but "conformal inclusion" normally means equality of conformal
vectors. That convention would make the following nonzero complement
confusing.

Repair: say that the affine currents are ambient weight-one primaries,
the level is positive integral, and the embedding is compatible with the
ambient unitary grading; equivalently state the semiconformal/coset
embedding hypotheses. Under these hypotheses the Sugawara stress and
omega_V - omega_A are commuting stresses. This also gives the beginning
reader an actual condition rather than the undefined word "standard."

6. PEDAGOGICAL DEPENDENCY: the primary-count formula uses Virasoro decomposition

Location: chapters/r03_characters.tex:229-243.

The positive-weight Kac-determinant calculation is correct. To count
actual primary components by multiplying by the Euler product, one also
uses decomposition of the module into Virasoro highest-weight summands.
This is immediate from the positive invariant form and finite-dimensional
energy spaces, but the positivity of all nonvacuum U-modules is proved
only in the next chapter.

Repair: add one sentence saying that, using the strong-unitarity
conclusion proved in Chapter ch:unitarity, the module decomposes
orthogonally by successively taking the complement of descendants in
each finite energy space. The irreducible-Verma calculation then gives
the formulas. The vacuum example is already justified by lattice
positivity. This is a dependency clarification, not a numerical error.

7. OPTIONAL WORKED STEP: derive the PF13 weight-four norm subtraction

Location: chapters/r06_correlators.tex:283-299.

The displayed gamma_4 formula and both specialized values are correct.
This is an especially useful calculation to make reproducible in a
textbook, since it distinguishes a null channel from a positive one.
With Q=5c_0+22, Virasoro Gram products give
 norm(partial^2 T_0)^2 = 20 c_0,
 <Lambda, partial^2 T_0> = 0,
 norm(Lambda)^2 = c_0 Q / 10.
Therefore
 norm(W_4)^2
   = B - 9c_0/5 - 512c_0/(5Q).
Dividing by N^2=c_0^2/9 simplifies to the stated
 48(c_0+2)(8-lambda Q)/(c_0 Q).
At c_0=8/5 and lambda=14/473 one has
 Q=30, 8-lambda Q=3364/473, and the prefactor is 18/5,
 giving 60552/2365. At the Potts point Q=26 and lambda Q=8.
These few lines would turn a correct asserted normalization into a
canonical hand-worked example.

Location: chapters/r04_unitarity.tex:383.
"Rank-eight holomorphic VOA classification" should be "central-charge-eight
holomorphic VOA classification." Rank can mean lattice rank, current-algebra
rank, or number of simple modules, so it is ambiguous in this context.
