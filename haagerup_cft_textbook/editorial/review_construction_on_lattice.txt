Conclusion
----------
The finite-size dictionaries, overlap normalizations, transfer formulas,
and distinctions among an exact MPS, a short frustration-free anchor, and
a longer gapped parent are sound under the stated assumptions. The new
solutions checked below agree with the chapter conventions. In particular,
I found no unwarranted claim that the short missing-channel Hamiltonian
is gapped or has exactly four ground states. The standard long-parent
conclusion depends on the actual module relations, eventual block
injectivity, the local open-line intertwining statement, and the usual
one-dimensional parent theorem, all of which the chapter distinguishes.

Repairs and precision suggestions
--------------------------------
1. l03_overlaps.tex:647--655. REPAIRED during this review by the author.
The previous unconditional 1/16 lower bound followed a generic x,y split
formula. It required equal asymptotic halves and matching vacuum shape.
The current general RHS G(x;1) exp[-c(f(x)-f(y))/2], followed by its
equal-halves specialization, is correct. No further action needed.

2. l03_overlaps.tex:465--470. Small missing premise. State that the
identical normalized scalar is Hermitian/self-conjugate and has a nonzero
two-point normalization, or specify a compatible conjugate-label version.
A charged order-three field can have C_XXX nonzero but <XX>=0. In that
case all three identical-label pair cumulants vanish, so the printed
quotient is undefined. The intended real neutral scalar obeys the needed
premise. Suggested first words: "For identical normalized Hermitian
scalar primaries in an ideal pants geometry...".

3. l04_tensor_networks.tex:129--146. Clarify which infinite-chain state
is meant. A simple positive leading eigenvalue does not alone exclude
other peripheral roots, so it does not ensure convergence of arbitrary
periodic-ring states to a unique clustering limit. The displayed formulas
are valid for the stationary state defined by the selected compatible
fixed metrics. To mean the unique clustering periodic-chain limit, assume
the primitive/injective component described immediately below. A short
sentence distinguishing these interpretations suffices; the later
primitivity, cat, and reduced-resolvent discussion is already correct.

4. l_solutions.tex:185--189. The cat entropy log 3 and nondecaying order
correlator are correct, but "the state has a finite-dimensional vacuum
degeneracy" attributes a Hamiltonian property to a state by itself.
Suggested replacement: "The state has three ordered product components
and a nondecaying order correlator; its standard symmetry-breaking parent
has a finite ground-state degeneracy." The final conclusion about absence
of logarithmic interval growth remains unchanged.

5. l04_tensor_networks.tex:428--431 and the associated solution. Optional
precision: say t is real. Otherwise the norm transfer uses |t|^2, rather
than t^2, and t=-1 versus general phases need a separate interpretation.
All displayed real-family special cases are correct.

Independent hand checks: MPS and exact gapped states
---------------------------------------------------
* The block-row tensor realizes the cyclic ordinary fiber trace even
  for rectangular edge maps. Its inserted transfer has the printed
  order A^t X A^{s dagger}; sigma=sqrt(l) r sqrt(l) has the
  normalized Schmidt spectrum. For real scalar t, the norm transfer
  is (t^2-1)I+J, with eigenvalues t^2+2,t^2-1,t^2-1.
* The entropy slopes at n=1,2,infinity are c/6,c/8,c/12. The
  finite-entanglement secant for a x+b+u/x is a-u/(x_a x_b).
  The centered resolvent and its failure on residual peripheral
  directions are stated correctly. For h=-P, h^2=-h gives the
  coincident variance -e-e^2, consistent with the sign convention.
* The Q-block norm identity uses zeta^2-v^2=1 and
  w^2=d-1-v^2 to give 1+2d. The free-module decomposition
  rho Q = Q plus alpha^2 Q plus M_f has the correct multiplicities.
  The all-length module trace is an ordinary trace; no extra
  quantum-dimension factor belongs in that chosen closure.
* The scalar fixed-module t_0 satisfies t_0^2+t_0=1/d.
  Its norm transfer is primitive. The variational energy formula
  follows by contracting three equal-color and six unequal-color
  cases against the asymptotic J/3 environment.
* In the ordered blocks det W=-u zeta is nonzero. In the fixed
  block det W=B^3(t_0+2)(t_0-1)^2 is nonzero. The color-isolating
  words and invertible endpoint rows give full Mat_3 spans.
  The complete local range, not merely four periodic vectors, is
  used for the open-line symmetry argument. Adjoints make the
  local range reducing and allow its orthogonal projector.
* The three-height missing-channel kernel counts are 3+6+6+9+12=36.
  At four heights dim H_open=168 and each of the two missing-channel
  projectors has rank51, so their common kernel has dimension at
  least66>36. This correctly blocks the simplest claim that the
  short terms already equal the longer MPS parent constraints.
* The four-vacuum rho matrix splits into [[1,sqrt3],[sqrt3,2]]
  on neutral states and -P on the charged plane. With
  b=(sqrt13-1)/2, b^2+b=3 and PF amplitudes b:1:1:1.
  Ordered probability p=3/(b^2+3), cubics p^-1/2,b/sqrt3,
  and -1/sqrt3, and the pointwise multiplication identities all pass.
  These are finite vacuum-algebra form factors, not automatically
  CFT structure constants; the text explicitly respects that scope.
* Var(Y_rho)<=13/4 and the covariance estimate yield an energy
  density difference O(L^-1/2) if Var(H_L)=O(L). A cat whose
  components have different energy densities instead has the
  displayed L^2 contribution. The distinction is necessary and
  is present.
