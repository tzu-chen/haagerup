RESULT

The existence statements should be retained. There is an actual unitary local
two-dimensional conformal net here, conditional only on the construction
inputs already stated, not merely a modular-invariant candidate. The local
analytic manuscript proves this before its final VOA fusion-form comparison.
The main improvement is to name the existence theorem and separate its net
conclusion from the categorical all-surface factorization theorem and from
analytic sewing on arbitrary Riemann surfaces. I found no reason to weaken
the diagonal theory, the selected E8 full theory, or the rational defect and
boundary formulas to conjectures.

PRIORITIZED CONCRETE REPAIRS

1. r05_full_theories.tex:28--31 and r08_sewing.tex:224--235: replace the generic
   phrase 'canonical rational full-CFT construction' with its exact route at
   least once. r04_unitarity.tex:550--555 already distinguishes the routes,
   but does not name the theorem that realizes the diagonal full theory.

   Suggested r05 replacement:
   'The diagonal Longo--Rehren construction applied to the completely
   rational net of Chapter~\ref{ch:unitarity} gives a unitary local full
   conformal net with this Hilbert space. The rational full-center
   construction organizes its sewing data. Merely writing the
   modular-invariant sum would not supply either construction.'

   The second sentence is optional; the first alone pins down actual
   existence without suggesting that a net is defined by a completed table
   of Riemann-surface amplitudes. No additional model-specific premise is
   missing: the net has complete rationality, positive finite-index sectors,
   and the ordinary-module/Hamiltonian identification needed for the trace.
   The selected extension full theory is also actual: H' is the positive
   holomorphic VOA identified with VE8, so H' tensor its antiholomorphic
   counterpart is the familiar lattice full theory. Its U embedding remains
   part of the choice, as the text correctly stresses.

2. r08_sewing.tex:176--188 and 224--235: add one compact scope paragraph,
   rather than weakening 'organizes oriented full-CFT sewing data'. That
   statement is a correct standard theorem in the TFT framework. The
   distinction is that its correlators are vectors in the modular-functor
   spaces, and interpreting them as analytic functions on all surfaces uses
   the corresponding analytic chiral-block and sewing comparison. The
   abstract MTC alone supplies neither that interpretation nor analytic
   convergence. The opening at 179--181 already says this correctly, but
   'full-theory existence' several paragraphs later can obscure the point.

   Suggested insertion after the algebra-object paragraph:
   'There are two different kinds of sewing statement here. The TFT theorem
   proves invariance under mapping classes and bulk and boundary
   factorization for its categorical correlators. To interpret these as
   analytic amplitudes on Riemann surfaces one also uses the chiral
   conformal-block theory and its sewing and convergence theorems. The
   annulus comparison in Chapter~\ref{ch:unitarity} establishes the
   positivity and VOA--net statements used in this construction; it is not
   itself a proof of every higher-genus analytic sewing theorem. The
   diagonal local conformal net already exists by the preceding net
   construction.'

   This does not assert that higher-genus analytic sewing is impossible or
   unproved in the literature. It identifies exactly what the arguments
   cited in this book establish. The phrase at 227--228 ('a long table ...
   does not by itself prove ... all sectors and genera') is true and can stay.

3. r08_sewing.tex:184--188: positivity should be attributed to the positive
   Q-system and unitary realization, rather than to the symmetric-special-
   Frobenius identities alone. Present text already mentions the unitary
   Q-system and 'positivity conditions', so this is a useful sharpening,
   not a discovered false theorem. Suggested replacement of the two vague
   sentences:
   'The Frobenius identities by themselves do not assert reflection
   positivity. Here the positive Q-system and the unitary chiral theory
   provide the adjoint and Hilbert-space pairings used by the unitary
   construction.'

   At 37--38, optionally define a reflection-positive channel as one whose
   two groups of insertions are related by reflection and adjoint. Its
   pairing is a Gram matrix of states. This is not a claim that the D matrix
   for arbitrary unrelated external fields is positive. The current
   qualification 'the appropriate matrix' avoids the mathematical error,
   but a beginning reader would benefit from the concrete Gram explanation.

PREMISE/CONCLUSION LEDGER

* Actual full local theory: hi_cft_c8_proof.tex:244--265 states the diagonal
  unitary full conformal-net theorem. hi_cft_c8_manuscript_analytic.tex:
  861--881 proves it from the completely rational net by Rehren's diagonal
  construction. It explicitly says the final annulus compatibility claim
  is not needed for this net construction or the character matching.
  Primary checked: K.-H. Rehren, Canonical tensor product subfactors,
  arXiv:math/9911149v2, Theorem 1.4, Proposition 1.5, Corollary 1.6.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9911149
  Corollary 1.6 constructs a local two-dimensional net from the chiral
  DHR system and the indicated Q-system/alpha-induction data; identity
  coupling gives charge conjugation, agreeing with the displayed diagonal
  partition function because all twelve labels here are self-dual.

* Categorical sewing: Fjelstad--Fuchs--Runkel--Schweigert, TFT construction
  of RCFT correlators V, arXiv:hep-th/0503194v2, section 1 and the oriented
  modular-invariance/boundary-factorization/bulk-factorization theorems in
  section 2 (in particular Theorem 2.13 for bulk factorization).
  https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0503194
  The paper explicitly formulates its theorem categorically; analytical
  issues enter through the structures assumed in identifying this model
  with chiral conformal blocks. The theorem genuinely covers oriented
  world sheets and their categorical factorization, not just the torus.
  It is not a theorem that every symmetric special Frobenius algebra is
  unitary. No star/positive hypothesis appears in that general construction.

* Analytic diagonal full fields: Huang--Kong, Full field algebras,
  arXiv:math/0511328, Theorem 3.8, constructs the diagonal genus-zero full
  field algebra under the stated rationality, cofiniteness and duality
  hypotheses. Their Modular invariance for conformal full field algebras,
  arXiv:math/0609570, proves convergence/continuation of the relevant
  genus-one full traces and modular invariance of the diagonal construction
  (Theorem 5.1). These are precise analytic results, not only formal sums.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0511328
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0609570
  They are useful optional source-guide anchors if the book names analytic
  genus-zero/genus-one conclusions. No need to introduce this additional
  route merely to support the already-established net existence theorem.

* Unitarity and reflection: r04 concludes complete unitarity, not merely
  independent positive metrics on modules. Together with a specified
  positive Q-system it supplies the correct unitary extension input.
  For reflected Euclidean insertions, the positivity mechanism is the
  Hilbert-space identity sum_ij conjugate(c_i)c_j <Theta(O_i) O_j>
  = ||sum_j c_j O_j Omega||^2 >= 0, with the usual separated/regularized
  insertions. Extending that statement to a particular all-surface analytic
  construction uses its own analytic implementation; FRS identities alone
  do not substitute for it.

R05/R07 CHECKS

r05:11--15 properly inherits construction and positivity hypotheses; all
subsequent character counting is within the two actually constructed
theories. Lines 581--590 correctly distinguish existence from explicit OPE
coefficients. No additional caveat is needed on spectra or thermodynamics.

r07:28--110 (Verlinde lines, twining/twisting) and 212--239 (Cardy annuli and
g factors) are standard consequences for the actual rational diagonal
theory. The derivation of the annulus constraint alone would not establish
all boundary sewing, but the text presents these as boundaries in the
canonical rational construction, not as arbitrary solutions of a NIM-rep.
An optional one-clause cross-reference to the positive full-center
construction is enough; do not make their existence conditional on a new
unverified model-specific input.

r07:345--414 correctly distinguishes the selected extension's all-module
category H3, trivial local-module category, and 36 rational U-preserving
bimodule defects. The right-module/opposite composition qualification is
now precise. The twisted Hilbert-space formula explicitly invokes the
rational defect construction for this selected extension and is not being
deduced merely from the finite fusion ring. Keeping 'preserving U' and the
specified embedding is essential; both are present.

The net-side compatibility of the categorical full center with local full
theories is also an established theorem, not a missing Haagerup conjecture:
Bischoff--Kawahigashi--Longo, Characterization of 2D rational local conformal
nets and its boundary conditions: the maximal case, arXiv:1410.8848,
identifies the full-center and generalized Longo--Rehren constructions and
relates the full net to the Morita class of the positive Q-system.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.8848
This is a useful concise primary reference if the source guide needs one
bridge between r07 and r08. It should not be paraphrased as classification
of all stress-tensor-preserving defects of the theory.

No chapter edits made. Recommended size of changes: roughly half a page
total, concentrating the distinction in r08 and naming Longo--Rehren once
in r05 or r04. Avoid repeating a generic disclaimer at every formula.
