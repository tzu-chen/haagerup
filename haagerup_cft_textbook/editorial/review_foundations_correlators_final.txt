1. Normalized products and the u correlators
==========================================
The coefficient at r06:26-28 is correct for the stated antilinear-first
Hermitian convention: projection to v_k supplies 1/N_k, while replacing
that output by v_k/sqrt(N_k) leaves the denominator sqrt(N_i N_j N_k).
It is paired with the radial adjoint at infinity, as the text states.
Cartan norms 2 and 78 give squared couplings 1/2 and 169/78=13/6.
The stress norm is c/2=4, so T/2 has unit norm and its coupling to a
unit h=2 field is h/sqrt(c/2)=1.

The three transported raw norm contributions are 4, (1/10)40, (1/10)40.
Thus ||u||^2=12 and its three coefficients in a unit momentum basis
all have modulus 1/sqrt(3). The momentum Gram matrix is 2I+2J, with
eigenvalues 8,2,2. Charge conservation therefore really is equality of
the positive and negative momentum multisets, not merely a necessary
condition. The counts 3+6+6 and the finite-distance powers in both
the chapter and ex:corr-fifteen's solution agree. Even pairings make
the lattice fields commute, and the paired cocycle/adjoint convention
leaves coefficient 1/9 for all surviving terms.

The resulting numerator 3-4x+4x^2 and both crossing identities check
algebraically. The values at 1/2 are 512/3 and 11/3. Distinct equal-charge
momenta sum to squared norm 12, whereas identical ones give 16. Hence
the first state has h=6 and its coefficient starts at x^2. Its three
orthogonal momentum components have magnitude 2/3, giving norm squared
4/3 and normalized coupling magnitude 2/sqrt(3). It is a sum of lattice
exponentials at that weight, so it is indeed a Virasoro primary.

For triangular subtraction, the reduced Taylor coefficients are
1,8/3,6,12,65/3. At order four, the lower global blocks contribute
  (8/3)(1/4)=2/3,
  (14/3)(9/10)=21/5,
  (58/9)(3/2)=29/3.
The remainder is 107/15. The charged reduced function begins
(4/3)x^6. The solution uses the reduced function rather than confusing
the raw x^2 power with exchanged weight two. At weight two, global
quasiprimaries consist of the total stress and Virasoro primaries;
derivatives of currents are not quasiprimary. Thus subtracting stress
contribution 1 from 14/3 really leaves 11/3 for the primary sum.

2. Weight-two resolution and the stress example
===============================================
The eight stated multiplets sum to 96, and the other dimensions are
5+9+3=17. The internal left/right designation is explicit and does not
mix internal sl2 spin with physical chirality. The level-one left
spin-two candidate is a null current descendant, as stated.

For commuting stresses, norms are c_i/2 and cross norms vanish.
L_2 acting on sum_i a_i T_i gives (sum_i a_i c_i)/2 times the vacuum;
L_1 and higher positive modes give no other condition. This verifies
the primary criterion and the three-dimensional difference space.
For A=c_2 T_1-c_1 T_2,
  N_A=c_1 c_2(c_1+c_2)/2,
  <A,A_(1)A>=c_1 c_2(c_2^2-c_1^2)=2N_A(c_2-c_1).
Both the chapter and solution then give
8(c_2-c_1)^2/[c_1 c_2(c_1+c_2)]. At the displayed central charges this
reduces to 23104/9243. Equal central charges kill the cubic coefficient,
not the positive norm.

The Ward-completion identity follows by applying the zero-mode
derivation and commuting its action through the primary projection.
The latter commutation uses invariance under the compact current action
and then its complexified Lie algebra. E F^p v_0=p(n-p)F^(p-1)v_0 is
correct for a dimension-n sl2 representation. The count 18,365 is
properly attributed to the recorded tensor calculation; this hand
review does not independently replay that large enumeration.

3. Spin-three normalization, pole residues, and odd-spin signs
=============================================================
The canonical positive generator has radial Hermitian mode convention
a_(n)^dagger=a_(4-n). Thus b=a_(1)a has
||b||^2=<a,a_(3)b>=A||a||^2=AN=B. For a weight-three primary this
Hermitian convention is consistent with PCT(a)=-a; PCT reality and
Hermitian self-adjointness of a chiral field must not be conflated.
The chapter does not make that conflation, and its phrase "canonical
positive normalization" selects the phase used in these equations.
The raw coordinate fields in the source have explicitly different
rescaling factors, so substituting them without rescaling would be
wrong, but no such substitution occurs here.

The OPE coefficients are mutually consistent under skew symmetry:
the simple pole is (1/2)partial b-(1/12)partial^3 T_0. The Ward
three-point function is 3N/(1-w)^2 when the third insertion is w;
its derivatives at zero give 6N and 72N. Likewise the b correlator
is B/(1-w)^4, giving derivative 4B. This last dependence follows
from translation and scaling even though b is not quasiprimary.
The simple-pole coefficient is therefore 2B-6N=2B-2c_0, as in
the chapter and ex:corr-spin3's solution. The remaining residues
and constant N^2 agree. Integer-spin locality gives a meromorphic
function on the sphere; the prescribed principal parts plus its
constant at infinity determine it completely. No finite-series
fit is being mistaken for this all-orders argument.

4. Direct level-four Virasoro Gram check
=======================================
Writing v=L_{-4}|0> and w=L_{-2}^2|0>, the elementary commutators give
  <v,v>=5c_0,
  <v,w>=3c_0,
  <w,w>=c_0(c_0+8)/2.
Since partial^2 T_0=2v and Lambda=w-(3/5)v, one obtains
  ||partial^2 T_0||^2=20c_0,
  <Lambda,partial^2 T_0>=0,
  ||Lambda||^2=c_0(5c_0+22)/10.

There is also a direct check that the printed subtraction really
is primary, rather than merely assuming its orthogonality. For
b=a_(1)a, the primary-mode commutator gives
  L_1 b=3 partial T_0,
  L_2 b=10T_0,
  L_3 b=0,
  L_4 b=3c_0|0>.
Its Gram overlaps are <partial^2 T_0,b>=6c_0 and
<Lambda,b>=16c_0/5. Dividing by the corresponding norms gives
3/10 and 32/(5c_0+22), precisely the two printed coefficients.
Subtracting them cancels every positive Virasoro mode through L_4;
higher modes vanish by weight. Thus W_4 is an intrinsic primary.

The residual norm divided by N^2 simplifies to the chapter's gamma_4^2.
For Potts, Q=26 and lambda Q=8; positivity makes the zero-norm vector
zero in the actual unitary algebra. For PF13, Q=30, the remaining
factor is 3364/473 and the prefactor is 18/5, giving 60552/2365.
All quantities use the intrinsic c_0, not the ambient c=8. The chapter
states this distinction explicitly.

5. Light-sector blocks, Gram data, and full-CFT scope
===================================================
The beta vectors have norm squared 2/3 and mutual product -1/3.
The finite Gaussian factors are x^(-2/3) and
(1-x)^(-<beta_i,beta_j>), with the specified real branch on (0,1).
This checks both diagonal and off-diagonal formulas and the need
for a path upon chiral analytic continuation.

At intermediate weight one, the beta Heisenberg vector projects
onto H_R with coefficient q_i/78; it has no projection onto H_L
or the root currents. The U-current Gram contribution is therefore
q_i q_j/78. Subtracting it from I-J/3 gives exactly the displayed
solution matrix. The mutually orthogonal vectors (1,1,1), (1,0,-1),
and (1,-2,1) have eigenvalues 0,35/39,1. In particular, the extremal
diagonal split is 2/39+8/13=2/3. Negative off-diagonal overlaps do
not contradict positive semidefiniteness.

The intermediate ambient lattice vacuum is the extension U plus b,
so a selected lattice block restricts to a particular combination
of these U channels. It is a genuine U chiral intertwiner block,
but not a complete channel basis. The two ambient lattice sectors
restricting to c_1 do not identify their extension sewing with the
diagonal-U bulk sewing. Consequently the warning against inferring
the latter's lightest-scalar bulk correlator from a single absolute
square is mathematically necessary and correctly scoped.

Conversely, the earlier u, current, stress, and spin-three fields
are in the U vacuum module. Their displayed meromorphic correlators
are actual chiral vacuum correlators, and pairing them with the
chosen antiholomorphic vacuum counterparts does give full vacuum-
sector observables. The opening paragraph correctly makes this
positive statement while reserving nonvacuum sewing for r08.
