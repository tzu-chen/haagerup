1. Positive partial energies and C2 control: 59-158
==================================================
The compatible grading and ambient weight-two quasiprimary conditions have
the correct distinct jobs. Compatibility makes the degree of each A_i mode
agree in the intrinsic and ambient gradings. Ambient weight two gives
[L0^V,L0^(e_i)]=0, so the partial operator preserves finite-dimensional V_n.
PCT reality and L1^V e_i=0 give the mode adjoint formula from the invariant
form. In particular L0^(e_i) is self-adjoint on V_n. The proof uses this
finite-dimensional adjoint statement; it needs no unbounded-operator
domain assertion or spectral theorem on the completed Hilbert space.

Each component is of CFT type: its compatible nonnegative grading and
vacuum sit inside V, and its degree-zero space is C1. Thus the CFT-type
finiteness theorems used here have their required grading hypothesis.
The regularity assumption supplies decomposition of the restriction of V
as an algebraic direct sum of ordinary simple A_i-modules. An A_i-module
map intertwines the actual intrinsic conformal mode, so multiplicity
copies do not acquire free energy shifts. C2,A commutes with this direct
sum. Finite module quotients and finitely many component simple types
therefore really do give one bound D_i for the whole restriction.

Primary check: Li proves regularity implies C2 finiteness; Abe--Buhl--Dong
Proposition 5.2 supplies the needed module quotient for irreducible weak
modules, not just the vacuum. Their Corollary 5.7 also supplies ordinaryness
of irreducible weak modules under C2 finiteness.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9807077
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0204021

For v in V_n orthogonal to C2(V), each partial-energy eigenvector of energy
above D_i lies in C2(V), so its coefficient in v vanishes. This remains
true without invariance of C2(V) under the partial energy. Applying the
expectation bound separately for each i and summing with t_i>0 is valid.
The proof never chooses a common eigenbasis. Ambient homogeneity of C2(V)
then identifies its finite-grade orthogonal complements with quotient
grades. No topological closure of C2(V) is being assumed.

2. Tops and nonnegative module energies: 167-230
================================================
The condition on all negative-degree modes is essential and is correctly
stated. For an ordinary simple component module, a higher-energy vector
in Omega would generate, by mode reordering, a submodule with no vectors
below that energy; simplicity excludes this. Consequently the highest
space is the genuine lowest top, and restriction to the semisimple top
algebra gives diagonalizable partial energies on the finite invariant E.

No positive Hermitian form on an unknown V-module is used. The separate
spectra are known component lowest weights. The equation
h dim(E)=sum_i t_i Tr_E L0^(e_i) proves both reality and nonnegativity of h,
even though the matrices need not commute. If h=0, diagonalizability and
the nonnegative spectra force each matrix to vanish. The unique-zero-top
condition identifies the relevant component summands with vacuum copies;
their intrinsic L_{-1} kills the top. Adding the stress identity then
kills the ambient L_{-1}. This is stronger than merely saying L0 is zero,
and is exactly what the next lemma needs.

3. Vacuum-like lemma and projectivity: 232-311
================================================
The translation induction is correctly indexed:
L_{-1}a_{(n+1)}v=-(n+1)a_{(n)}v, and its coefficient is nonzero for every
n>=0. Lower truncation begins the downward induction. The resulting
creation-type condition supplies the V-module map a -> a_{(-1)}v by
module associativity. The lemma concerns a weak module and requires no
weight decomposition, positivity, or rationality of that module.

The projectivity proof first establishes finite length and ordinary
composition factors from the C2-cofinite module theory. This is before
the argument about their weights and avoids assuming semisimplicity.
Generalized weight spaces are exact: a map commuting with locally finite
L0 sends each generalized eigenspace to its counterpart, and decomposing
a preimage into its finitely many generalized components proves
surjectivity on that eigenspace. Induction on length therefore gives
real nonnegative weights in E, including the absence of any target for
a negative-degree mode from E_[0]. It also gives E_[0] -> V_0 onto.

Partial zero modes preserve E_[0] because their ambient degree is zero.
Compatible-top semisimplicity applies on this finite space. Ambient L0
is nilpotent there, so its trace is zero even before it is proved
diagonalizable. Nonnegative component traces again force all partial
energies to vanish. The vacuum-top argument kills their translations.
The lifted vector therefore gives a section with pi(s(a))=a. This proves
projectivity against every epimorphism in the specified category, not
only extensions with a simple kernel.

5. Moderate growth, rigidity, and semisimplicity: 336-425
=======================================================
The source theorem is used with the correct hypotheses. Etingof--Penneys
Theorem 1.1 requires a Cauchy-complete linear braided category with finite
Hom spaces and scalar endomorphisms of the unit, and a non-negligible
indecomposable whose nth tensor-power endomorphism space has dimension
less than n! for some n. It does not assume an abelian category with
biexact tensor product. The statement at 355-361 is therefore accurate.
  https://arxiv.org/html/2412.17681v3#S1

The underlying finite abelian category has a finite-dimensional algebra
model and a regular projective generator P. The surjection from
P^{oplus d0(Z)} onto Z is valid; right exactness alone preserves it after
tensoring with X. Iterating the bound from Z=1 gives
d0(X^n)<=d0(1) C_X^n. Faithfulness of the algebra model bounds End by the
square of vector-space dimension. The factorial inequality thus follows
without assuming exactness of tensoring or rigidity.

For every nonzero indecomposable, its evaluation is a nonzero map onto
the simple unit, hence an epimorphism. Projectivity of the unit splits
this specific epimorphism, which is precisely non-negligibility in the
primary theorem. Idempotent completeness and finite indecomposable
decompositions are available in a finite abelian category. The theorem
therefore makes each summand rigid, and finite direct sums are rigid.

Only at this point does the proof use both tensor adjoints and exactness.
The usual dual adjunction Hom(X,-)=Hom(1,- tensor X*) then makes every
object projective. All short exact sequences split. There is no appeal
to categorical quantum dimensions, nonzero categorical traces, or
unitarity of the unknown module category in this passage.
