Cross-chapter physical consistency review
Reviewer: lattice author, October 5, 2026.
Read-only review; no numerical work or chapter edits.

Scope and reading context
-------------------------
Read the actual master-file ordering, reading guide and notation appendix;
r01, r03, r05--r08; c06; and the physical interpretation, normalization,
scope and worked examples throughout l01--l06. Revisited the completed
charge-six extension audit to check the actual-versus-selected extension
distinction. Local derivations were already reviewed; this pass concentrates
on whether their objects and conventions agree across chapter boundaries.
Line references below are to the files as read during this review.

Overall result
--------------
No contradictory physical identification was found. The text keeps the
diagonal c=8 theory, selected E8 extension, rational C6 commutant,
irrational chiral commutant, physical rho-projector chain, and exact gapped
comparison Hamiltonian distinct. The local estimates are consistently
conditional when applied to a continuum limit. There is, however, one
concrete symbol collision that should be repaired, and three smaller
notation changes would substantially reduce first-year-reader confusion.

1. REPAIR: the length-three path Hilbert space prints as the category H3
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
l01:44--89 uses \HH_L for the regular periodic Hilbert space, including
the statement \dim\HH_3=36.
preamble.tex:49 defines \HH=\mathcal H.
The fusion category is also \mathcal H_3, throughout the book and explicitly
as \HH_3 in c06:690--707.

Thus two different objects have exactly the same printed name, and the
sentence about dimension becomes especially misleading:
  path-space dimension at L=3 = 36;
  fusion-category global dimension = 3(1+d^2), not 36.

Suggested repair: use \HH_{\mathrm{path},L} for the finite chain space
throughout l01, including the numerical subscripts in the counting example.
Keep \mathcal H_3 for the category. This is a small localized change and
does not require changing the full-CFT Hilbert-space notation \HH_diag.
If the notation appendix gains a chain row, give this path-space notation.

2. REPAIR ADVISED: c_L is both a chirality and a circumference label
-----------------------------------------------------------------
l02:20 uses c_L=c_R=c, in agreement with r01 and the reading guide.
l02:170,183 then uses c_L^(T) for the finite size-L stress estimator.
The notation is defined locally, so the formula is not wrong, but this is
exactly the factor-of-two-sensitive passage where L should not change
meaning silently.

Suggested repair: write \widehat c_T(L)=8|M|^2/g_T^2 and use the same table
heading. The quoted c_18^(T) can become \widehat c_T(18).
This also advertises that it is a finite diagnostic rather than the
limiting left central charge.

3. REPAIR ADVISED: preserve the thermal dimensionless-variable convention
------------------------------------------------------------------------
r05:291 defines b=2*pi*beta*v/L, so the modular fixed point is b=2*pi.
l02:346--374 instead defines b=L/v, the physical inverse temperature at
the modular fixed point. All equations in each passage are internally
correct, but a reader carrying the first convention into the second
will introduce factors of 2*pi.
c06:510--527 uses beta directly against L0-c/24, hence dimensionless
chiral inverse temperatures.

Suggested minimal repair:
  l02: call the physical fixed temperature beta_* = L/v throughout;
       c=6 beta_* mu/pi, beta_* m3=3m2, etc.
       Add "It corresponds to b=2*pi in Chapter [full theories]."
  c06: at the two-temperature trace, add "Here beta and epsilon are
       dimensionless coefficients of conformal energies."
There is no need to rewrite any derivation or introduce another
temperature convention.

4. REPAIR ADVISED: the chain translation reuses the constructed VOA's U
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
l01:232--274 names the translation U immediately after many chapters in
which U denotes the central-charge-eight vertex algebra.
This is local and not a mathematical contradiction, but the use is avoidable.
Suggested name: U_tr, with U_tr^L=1, leaving the color action A unchanged.
The Fourier sign and all momentum selection equations are correct.

5. Optional short bridge: name the charge-six full theory explicitly
-------------------------------------------------------------------
c06:99--166 carefully constructs the chiral pair C6 subset V_N and its
transported extension algebra. It then describes a route to physical
charge-six defects, with the correct analytic qualifications. Unlike
r05, this section does not display the resulting full Hilbert space.
For a first-year physics reader, one short paragraph could say that,
once the quoted positive extension/full-theory bridge is applied, the
ordinary diagonal lattice theory is
  H_{N,diag} = direct sum over lambda in N*/N of
              V_{N+lambda} tensor overline{V_{N+lambda}},
with 39 lattice sectors and c_L=c_R=6.
The transported noninvertible defects preserve C6, not every V_N current.
This would immediately explain how a pointed maximal lattice category
coexists with the smaller-algebra-preserving Haagerup defect factor.

Keep the original-versus-selected qualification: the transported algebra
in c06 is the actual original E8/U algebra A. Its boundary category is
whichever H2/H3 possibility that original embedding realizes. Do not rename
it the selected canonical H3 algebra without making that additional
selection. The chapter currently preserves this scope; the extra paragraph
would only make the full-theory object visible.

6. Optional very small notation/readability aids
------------------------------------------------
* c06:358--367 calls the full opposite irrational commutant M_f.
  l05:171 uses M_f for the alpha-fixed finite algebra module. Both are
  explicitly defined, but M_fix in l05 would eliminate a possible false
  connection between unrelated constructions.
* r06:173--182 calls the two internal sl2 labels "left and right" and
  refers to a "right spin-two multiplet". These are internal current
  spins, not spacetime conformal spins or opposite chirality. r05:196--198
  already explains this correctly. A short reminder in r06 would make
  the weight-two counting easier to enter independently.
* The l01 category notation H2/H3 is plain H, while most earlier chapters
  use \mathcal H_2/\mathcal H_3. Once path-space notation is repaired,
  harmonizing the category font would be useful but is not essential.

The six theory layers, checked against their actual claims
----------------------------------------------------------
A. Diagonal U theory:
   r05:20--31 specifies canonical rational sewing and twelve U-module
   pairings. Its nine lightest scalars have Delta=2/3, and the full
   invariant sector for all twelve Verlinde lines is U tensor Ubar.
   r07 derives that projector from modular orthogonality. The theory
   carries the center's twelve canonical Verlinde lines; it is not
   being silently assigned the six-line H3 action of the E8 extension.

B. Selected E8 extension:
   r05:33--57 uses H', not the original H, and the actual canonical
   algebra 1+b+2a. Equal characters of a and c0 are explicitly not
   used to identify algebra multiplication or the embedding.
   r07 uses C=Rep(U)_A for its six boundaries and C x C^op for its
   36 U-preserving defects. The first-factor action has invariant
   space U tensor H'bar, giving 1488 dimension-two scalars; both
   factors give 36. These agree with r05.

C. Rational charge-six commutant:
   c06 supplies mutual lattice commutants, a specific rationality
   theorem, Rep(C6)=P39^rev x Rep(U), and the transported original
   algebra including its multiplication. It does not identify C6
   with the pointed lattice extension V_N.
   The reverse coset's 6 Z_L0 is a repeated-branching prescription,
   not a six-vacuum critical theory or an identified gapped vacuum
   module. This is consistent with the warning in r08:284--299.

D. Irrational chiral commutant:
   c06 distinguishes the exact semiconformal stress e of charge c_*,
   the actual N_e vacuum algebra, and several proposed full spaces.
   It does not equate a commuting stress with a tensor factor.
   The conditional trace-divergence theorem assumes the all-grade
   opposite-algebra identity. The strict diagonal continuum theorem
   has a different state-space hypothesis and gives c_eff=1.
   Neither is treated as a global no-go for c_* local theories.
   l02 and l06 correctly refrain from relabeling c_* as the chain's
   measured central charge.

E. Physical chain:
   l01 fixes the real unitary H3 associator, regular periodic closure
   and H=-sum P_rho. The finite H2/H3 trace distinction and the
   open-H2 entropy warning do not migrate between Hamiltonians.
   l02--l04 retain finite-size, finite-source and finite-entanglement
   qualifications. The inventory of charges does not identify tube
   modules or imply a fully specified continuum category.

F. Gapped comparisons:
   l05's Q lies in the nonbraided H3 line category; its right modules
   are vacua for a category action, not the braided extension-module
   category Rep(U)_A from r07. This distinction is explicit.
   Four exact tensors, a sufficiently long parent and its positive
   gap do not prove the short missing-channel parent or the physical
   rho-projector Hamiltonian is gapped. The theorem's Hilbert-space
   and symmetry scope is correct.
   Vacuum Frobenius form factors are ordinary finite-algebra moments,
   not CFT three-point coefficients unless a separate limiting
   operator projection is demonstrated.

Normalization checks passed
----------------------------
* c always denotes one chiral charge: r01 cylinder shift, r05
  E=Delta-2/3 and E_vac=-4*pi*v/(3L), l02 Casimir term, and the
  thermal modular moments all agree. The suggested temperature rename
  above removes only a symbol conflict.
* Stress norm c/2, unit stress sqrt(2/c) T, and the unit external
  primary coupling h sqrt(2/c) agree across r01, r06, l02 and l03.
  At c=8,h=2 the coefficient is one. The pants Schwarzian uses one
  chiral stress and gives B sqrt(c/8), with no extra factor of two.
* The primary pants ratio divides by the same-shape vacuum overlap;
  cylinder factors are not inserted twice. The vacuum amplitude
  exponent is c/8 (the squared-overlap logarithm has coefficient c/4).
  Its outgoing conjugation matches the OPE coefficient convention.
* r01, r05 and l06 all use h=p_L^2/2 and the circle convention
  p_L=m/R+nR/2, self-dual R=sqrt2. The l06 shift quotient R/n
  and the r05 enhanced-current boost have this same convention.
* The r06 raw lattice gauge has norms (4,40,40); normalized
  exponentials have unit norms. The distinction is explicit, so
  the defining u's norm 12 and the 1/sqrt3 unit expansion agree.
* Chiral adjoints stay chiral in r01 and r06. Full scalar dimensions
  are twice the chiral top weight only under the specified equal
  pairing. Fractional twisted spins in r07 are not asserted to be
  ordinary bosonic local spins.
* The physical Fourier sign in l01 raises lattice momentum by m.
  l02 consequently uses H_2 -> (2*pi*v/L)(L_-2+bar L_2).
  Carrier momenta +/-2*pi/3 are separated from residual conformal
  spin and from the proposed internal translation C3 in l03/l06.
* The line invariance eigenvalue is d, not 1, in both r07 and l01.
  An intrinsic alpha-neutral state need not be fully invariant;
  l02's neutral light chi/epsilon fields are separated from the
  source-visible fully invariant singlet.
* Closed-ring entropy uses c/3, open-chain entropy c/6, and the
  one-cut finite-entanglement formula c(1+1/n)/12. Cat mixing and
  the ordinary height partial trace are identified separately.
* Top dimensions, categorical dimensions and primary-component
  multiplicities are kept distinct in r03/r05. Character equality
  a=c0 does not erase their categorical or defect distinction.
* The Cardy phase test in l06 assumes the actual identity-algebra
  sewing, complete isolated shells and positive nonvacuum weights.
  It does not contradict the diagonal c8 construction: that theory
  does not have the required isolated real two-dimensional light
  shells. Its c1 top has dimension three, hence nine bulk components.
