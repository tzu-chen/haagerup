1. Main repair: c05:750--774 hides the derivation of the three C_j.

The third indicator alone forces cubic twists; it does not by itself
force one copy of each cubic root or equal C_j dimensions. The existing
phrase 'nu3(alpha)=1, which fixes the latter cubic twists' should name
nu1(alpha)=0 as well. A short self-contained derivation can use:

  R=rho+alpha rho+alpha²rho;
  FI(alpha)=FI(alpha²)=3alpha+3alpha²+3mR.

Adjunction gives squared norms and mutual inner product3 for I(alpha)
and I(alpha²). Thus they are the same multiplicity-free sum of three
simples C_j, disjoint from I(1). Centrality makes the multiplicities
of the three noninvertibles in each F(C_j) equal, while adjunction
gives one copy of each alpha and alpha²:

  F(C_j)=alpha+alpha²+b_jR,  sum b_j=3m,
  dim(C_j)=2+3b_j d_m.

The positive-dimensional nu3 trace is a sum of unit complex numbers
with positive weights attaining its maximal real value. Therefore
theta_j³=1 for each j. Now nu1 gives
sum_j(2+3b_j d_m)theta_j=0. The real quadratic field Q(d_m) and
Q(zeta3) have intersection Q, so this splits into sum theta_j=0
and sum b_j theta_j=0. Three cubic roots summing to zero occur once
each; the second equation then forces all b_j=m. This proves the
claimed twists AND equal dimensions s_m. It also explains why all
remaining simples forget to n_j R: all invertible-containing central
summands have been exhausted by the three induction objects.

The existing I(1)=1+X+2Y branching, Dim(F)=3s_m, and positive spherical
structure are sufficient here; no prior H3 identification is needed.
