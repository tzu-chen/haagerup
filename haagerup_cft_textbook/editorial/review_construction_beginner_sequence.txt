1. c01_lattice_embedding.tex
----------------------------
(a) Lines 22-29: S(...) and the twisted group-algebra notation are used
without their elementary meaning being explicit. Proposed sentence after
the displayed space:
  "Here S denotes the symmetric algebra of the negative oscillator modes,
  and the momentum algebra has multiplication
  e^alpha e^beta=epsilon(alpha,beta)e^(alpha+beta)."
This is just a definition of the symbols already in the display.

(b) Line 130, priority: "simple current" first appears here; the earlier
fusion primer defines invertible objects but does not give this VOA term.
Proposed replacement:
  "Here J is a simple current, meaning a simple module invertible under
  fusion, and J has order three: J fused with itself three times is B."
One can retain the current displayed H=B+J+J^2 without changing notation.

(c) Lines 320-326: the explicit PCT formula leaves h_i unchanged while
claiming antilinearity. This is correct for h_i in the real Cartan, but the
opening lattice section has h=L tensor C. Add before the formula:
  "In the explicit formula take the h_i in the real lattice Cartan and
  extend antilinearly to complex coefficients."
This prevents a beginner from treating arbitrary complex h_i as fixed.

2. c02_finite_closure.tex
-------------------------
(a) Lines 114-133: N_d first appears in the theorem without a definition.
After N=N(S_1+...+S_h), add:
  "A subscript d on N denotes its homogeneous part of conformal weight d."

(b) Lines 244-253, priority: E_L,E_R were states in c01, but from here they
act as operators on quotient spaces, and later FE is a composition. Add:
  "In this representation-theoretic section E_L,E_R,F_L,F_R abbreviate
  the zero-mode operators of the corresponding current states."
The existing compact rescaling does not affect FE or the raising images.

(c) Lines 285-287: "nine independent highest left fields" is an unexplained
count after the 28-constituent table. A short provenance reminder would help:
  "The nine primitive tests are the eight listed nontrivial weight-two
  multiplets and one independent neutral stress; affine normal states and
  the ambient stress are already controlled."
This agrees with the original eight multiplets plus retained singlet. Here
"left" means the first input of f_a, not the L current factor; replacing
"highest left fields" by "highest fields in the first input" avoids that
second ambiguity without adding a sentence.

(d) Lines 447-450, priority: "charged" and "bottom" have not been defined
for these gate witnesses. Add before the witness discussion:
  "Here charged means a nontrivial representation of the retained current
  zero modes, neutral means a current singlet, and q_- is a lowest-weight
  vector of the triplet Q."
These are internal current labels, not charges of a Haagerup defect line.

3. c03_factors.tex
------------------
(a) Lines 26-30: L(4/5,0) and L(4/5,3) occur without a local notation
dictionary. Proposed sentence:
  "We write L(c,h) for the simple highest-weight Virasoro module of central
  charge c and lowest weight h."
The earlier Ising discussion explains the modules but does not explicitly
establish this L(c,h) notation.

(b) Lines 140-147, priority: C(G,q) is not defined in the preceding chapters.
The twist following the display helps, but does not explicitly say what
the general notation denotes. Add:
  "The notation C(G,q) denotes the pointed braided category with simples
  g in G, fusion given by group addition, and twist exp(2 pi i q(g))."
This also serves the later order-thirteen and order-thirty-nine uses.

(c) Lines 201-205: three beta vectors precede a 2-by-2 Gram matrix. Replace
"Their Gram matrix" by "The Gram matrix of beta_1,beta_2". This is only a
small disambiguation, but it makes the linear dependence beta_3=beta_1+beta_2
visible rather than inviting the reader to look for a missing row.

(d) Lines 554-558, optional: the generator change taking 3j^2/13 to
-j^2/13 can be made explicit in one clause:
  "Replacing the old order-thirteen generator by twice itself gives
  3(2j)^2 congruent to -j^2 modulo thirteen."
No new theorem or classification is needed for this relabeling.

4. c04_regularity.tex
---------------------
(a) Lines 163 and 180-182, priority: "Zhu algebra" appears before it has
been described. At its first mention, add a parenthetical gloss:
  "the associative algebra that encodes degree-zero mode actions on
  lowest spaces".
The proof needs this interpretation and the stated semisimplicity theorem;
it does not need a new section giving Zhu's explicit quotient construction.

(b) Lines 317-325, priority: D has denoted translation throughout the previous
chapters, but DX now denotes categorical duality. Make the change explicit:
  "In this categorical section D denotes contragredient duality, so DX is
  the contragredient object; it is not the translation operator."
The following Hom formula then defines the exact duality property used.

No additional sentences are needed in the C2 energy cutoff or projective
vacuum proofs: the direct-sum quotient argument, noncommuting-energy warning,
generalized-weight exactness, and right-exactness example now supply the
important missing premises. Likewise the proof explains Cauchy completeness
where it is needed; it need not be defined a second time.

5. c05_branching.tex
--------------------
(a) Lines 96-98: "four traces" is followed by L being called an energy and
P,Z being called energies. For literal units, say:
  "On a core top let L,P,Z be the traces of the left affine, Potts, and
  PF13 energy operators, and let Q=Tr H_R(0)^2."
This preserves the excellent subsequent warning that W is bookkeeping on
an isolated row rather than that row's ambient energy.

(b) Lines 250-252, optional: the four mixed lattice sectors may surprise
a reader who remembers only the two glue sectors of H/B. Add:
  "There are four such B-module sectors because each of the two Z/3
  discriminant coordinates is nonzero; the E8 extension chooses two of
  them as its glue sectors."

(c) Lines 422-429, priority: "polar coefficients" and "cusp" are used
before the character chapter, which now follows unitarity. Add:
  "A polar character term is a negative power of q, equivalently a state
  of weight below c/24; the cusp is q=0, or tau tending to i infinity."
The explicit theta example then proves the needed cancellation mechanism;
there is no need to move the general character chapter earlier again.

(d) Lines 743-745, priority: d_m is used in the indicator bridge before
its definition in the next section. The ring was already derived, so
"the rank-six fusion ring below" is also a misleading direction. Replace
the opening with:
  "For the rank-six ring just derived, let d_m be the positive solution
  of d_m^2=1+3md_m. Its simple dimensions are 1,1,1,d_m,d_m,d_m."
The later radical expression can remain as the explicit solution.

(e) Lines 753-754 and 814-816: name the residual simple and twist before
the moment formulas. A compact replacement at 814 is:
  "Every remaining central simple Z_j has twist theta_j and forgets to
  n_j R, with n_j a positive integer."
The earlier bridge may call these "a residual induced summand" without
assuming the reader has already met that particular index convention.

(f) Lines 874-880 and 923, priority: phi and mu are undefined arithmetic
functions at their first use. phi earlier meant the golden ratio in the
Potts table. Add before the root-sum estimate:
  "Here phi(n) is Euler's totient, the number of residues coprime to n."
At the primitive-twentieth-root sum add "where mu is the Moebius function";
its usual description (zero for a square factor, otherwise (-1)^r for r
distinct prime factors) fits in the same sentence if desired. The later
thirteen-conductor paragraph presently supplies this name too late.

6. c06_cosets.tex
-----------------
(a) Lines 104-107 and 338, priority: u_0 denotes a norm-78 rank-one generator
in the rank-six lattice presentation, then a specific norm-20 plane vector.
This is an unnecessary apparent contradiction within one chapter. Rename
the earlier generator gamma_78 (two occurrences) and expand its definition:
  "Here omega_i is a fundamental weight of the ith A2 factor, alpha
  generates A1, and gamma_78 generates the rank-one lattice <78>."
Retain the norm values and leave the later u_0=(ell+h)/2 unchanged. Defining
the fundamental weights also explains the asserted order-six glue directly.

(b) Lines 148-153, priority: superscript rev appears before a local gloss,
whereas op is explained a little later. Add at first use:
  "The superscript rev reverses the braiding and twist; it does not reverse
  tensor order, which is denoted op below."
The notation appendix says this correctly, but occurs after this chapter.

(c) Lines 278-280, priority: the equation U_1=A_{1,1} direct-sum A_{1,39}
mixes a finite current Lie algebra with infinite-dimensional affine VOAs.
Proposed replacement:
  "The retained current Lie algebra is U_1=sl2 direct-sum sl2, generating
  the affine subalgebra A_{1,1} tensor A_{1,39}. Any transparent compact
  current denominator lies in this retained current algebra."
This also makes the meaning of the Sugawara central-charge bound clearer.

(d) Lines 633-645, optional: the character notation for branching spaces
is inferable but a short definition would make the square-sum inequality
transparent:
  "Here chi_{B_lambda}(beta)=Tr_{B_lambda}
  exp[-beta(L^e_0-c_*/24)], including the same chiral Casimir shift."
It prevents a reader from switching to unshifted degeneracy series midway
through the two-temperature trace proof.

(e) Line 875, optional: gloss dominant as "every simple target is a summand
of an image". The category was constructed to have this property, so the
gloss explains the theorem's hypothesis without adding a new argument.
