Pass, with one concise premise clarification
--------------------------------------------
c06_cosets.tex:400--404 should say "For any simple separable Frobenius
sewing algebra A..." Simplicity here is as an A--A bimodule. It ensures
the bimodule category is a fusion category with simple unit and supports
the displayed normalized FP-dimension formula. An arbitrary decomposable
separable algebra instead gives a multifusion category, for which a
single uniform ratio is not the usual fusion-category normalization.
The integrated W3 text already states the simple-bimodule premise at
l06_identification.tex:1030--1032, and the orbifold proof spells it out
at lines803--805. No stronger exclusion or new mathematical topic is
needed.
