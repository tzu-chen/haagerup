Checks that pass

1. l02:108-145: the internal spin-flip operator P=(-1)^N gives NS momenta
   with even physical fermion parity and R momenta with odd physical parity.
   Occupying the critical R zero mode imposes the projection at zero energy;
   it does not produce an extra physical twofold ground-state degeneracy.
   The NS pair at opposite momenta is in the energy family. The same-direction
   pair has even total parity, spin +/-2, and is the chiral stress descendant.
   The local bosonic continuum space at l02:317-332 includes the three diagonal
   Ising modules and excludes a lone chiral fermion as an additional local field.
   Consequently it is consistent with later integer-spin local-boson tests.

2. l02:184-220,276-361: E_NS=-2J csc x, E_R=-2J cot x, x=pi/(2L), v=2J;
   their difference is 2J tan(x/2). The energy and stress gaps are
   8J sin x and 16J sin x cos^2 x. Hence their stress-normalized ratio is
   sec^2 x, whereas the known-velocity energy dimension is sin x/x.
   They approach one from opposite sides exactly as stated. The vacuum-energy
   estimator approaches 1/2 from above, with correction 7pi^2/(480L^2).
   This does not conflict with the below-1/2 Ising pants estimators quoted at
   l03:683-685: these are different finite observables and calibrations.

3. l02:299-310: bare L_{-2}|0> has norm squared c/2=1/4; the displayed
   two-fermion state has norm one. This agrees with the normalized-stress
   matrix-element estimator later in l02 and the factors sqrt(c/2) in l03's
   pants amplitudes. The connected Ward invariant is likewise consistent.
   The total-momentum alias at L=4 is now stated correctly.

4. r05:193-325: with both chiral current algebras using +i epsilon, the
   unit-normalized isotropic scalar has C_OOO=-4/(sqrt(3) k). For the stated
   e^{-S_0-g int O} convention and increasing length, the shell cumulant gives
   beta_length=-pi C_OOO g^2=4pi g^2/(sqrt(3) k). Negative g is marginally
   irrelevant. Positive g loses weak-coupling control; no endpoint or mass gap
   is inferred. The Cartan comparison correctly distinguishes a vanishing
   quadratic obstruction from the all-order commuting-current theorem.

5. r05:327-394: O acts by 2 t_L.t_R/(sqrt(3) k), so for left/right spins
   1/2, q_J=[J(J+1)-3/2]/(sqrt(3) k). The two external neighborhoods give
   delta Delta=2pi g q_J. Integrating g~-[B_k log(r/a)]^{-1} yields logarithm
   powers 4pi q_J/B_k=-3/2,+1/2 at k=1. The limiting two-point power is r^{-1}.
   The final paragraph explicitly prevents an inference about the H3 chain,
   its endpoint, or transport of categorical junctions.

6. l03:800-834: the Hellmann--Feynman direction is a response to a specified
   bare lattice perturbation, not automatically the continuum beta direction.
   The nonparallel finite drifts are therefore used only to reject the stated
   one-parameter finite explanation. This is consistent with the new running
   example and with mixing of a lattice source with several continuum fields.

7. l04:365-389: the finite-entanglement entropy and order-amplitude slopes are
   expressly conditional on a suitable single-scale regime. The SU(2)_1
   logarithms in real-distance correlators are not silently assigned to these
   MPS observables. l06:1041-1046 also explicitly distinguishes the modular
   category Ising_{5/2} from the ordinary charge-1/2 Ising CFT just solved.

What is genuinely missing at the l06 ending

l06:15-31 already gives the four-layer checklist. Repeating that checklist at
the ending would not add much. The phrase "a limiting primary-field dictionary"
at l06:1161-1163 would benefit from an operational explanation of how the same
dictionary is constrained by different measurements, and from separating a
strong numerical identification from a proof of a scaling limit.

Suggested insertion, approximately 330 words, after that phrase or as a short
subsection immediately before the closing paragraph (editor may shorten):

"To make such a dictionary concrete, choose a candidate full CFT and a fixed
scaling-dimension cutoff. Track the entire low-energy space below that cutoff
along a specified size sequence, including the sectors required by the
proposal. A single assignment of these spaces must match rescaled energies,
residual momenta, and symmetry actions. Near-degenerate multiplets permit a
change of basis; they do not permit a different field identification for each
observable. The required vacuum and velocity must likewise be shared.

For each proposed continuum field, choose a controlled sequence of lattice
operators and its normalization and mixing coefficients. Use that same choice
for vacuum-to-field amplitudes, matrix elements between primary states, and
descendant matrix elements. The first group fixes the two-point normalization;
the second tests OPE coefficients; the third tests the Ward identities and
descendant assignment. Fitting an independent scale to each matrix element
would discard precisely the consistency that makes this a field dictionary.
Several microscopic recipes for the same field provide additional checks when
their separately fixed limits agree.

A correction model should then explain several observables using the same
irrelevant or running couplings and predict levels or amplitudes not used in
the fit. The solved Ising example shows that two valid finite-size calibrations
can approach the same limit from opposite sides. The current-current example
shows that one running coupling can produce different logarithms in different
fields. These are lessons about consistency, not correction exponents to
assign to the Haagerup chain.

A finite, mutually consistent table can support a strong numerical
identification. A proof that the chain has that scaling limit requires further
control of convergence of the state and operator data, compatibility as the
cutoff increases, and the resulting continuum correlations. The existence of
the candidate CFT is a separate question from this convergence. Finally, the
lattice lines must have the claimed limiting action and junction spaces, with
associators equivalent to the physical H3 data. Agreement of loop dimensions
or fusion multiplicities alone does not establish that part of the dictionary."

An optional short clarification of the alternative in l06:1164-1166:

For a gapped phase with q thermodynamic vacua, the finite-size diagnostic would
separate a collapsing cluster E_a-E_0 -> 0 (a=0,...,q-1) from a positive bulk
scale liminf(E_q-E_0)>0, with finite connected lengths inside pure vacua. This
does not prescribe an exponential splitting law, and q is not automatically
four merely because the exact comparison tensors have a rank-four vacuum
module. This is a useful precise reading of "complete collapsing vacuum
cluster" if the editor wants one additional displayed formula.

Follow-up: integrated replica section, l04:365-470

The integrated derivation passes the hand checks:
{g^{1/n},g}=(1-n^{-2})/(2g^2), (g'/g)^2=(u-v)^2/
[(w-u)^2(w-v)^2], and summing n sheets yields h_n=c(n-1/n)/24.
The interval moment exponent is -2 Delta_n, the single-cut exponent is
-Delta_n, and division by 1-n gives the two stated entropy coefficients.
The n->1 and largest-Schmidt-probability qualifications are appropriate.
For c=1/2 the interval coefficients 1/6,1/8,1/12 and the one-cut
coefficients 1/12,1/16,1/24 all pass. Applying the interval inversion c=3s
to a true single-cut slope s=1/12 indeed returns the wrong value 1/4.
The physical massive length versus finite-bond MPS length distinction is clear.

One necessary premise repair was sent immediately to root:
l04:464-466, global orthogonality of the components is insufficient for the
Schmidt probabilities to concatenate. Require mutually orthogonal Schmidt
supports on both sides of the cut, as for the ordered product cats, or state
directly that the reduced state has three orthogonal blocks with weights 1/3.
Counterexample: the three qutrit states
|Phi_b>=(1/sqrt(3)) sum_{a=0}^2 omega^{ab}|a,a>, b=0,1,2,
are globally orthogonal and each has Schmidt probabilities (1/3,1/3,1/3);
their normalized equal superposition is |0,0>, whose entropy is zero.
With the stronger block-support premise, p_a/3 repeated three times and the
additive log(3) formula are correct.
