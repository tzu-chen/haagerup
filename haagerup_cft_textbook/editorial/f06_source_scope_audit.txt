The chapter makes the following distinct claims.

1. The determinant ansatz proves linear transport/rotation identities
for reciprocal weights on finite-index Gamma subset 2Z^2. It does not
solve nonlinear equations for arbitrary quotients. Chapter derives
representative descent, exceptional axis, pair product, and Klein-four
one-sheet example. Sources: hi_noncyclic_determinant_linear_proof.tex,
hi_noncyclic_analytic_note.tex. There is no all-noncyclic existence claim.

2. The known Z4 x Z2 category is published GIS Theorem 4.5. Independently
verified primary arXiv HTML 1501.07324v1, theorem and proof. The exact
gamma identification F2=lambda8^-3, F3/F1=lambda8^2 is local work:
hi_noncyclic_gis_gamma_proof.tex reduces four cyclic quadratics by exact
ideal-membership certificates, then positivity selects the real branch.
The textbook imports the finite elimination and derives the branch
selection by hand; it does not claim a new independent elimination run.
The gamma convention was checked directly against
hi_even_cyclic_analytic_proof.tex and hi_shintani_meromorphic.py:
F_k=1/gamma(omega2+k)=Sin2(1+kD/(D+1);D,1).
Sin2 is reciprocal gamma, so the chapter explicitly warns about the
opposite sine-shift direction.

3. The Z2 x Z6 algebraic existence/unitarity certificate is complete in
current supplied sources; the proposed four gamma products remain open.
Read hi_noncyclic_gamma_identification_status.md and
hi_noncyclic_ansatz_z2xz6_exact.tex plus the two manifests. The chapter
labels full 1,728 quadratic / 248,832 cubic residual reductions as recorded
construction input and does not rerun them. It derives the finite-algebra
method and conjugation-to-Hermitian implication. Six positive coefficient
embeddings are not called six inequivalent categories. No novelty claim.

4. Faithful reconstruction uses the algebraic free-product extension in
hi_noncyclic_reconstruction_extension.tex. Chapter explains the finite
function-algebra translation action, orthogonal-corner endomorphism,
and why the intertwiner grading argument is needed; it does not assert
that faithfulness establishes a positive adjoint. All finite abelian
reconstruction assertions retain full eta=1 transpose-equation premise.

5. Extended Haagerup existence, seed data, and moment reconstruction are
published BMPS/GMPPS results, verified against primary sources
https://arxiv.org/html/0909.4099v2 (Section 6 and Appendix A) and
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1810.06076 (Lemma 5.4, Remark 5.6, Proposition 5.7).
Textbook includes finite connected bipartite graph and faithful positive
trace hypotheses. The moment-to-S^2=f calculation and projection split
are derived on page. Jellyfish reduction remains published input.

6. Word tensor orbit packaging and independent exact finite verification
are local derived work, sourced from
extended_haagerup_reconstruction_and_coefficients.tex and
extended_haagerup_coefficients_results.json. The 21 seeds themselves are
published. All 6,561 words are determined but the seed table is not fully
reprinted. The 96-block audit is recorded, not rerun. The complete planar
presentation is not called an enumerated skeletal F table. Low-depth 77
F blocks and 90 scalar pentagons retain their limited diagnostic scope;
general pentagons follow from reconstruction and basis overlaps.

7. Field distinction verified against primary Morrison--Snyder,
https://arxiv.org/html/1002.0168v2, Theorem 1.2. The chapter defines a
split rational form and distinguishes generator coordinates from
orthonormal-gauge square-root extensions. Its lambdaEH=i*sqrt(x-2)
and the paper's parameter mu=-1/lambdaEH generate the same field:
(x^2-6x+5)(x-2)=-5 follows from the index cubic.

8. The Morita non-identification is a hand deduction from positive
FP dimensions, not from possibly negative spherical dimensions. The
EH dimension cubic is irreducible mod 2, whereas HI dimensions have
degree 2. No categorical construction here is claimed to give a new VOA,
sewn local CFT, or identification of the physical H3 chain limit.
