HAND CHECK OF THE DRAFT

With beta=e7+e8 and gamma=e6-e7,
nu=(2beta+gamma)/3=(e6+e7+2e8)/3 satisfies
(nu,beta)=1, (nu,gamma)=0, nu^2=2/3. A root r has A2 projection nu
exactly when r6=r7 and r7+r8=1.

The integer roots satisfying this are e8+/-ei for i=1,...,5
(ten roots), and e6+e7 (one root). The half-integer roots have
last three coordinates all +1/2; the first five signs have even
minus parity, giving sixteen roots. Thus there are 27 projections
onto the E6 complement, each of squared norm 2-2/3=4/3.

For r=e6+e7, the E6 projection w0=(2/3)(e6+e7-e8) pairs to one
with the sixth E6 simple root in the preceding example and to zero
with the other five. It is the endpoint fundamental weight.

Let eta=(-e1-e2-e3-e4-e5+e6+e7-e8)/2. Then (w0,eta)=1 and
s_eta(w0) has first five coordinates +1/2 and last three
(1/6,1/6,-1/6). The D5 reflections in the first five coordinates
give all sixteen even-minus choices. Reflecting a choice with four
first-coordinate minus signs in eta gives one of the ten weights
of the form +/-ei-(e6+e7-e8)/3, and the D5 reflections give all ten.
This connects all 27 weights in one E6 Weyl orbit. Distinct lattice
momenta give one-dimensional weight lines; the root zero modes
connect them, so this is the minuscule irreducible 27.

The A2 orbit {nu,nu-beta,nu-beta-gamma} has three elements.
Its Weyl action commutes with E6, gives three copies of the 27
weight set, and preserves the glue class. Negating the roots
produces the dual 81-dimensional class. The earlier 162-root
count ensures these two sets exhaust the roots outside E6+A2.
Therefore, with this choice of fundamental versus dual labels,
  248 = (78,1)+(1,8)+(27,3)+(27bar,3bar).
Reversing the A2 fundamental-label convention swaps 3 and 3bar;
this is just relabeling, not a different dimension statement.

The conformal weights of the two factors are (4/3)/2=2/3 and
(2/3)/2=1/3, so these tensors already sit at total weight one.
There is no oscillator contribution to a nontrivial glue top at
that weight. The six-plus-two Cartan oscillators belong to the
adjoint summands, making their dimensions 72+6=78 and 6+2=8.

SCOPE

The draft calls this finite branching under e6+sl3 and explicitly
distinguishes it from restriction to U. It does not infer simple
U-modules, Haagerup junctions, or a selected extension algebra from
an equality of characters. The c05 finite irreducibility and
extension-algebra inputs remain separate.

No change is required for correctness. The draft is useful if one
additional short worked example fits the final pedagogical scope.
