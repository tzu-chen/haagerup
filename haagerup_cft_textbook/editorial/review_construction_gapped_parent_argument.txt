Detailed checks
---------------
MODULE TYPING. mu_M:M tensor Q -> M is a right action. The left
category action is c tensor M with induced action
(id_c tensor mu_M) a_(c,M,Q). It does not require a braiding or a
tensor product of two Q-modules. The module coefficient with input
simple height a, physical algebra label rho and output height b is
therefore of the stated type. The support list and free-module
decomposition rho Q=Q+alpha^2 Q+M_fix give exactly the multiplicities
(1,0,1,3,2,2) in (1,alpha,alpha^2,rho,alpha rho,alpha^2 rho).
Frobenius reciprocity gives End_Q(rho Q) dimension3. After two distinct
simple summands, its nonzero orthogonal complement must be simple.
The exact module input is substantive: neither object supports nor
fusion-ring matrices alone would prove these action equations.

SPECIALNESS. The unit-output block is 1+2d. For the rho output,
2+v^2+2w^2+zeta^2=2+v^2+2(d-1-v^2)+(1+v^2)=1+2d.
This uses precisely the two identities printed in the example. It
does not substitute for the imported Frobenius or associativity checks.

ORDINARY TRACE. The local induced-action fiber is
direct_sum_a Hom(b,c tensor a) tensor M_a. Heightwise module
isomorphisms cancel between consecutive edges, leaving ordinary
cyclic traces on finite multiplicity fibers. Hence each copy of a
simple module contributes one ordinary trace, with no qdim factor.
All four modules here have one-dimensional supported fibers, so the
physical amplitudes reduce literally to products of scalar W weights.
The finite-ring length3/4 checks are normalization controls, while the
telescoping proof is the all-length argument. The distinction between
canonical finite-ring tensor normalization and orthonormal limiting
vacua at lines242--248 is correct and important.

BLOCK INJECTIVITY. For an ordered block the word
a,R_j,I_j,R_j,b has nonzero amplitude for every supported endpoint
pair. Its unique invertible height excludes the other modules.
Invertibility of W_j turns the three endpoint rows into a basis,
giving nine independent virtual matrices. For the fixed block an
all-noninvertible five-site word can visit all three colors with any
prescribed endpoints; this excludes every ordered block. Its nonzero
edges and invertible W_f give the other nine matrices. Thus the
direct-sum span is all four Mat_3 blocks, stronger than four separate
primitive norm graphs. W_Q has determinant -u*zeta. W_f has eigenvalues
B_f(t_0+2), B_f(t_0-1), B_f(t_0-1), and neither excluded t_0 value solves
t_0^2+t_0=1/d.

LOCAL RANGE AND CONSTRAINTS. For arbitrary virtual boundary matrix X,
the endpoint coefficient is sum_c W_M(b,c) X(c,a). Invertibility makes
these coefficients arbitrary, proving that the printed endpoint-vector
span is the full ordinary MPS range. Distinct physical endpoint pairs
are orthogonal. Therefore Pi_ell fixes the endpoints and acts within
admissible interiors; adjoining the outside path cannot create a bad
boundary edge. This is a reducing restriction of the full six-state
operator. The line proof uses the full interval range with boundary
coefficients, not only four periodic vectors. Closure under all simple
lines and their duals supplies the reducing global range, as explained
in clarification3 above.

PARENT-THEOREM SCOPE. The source invokes the finite-family/block-injective
MPS theorem for sufficiently long fixed interaction range, with the
actual local range/intersection construction. The finite block span
separates the states, and their primitive transfers exclude a hidden
periodic-block obstruction. One-site translation follows by placing
the same term at every site; the five-site word witness is not a
physical unit-cell change. No minimal range or gap constant is claimed.
The four periodic MPS are admissible, so restriction leaves all four
ground states and inherits the full parent gap. The construction is
independent of the target negative-rho projector Hamiltonian.

SHORT-ANCHOR COUNTS. At three heights: equal invertible endpoints give
3 dimensions; invertible/noninvertible and reversed endpoints give6
each; equal noninvertible endpoints give3*(4-1)=9; unequal ones give

in every sector, not only a total-dimension count. The sum over physical
tensors is the invertible block diagonal W, so a full word span at one
length remains full at all larger lengths. Thus dim S4=36 as claimed.
Open admissible-word counts satisfy D1=6,D2=15 and
D_(ell+2)=3D_(ell+1)+D_ell, giving51 and168. Each missing-channel
projector at four heights has rank51 after recoupling; the color
permutation disappears in the all-endpoint sum. Hence the joint kernel
has dimension at least168-51-51=66>36, without commutativity. This is
an exact obstruction to identifying the short anchor with this local
longer parent. It is not a proof that the short anchor is gapless.

VACUUM FORM FACTORS. With b=d-2, b^2+b=3, the neutral rho matrix is
[[1,sqrt3],[sqrt3,2]]. Its d eigenvector has amplitudes b:1:1:1 in
the four orthonormal vacua. The idempotent action and self-adjointness
give theta_f=b^2 theta_o, consistent with that normalization. With
p=3/(b^2+3), the displayed functions have zero means (where required),
unit two-point norms and cubics p^(-1/2),b/sqrt3,-1/sqrt3. The radical
identity p^(-1)=(13-sqrt13)/6 and all pointwise vacuum-algebra closure
identities pass. Rank-two and rank-six neutral cubics d-d^(-1)=3 also
pass. The rank-six charged magnitude is expressly conditional on a

properly distinguishes these gapped form factors from CFT OPE data.

(Y_rho-2)^2=5-Y_rho. Since the two rho eigenvalues are d and -1/d,
