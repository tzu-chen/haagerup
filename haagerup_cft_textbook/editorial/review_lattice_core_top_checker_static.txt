CUTOFF

The loop over m=-6,...,6 supplies one representative of every charge
class modulo thirteen and verifies the uniform lattice-Potts bound
14/13. For any omitted |m|>=13, its quadratic energy alone satisfies
  m^2/156 >= 169/156 = 14/13 + 1/156.
Both the parity and Potts terms are nonnegative. The PF contribution
is identical for representatives of the same m mod13, so it cancels
when the two candidates in that charge class are compared.
Thus every omitted candidate is strictly worse than an included one
in its own class, for every a and either family. Minimizing the total
energy directly over m is equivalent to the chapter's nested
minimization over charge class and representative.
The cutoff is therefore justified; it is not an empirical search
window inferred from matching the expected table.

TWO MINOR PRECISION POINTS

1. Script:12 says "at most25 candidate rows ... at once." During
   construction of the next candidates list, the previous minimizers
   list still retains some old tuples until :63 reassigns it.
   Its largest retained set is eleven rows (the y2 top), so a safe
   exact description is "25 current candidate rows plus at most
   eleven retained minimizer rows." These share their Fraction
   objects with their tuple references rather than copying whole
   arrays. This has no material effect on either proposed budget.
   Adding spaces in the docstring's "most25", "below1MiB",
   "below32MiB", and "The128MiB" would also improve readability.

2. max_rss_mib and elapsed_seconds are sampled before final JSON
   serialization and file writing. Their recorded values are therefore
   a high-water mark and elapsed time through report preparation,
   not a measurement covering the last serialization/print. The extra
   output is tiny and poses no resource issue. If metadata is meant
   to claim whole-program peak/time, name it "before_report_write" or
   take a second measurement for the final printed summary.
