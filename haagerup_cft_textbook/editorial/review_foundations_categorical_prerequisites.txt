Priority 1: make rigidity and pivotal trace operational rather than only pictorial.
  f01_fusion.tex:78-95 explains cups/caps and Frobenius reciprocity verbally;
  :105-110 invokes pivotal structure without defining it. The first serious
  later uses are the Hopf-link trace in f04:43-46, the rigidity distinction in
  c04:300-310, and the newly integrated indicator definition in c05.
  A reader can currently understand the pictures but cannot write the domains
  of an evaluation map, identify the role of X -> X**, or calculate the
  categorical trace of a nonsimple object. This is a real prerequisite gap.
  Repair: add the typed evaluation/coevaluation maps and two snake identities
  after f01:82, then a short pivotal/trace paragraph before the dimension
  character. A Fibonacci multiplicity-block example computes
  Tr_cat(lambda on 1, B on tau^2)=lambda+phi Tr(B).
  This directly prepares the dimension-weighted twist moments, with no new
  chapter or diagram package. Draft A below is about half a page plus formulas.
  A local continuation in c04 should spell out the defining GV adjunction
  Hom(Y tensor X,1)=Hom(Y,DX): the evaluation is the image of id_DX and is
  therefore nonzero. This closes the otherwise implicit nonzero-evaluation
  step in c04's proof while making clear that a coevaluation is still absent.

Priority 2: identify the center-reconstruction theorem and explain module
dimensions before c05 uses both.
  f04_modular.tex:376-392 states the global dimension formulas and the
  canonical special case Z(H3)_A=H3. It does not state the converse/general
  Lagrangian reconstruction theorem. c05_branching.tex:269-280 invokes
  "the center theorem" to infer Rep(U)=Z(Rep(U)_H), which is not just the
  earlier Morita-invariance theorem. The connection through a central free
  module functor has never been introduced.
  Also, no earlier equation says that the same A-module has different
  dimensions as an underlying ambient object and as an object of C_A.
  Without this, the appearance of the unit A with ambient dimension 3s,
  yet module dimension 1, is an avoidable source of confusion.
  Repair: one compact paragraph defining central functor as a braided lift
  to the center, the free functor X -> X tensor A, the precise Lagrangian
  reconstruction statement, and FPdim_CA(M)=FPdim_C(M)/FPdim_C(A).
  Reuse the pointed center: a three-summand flux module has ambient
  dimension 3 and module dimension 1. Do not duplicate the full nine-object
  condensation example already in r07; point forward to that derivation.
  Draft B can follow f04's algebra-module-dimension equations. It provides a
  label for replacing c05's unqualified "center theorem" with a cross-reference.
  Primary theorem checked directly: Davydov--Mueger--Nikshych--Ostrik,
  arXiv:1009.2117v2, Definition 2.4 (central functor), Section 3.3,
  Lemma 3.11, Corollaries 3.28 and 4.1. Corollary 4.1 assumes a nondegenerate
  braided fusion category and connected etale A with FPdim(A)^2=FPdim(C);
  it does not require a categorical C* structure. Thus it is available at
  c05's pseudo-unitary stage without anticipating the later analytic chapter.

Priority 3 (smaller, but worth repairing): define the module-category action
and add the indecomposability premise to the dual-category sentence.
  f03_centers.tex:341-346 introduces a module category only by analogy and
  calls its endofunctor category a fusion category without stating finite
  semisimplicity and indecomposability. These are needed: for a decomposable
  module category the dual can be a multifusion category with nonsimple unit.
  A reader is also never told what makes an endofunctor a module endofunctor.
  Repair: replace those six lines with an action bifunctor, its module
  associativity isomorphism, and the commuting-action condition on a module
  functor. Fix a right-module convention with composition as tensor product.
  The one-simple category Vec with its untwisted Vec_Z3 action gives a tiny
  concrete example: the endofunctor W tensor - carries an operator U with
  U^3=1, so its three simple possibilities are the three Z3 characters.
  This distinguishes the one simple object of the module category from the
  three simples of its dual. Draft C is a concise replacement, not a new section.
