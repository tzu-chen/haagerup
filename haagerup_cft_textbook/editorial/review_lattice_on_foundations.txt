Overall conclusion

The principal displayed mathematics passes this review. In particular, the
Fibonacci pentagon matrices implement the stated channel order, the physical
H3 recoupling block is the correct one in the source convention, the ordinary
fusion algebra and tube algebra are clearly separated, the center dimensions
and cosine-block calculations are correct, and the arithmetic chapter keeps

I found no correction to the main Fibonacci, H3 F, projector, modular-block,
Gauss-sum, Verlinde, or tube-dimension formulas. One scope/precision issue in
the field-embedding paragraph should be repaired; the other findings are
notation and reader-accessibility issues. The structure is suitable for a
beginning PhD reader, with derivations generally preceding their uses.

Findings and proposed fixes

1. f05_arithmetic.tex:271-276 [medium: exact mathematical scope]
   The paragraph says polynomial equations over a number field and an
   embedding sending delta_n to d_n suffice to apply sigma to every
   coefficient. An embedding of the dimension field is not itself defined
   on all gamma-sample coefficients. Moreover, equations over an arbitrary
   number field may have constants moved by sigma; the correct reason for
   preserving this particular equation system is that it is over Q after
   adjoining the dimension variable and clearing denominators.

   The repository proof deliberately removes the need to assume algebraicity
   of the gamma samples: proof_revised/reconstruction.tex, Lemma 'Changing
   the dimension embedding', extends the dimension-field automorphism first
   to Qbar and then, after fixing a transcendence basis, to an automorphism
   of C. It applies that automorphism to all solution coefficients together.

   Proposed replacement: 'The dimension-field automorphism interchanging
   delta_n and d_n can be extended to an automorphism sigma of C. Apply this
   same automorphism to every coefficient of the solution. The reconstruction
   equations are polynomial over Q after nonzero denominators are cleared,
   so the transformed entries satisfy the same equations with dimension d_n.
   No prior proof that every gamma sample belongs to a specified number field
   is needed.' Then keep the excellent complex-conjugation/positivity caveat.

2. f03_centers.tex:148 [minor but definite typesetting correction]
   Printed expression is mu mu^dagger = beta,1_A, with an unwanted comma.
   Replace by \mu\mu^\dagger=\beta\,1_A (or \beta 1_A).

3. f02_haagerup.tex:301 [minor: terminological ambiguity]
   'The twelve central charges encountered later belong to the tube algebra'
   can be read as twelve Virasoro central charges. Replace 'central charges'
   with 'center sectors' or 'simple center charges'. This matters in a book
   whose principal target already has two uses of central charge.

4. f04_modular.tex:360 [minor: zero object versus vacuum label]
   A=I(1) is displayed as 0 direct-sum b direct-sum 2a. Earlier text says
   label 0 is vacuum, so the formula is interpretable, but additive-category
   readers will naturally parse 0 as the zero object. Use \one\oplus
   b\oplus a^{\oplus2}, or M_0\oplus M_b\oplus M_a^{\oplus2}; explain
   the coefficient vector is still indexed by (0,b,a,...).

5. f05_arithmetic.tex:253-267, source note at chapter end [medium editorial
   scope; not a mathematical disproof]
   The all-odd existence theorem is labeled 'quoted', while the source note
   blends Evans--Gannon and the 'more recent uniform construction'. The exact
   universal theorem here is the repository manuscript's research result,
   with Evans--Gannon supplying reconstruction; the local audit is not formal
   verification or a claim of independent literature acceptance. The text
   should say explicitly 'quoted from the repository's uniform-construction
   manuscript' or use the same construction-input convention as the c=8
   research theorem. This is consistent with the author's precise unitarity
   limits already present. The source theorem treats odd n>=3 and records
   n=1 separately, so writing that separation here would also be clearer.

6. f05_arithmetic.tex:127-136 [optional but high-value worked bridge]
   Add the order-three positive-embedding check
     A_00=(d-2)/(d-1)=(sqrt(13)-1)/(sqrt(13)+1)
          =(7-sqrt(13))/6.
   This is exactly the H2 invariant core entry from f02, and visibly differs
   from H3's (2-sqrt(13))/3. It turns the warning 'the ansatz selects a family'
   into a concrete two-line example directly relevant to the physical chain.

7. f01_fusion.tex:331-348 [optional explanatory completeness]
   The Fibonacci matrices are correct, but the intermediate basis order is
   only described as 'analogous'. A compact table would let a novice derive
   the matrices instead of trusting them. Along the three-step route use
   (g,f)=(1,tau),(tau,1),(tau,tau) in (a(bc))d and
   (g,h)=(1,tau),(tau,1),(tau,tau) in a((bc)d); the final right tree uses
   (k,h)=(1,tau),(tau,1),(tau,tau). The short-route intermediate (ab)(cd)
   uses (e,k) in the same ordered pairs. Then A mixes rows 1/3, B mixes 2/3,
   and the explicit permutation in C is directly explained.

8. f03_centers.tex:98-105 [optional wording]
   'Tensor square of the fundamental shaded strand' should remind the reader
   that the basic bimodule is N-M and must compose with its oppositely shaded
   dual to land in an even part. Say 'the fundamental strand followed by its
   oppositely shaded dual' or add a parenthesis. The dimension I-1=d and the
   identification in the relevant H2 even part are correct.
