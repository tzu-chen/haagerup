3. EXACT ISING BENCHMARK: PARITY, ENERGIES, AND SCALING

The Hamiltonian uses Pauli matrices, lattice spacing one, and coupling
J. The Jordan-Wigner definition with sigma^+ gives n=(1-sigma^z)/2.
Its boundary condition c_(L+1)=-P c_1 is consistent with the bond
identity and with physical NS-even plus R-odd Fock-space projection.
The unprojected direct sum would double count the spin Hilbert space.
At criticality the R zero mode can supply the required odd parity
without changing the energy; it does not imply a second physical R
ground state after projection.

The dispersion is epsilon(k)=4J|sin(k/2)|. Thus the infinite-volume
velocity is 2J. With x=pi/(2L), the displayed telescoping trigonometric
sums give exactly
  E_NS=-2J csc x,       E_R=-2J cot x.
The expansion
  E_NS=-4JL/pi-pi J/(6L)-7 pi^3 J/(1440 L^3)+O(L^-5)
therefore gives vc=J and, after the independent dispersion calculation,
c=1/2. The finite known-velocity Casimir estimate is
  c(L)=1/2+7 pi^2/(480L^2)+O(L^-4).
All coefficients in the chapter agree.

The spin-family gap is E_R-E_NS=2J tan(x/2). Its dimension in the
known velocity is 1/8+pi^2/(384L^2)+O(L^-4). The Perron-Frobenius
argument assigning momentum zero to the ground state in each physical
parity sector is sound: adjacent pair flips span the even bit changes
within either fixed-parity configuration space, so the nonpositive
off-diagonal matrix is irreducible there. The positive ground vector is
translation invariant.

The NS pair with momenta +/-pi/L is parity allowed and has
  g_epsilon=8J sin x.
It limits to weights (1/2,1/2), not a single chiral fermion. The pair
pi/L,3pi/L is also parity allowed and has
  g_T=4J(sin x+sin 3x)=16J sin x cos^2 x.
Its total momentum is 4pi/L and its known-velocity dimension is
  2-7pi^2/(12L^2)+O(L^-4).
The Majorana identity L_-2|0>=(1/2)psi_-3/2 psi_-1/2|0> has norm
squared 1/4=c/2. The sign and factor follow from
T=-(1/2):psi partial psi: and fermion anticommutation.

The projected continuum Hilbert space is correctly the diagonal Ising
sum of vacuum, energy, and spin families. A single unprojected chiral
fermion is not an additional local field in this bosonic periodic model.

5. MOMENTUM CONVENTIONS AND THE STRESS ESTIMATOR

l01's translation convention and H_m=sum_j exp(-2pi i m j/L) h_j
give U_tr H_m U_tr^-1=exp(2pi i m/L) H_m. This agrees with l02's
H_2 -> omega(L_-2+bar L_2), and l03's
H_+1 -> omega(L_-1+bar L_1), with omega=2pi v/L.
Positive H_m raises the residual momentum by m in these conventions.
Carrier momenta, residual conformal spin, and spin modulo the finite
ring's momentum lattice are distinguished correctly.

For a normalized stress state,
  |M|^2=omega^2 c/2,       g_T=2omega,
so c_hat=8|M|^2/g_T^2. The norm here is the extensive energy Fourier
source. It is not the 1/L-averaged microscopic scalar source in l03.
The book and solutions keep those definitions distinct.

The improvement h_j -> h_j+B_(j+1)-B_j contributes
  delta H_m=(e^(ik)-1) sum_j e^(-ikj) B_j.
For fixed nonzero m and a nonvanishing scaling matrix element, it
scales as L^-Delta_B. Thus a dimension-one current can contaminate a
dimension-two energy-density source at the same L^-1 order. The
solution properly retains selection-rule and fixed-m qualifications.

The nonidentity-threshold discussion is also algebraically correct:
c_eff=c-24h_0, norm(L_-1)^2=2h_0, and
norm(L_-2)^2=4h_0+c/2. A controlled full level-two source weight returns
c+8h_0 under the vacuum estimator, not c_eff. The need to sum a
nonorthogonal/mixing descendant cluster is explicitly stated.
For the stated cigar assignment k=4 gives c=5, h_0=1/8, c_eff=2,
and this stress-weight diagnostic equal to six. The claim remains
conditional and is not broadened to all noncompact theories.

7. THERMAL AND RESIDUAL EXERCISES

The spectral residual estimate follows directly from a sum of positive
weights, and the outside-cluster squared weight is bounded by
||r||^2/gamma^2 only with an independently supplied separation gamma.
The two-size Casimir solution has the correct overall minus sign and
retains the difference of the per-site remainders divided by the
inverse-square-size difference.

The thermal convention is physical beta, beta_*=L/v. It matches r05's
dimensionless b=2pi beta v/L, whose self-dual value is 2pi. With
a=pi c/(6 beta_*), the first condition gives
c=6 beta_* <g>/pi. The third condition is beta_* m_3=3m_2, and the fifth
is 15 beta_*^2 m_2-25 beta_*^3 m_3+10 beta_*^4 m_4-beta_*^5 m_5=0.
The Stirling coefficients, alternating derivative signs, and use of
central moments rather than cumulants all agree. A single projected
charge sector need not be modular S-invariant; exchanging cycles
introduces a spatial twist. The solution explains this premise.
