\begin{abstract}
The existing Lean proofs verify a few finite algebraic implications;
they do not formalize the lattice construction or the Haagerup CFT
theorem. This assessment distinguishes a useful conditional lemma from
an end-to-end formalization, records reusable library infrastructure,
and assigns conversion difficulty to every section of the manuscript.
The original coverage assessment is retained, with navigation updated
to the current manuscript's sections and appendices; an additional
assessment covers the physics sections and their new exact datasets.
The frozen original remains available with the archived baseline.
Two additional Lean lemmas check
the real-number rearrangement and positive division used in the thermal
tail bounds, with their analytic premises explicit. The Python
arithmetic certificates and outward-rounded intervals remain separate
from these kernel-checked implications.
The estimates describe dependency and proof-engineering difficulty,
not a prediction of elapsed time or a claim that no relevant external
formalization exists.
\end{abstract}
