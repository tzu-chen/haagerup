# The square-root conjugation is an ambient diagram involution

This is an exact all-weight identity in the lattice model, derived
algebraically without a numerical job. It explains why conjugating the
defining sqrt(13) does not by itself select the proper branch.

Let sigma fix sqrt(-3) and negate sqrt(13). All lattice vertex products,
the six K currents, and omega_B are rational in the chosen Chevalley
gauge. Therefore applying sigma coefficientwise to every mode word gives

    sigma(U_n)=U'_n,  dim U'_n=dim U_n

for every n, where U' is generated by K,omega_B,sigma(u). This preserves
all exact word identities and graded ranks, not just the checked grades.

In fact U' is an ordinary automorphic image of U. Let pi be the E6
diagram permutation (0 4)(1 3), fixing2,5 and both A2 nodes6,7. In the
cocycle gauge used by `hi_cft_20260923_c8_exact.py`, its lift is

    g(e^alpha)=eta(alpha)e^(pi alpha),
    eta(alpha)=(-1)^(alpha0 alpha1+alpha1 alpha2
                    +alpha2 alpha3+alpha3 alpha4),

and it permutes Heisenberg oscillators by pi. This sign is the quadratic
coboundary accounting for reversal of the four E6 chain edges in the
lower-triangular lattice cocycle. The branch edge(2,5) is not reversed.
Consequently g is an all-weight lattice VOA automorphism, and g^2=1.

Every simple-root exponential occurring in the right raising current
has eta=1. Its coefficients and those of the lowering current are
invariant under pi, so g fixes the right sl2 pointwise. The left root
A0=(-1,-2,-3,-2,-1,-2,0,0) is fixed and has eta=1, so g also fixes the
left sl2 pointwise. As a diagram lift in the compact Chevalley form it
preserves the unitary structure and fixes omega_B.

For the three norm-four momenta in the actual u recipe,

    a=(0,0,0,0,0,-1,1,1),
    b=(0,0,0,1,1,-1,0,0),
    c=(1,1,0,0,0,-1,0,0),

one has g(e^a)=e^a, g(e^b)=-e^c, g(e^c)=-e^b. With

    lambda_plus =(sqrt(13)+3sqrt(-3))/20,
    lambda_minus=(sqrt(13)-3sqrt(-3))/20,

this gives

    g(u)=e^a-lambda_minus e^b-lambda_plus e^c=sigma(u).

Hence g(U)=sigma(U) to all weights. The semilinear operation g sigma
fixes the defining generators; this supplies a descent datum for U over
Q(sqrt(-3)). It does not supply a complex-linear projection onto U.
After extending scalars, a semilinear fixed form can be the full ambient
VOA. Thus this descent is compatible both with U=B and with proper U.

The two possibilities still require a genuine invariant subspace,
noninvertible projection, or an all-weight presentation. In particular,
the equality of every conjugate word-span rank is not an upper bound
showing that the known six-dimensional current span is complete.
