# Charged zero-product span: bounded positive certificate

The new producer reads the immutable exact 956-dimensional S3 checkpoint,
its exact 634-dimensional normal subspace, the prior exact raising certificate,
and all saved lower-closure records. It never writes any of these inputs.
Only root may launch it, serially through the safe launcher. No computation
has been launched by the implementing agent.

Let C be the full K-submodule of P=S3/N3 generated by zero products of two
charged weight-two fields. Zero-mode covariance shows that C is contained
in the linear span of charged-pair zero products. The producer forms seeds
B(a_highest, k_0 s) from the already exact lower relations. A current kills
the singlet part of S2; every remaining part is charged or normal, and the
normal right contribution is zero modulo N3. Thus every seed is in C.

The producer computes the two current lowering images of all 322 quotient
lifts at the fixed split prime. These are specializations of actual vectors
of P because exact S3 is already current-stable and N3 is current-stable.
If the lowering images and charged seeds have rank 322, their actual vectors
span the known exact 322-dimensional P. Therefore P/C=(F_L,F_R)(P/C).
The commuting lowering operators generate a nilpotent ideal on the finite
module P/C; iteration proves P/C=0. This is a positive spanning argument,
not an inferred characteristic-zero upper rank from a prime calculation.
An incomplete rank is explicitly inconclusive.

The retained grade-three basis has at most 300,000 sparse terms. The same
loading and mode engine previously used about 0.227 GiB with exact field
coefficients; this producer uses prime integers. At most 960 bracket
coordinate vectors and 720 current coordinate vectors are additionally
retained, each in a small Cartan block. The 322-dimensional echelon table,
644 lowering records and at most 322 additional independent seed records
(normally 28, if the lowering-image rank is unchanged at the prime)
are small compared with the input basis. Primitive caches are bounded
128/256/512 and cleared every 40 lowering records. A conservative expected
peak is below 0.5 GiB; the producer enforces a 1 GiB RSS review boundary
and requires an address limit at most 3 GiB. The standard 32 GiB host
reserve and two-CPU/thread limits remain mandatory.

Suggested first run is at most 300 seconds, with a 320-second external
timeout. There are no subprocesses, native dense solves, workers or
detached jobs. SQLite is opened immutable/read-only, capped at 256 MiB;
decompressed relation records are capped at 8 MiB and the output at 32 MiB.
Only source reading, editing and syntax compilation preceded review.
